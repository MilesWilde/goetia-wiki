# Shiva

[[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]’s introductory essay uses the story of the immeasurable Mahalingam as a comparison. A footnote connects that image with Shiva the Destroyer, while the story names [[Brahma (Goetia)|Brahma]] and [[Vishnu (Goetia)|Vishnu]] as unable to reach its ends.

The comparison belongs to the introductory essay, not the seventy-two-spirit catalogue.
