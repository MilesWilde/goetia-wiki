# Osiris

A marginal note identifies [[Osorronophris (Goetia)|Osorronophris]] with [[Osorronophris (Goetia)|Asar-un-nefer]], the perfected Osiris. The same commentary contrasts an Osiris formula with a [[Horus (Goetia)|Horus]] interpretation.

These associations belong to the marginal discussion of the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]], rather than the numbered Goetic catalogue.
