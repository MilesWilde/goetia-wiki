# Bacchus Diphues

Bacchus Diphues appears in a numerical interpretation of the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]’s conclusion. [[Zeus (Goetia)|Zeus Arrhenothelys]] and [[Heru-pa-kraath (Goetia)|Heru-pa-kraath]] occur in the same marginal grouping. The note gives a correspondence rather than a Goetic profile.
