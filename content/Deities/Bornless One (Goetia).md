# Bornless One

The [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]] addresses the Bornless One as creator of opposites, life, earth, and heaven. By the conclusion, the speaker identifies with this power as the Bornless Spirit and uses the self-designation Heart Girt with a Serpent.

The electronic notes identify the invocation as an addition adapted from the Stele of [[Jeu the Hieroglyphist (Goetia)|Jeu the Hieroglyphist]]. Its names, speaker identities, and marginal interpretations are distinct textual layers.
