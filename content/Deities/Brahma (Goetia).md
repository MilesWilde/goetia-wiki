# Brahma

Brahma is named in [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]’s introductory comparison concerning the immeasurable Mahalingam. The story describes Brahma and [[Vishnu (Goetia)|Vishnu]] traveling in opposite directions without reaching its ends.

This is a mythic comparison in the essay, not a Goetic office or rank.
