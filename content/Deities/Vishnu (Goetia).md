# Vishnu

Vishnu is named with [[Brahma (Goetia)|Brahma]] in [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]’s introductory comparison concerning the immeasurable Mahalingam. Neither reaches an end in the story as recounted there. The passage is a comparison in the introductory essay.
