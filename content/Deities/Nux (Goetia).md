# Nux

Nux is a Greek-name reference in a marginal correspondence attached to the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]. It appears with other symbolic names rather than in an independent biography. This note preserves the edition’s form instead of replacing it with an externally supplied spelling.
