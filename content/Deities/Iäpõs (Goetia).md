# Iäpõs

Iäpõs is one of the names of the power addressed by the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]. Its marginal form is Ia-Apophrasz. The name belongs to that invocation and annotation, rather than to a separate numbered spirit profile.
