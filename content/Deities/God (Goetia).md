# God

The conjurations appeal to a supreme creator and portray this authority as superior to the spirits and their kings. The text uses God, Lord, Most High, and many divine names within those appeals.

The names are grouped by their passages in the divine-name reference. They do not each establish a separate being, and this note does not merge unrelated deity figures from the commentary.
