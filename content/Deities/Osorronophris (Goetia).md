# Osorronophris

Osorronophris is a name of the power addressed by the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]. A marginal note identifies it with Asar-un-nefer, the perfected [[Osiris (Goetia)|Osiris]].

That identification is explicitly an annotation. It is recorded here alongside the invocation’s wording rather than used to erase the distinction between the invocation and its commentary.
