# Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is named in the Rosicrucian formula in the preface. The electronic editor explains the formula with its related references to [[God (Goetia)|God]] and [[Jesus (Goetia)|Jesus]]. Its role belongs to the framing invocation.
