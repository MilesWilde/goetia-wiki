# Typhon Seth

Typhon Seth is addressed in the Greek prefatory curse. The electronic notes translate and discuss that text and its relationship to the wording of the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]].

The name sequence includes Ereschigal without an independent profile. This note does not identify Typhon Seth with other similarly named figures beyond the source’s explicit statements.
