# Ra Hoor Khuit

Ra Hoor Khuit appears in the marginal discussion of the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]] as perfected [[Horus (Goetia)|Horus]]. The name belongs to the commentator’s [[Horus (Goetia)|Horus]] interpretation, rather than to the seventy-two numbered spirits.
