# Angel of Paphrõ Osorronophris

The [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]’s speaker claims the identity Angel of Paphrõ Osorronophris. The marginalia offer Apophrasz, Apephi-Asar-un-Nefer, and Ptah-Apophrasz-Ra as substitutions or glosses.

These forms are kept together as annotated ritual identities; the passage does not provide a separate angelic biography or catalogue number.
