# Heru-pa-kraath

Heru-pa-kraath is named in the marginal numerical interpretation of the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]’s conclusion, alongside [[Bacchus Diphues (Goetia)|Bacchus Diphues]] and [[Zeus (Goetia)|Zeus Arrhenothelys]]. The supplied passage does not add an independent appearance or office description.
