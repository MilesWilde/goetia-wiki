# Jesus

Jesus is named in the Rosicrucian formula in the preface. The electronic editor translates and explains that formula alongside its references to [[God (Goetia)|God]] and the [[Holy Spirit (Goetia)|Holy Spirit]].

This is a Christian invocation in the book’s framing material, not a numbered spirit description.
