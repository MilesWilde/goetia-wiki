# Zeus

Zeus appears in the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]’s marginal correspondences. The form Zeus Arrhenothelys also occurs in the numerical interpretation of the invocation’s conclusion.

The annotations provide these associations without assigning a Goetic office or rank.
