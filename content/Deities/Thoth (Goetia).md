# Thoth

The [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]] includes the word Thoteth. A marginal note identifies Thoteth as a form of Thoth.

This identification belongs to the annotation; it does not make Thoth one of the numbered Goetic spirits.
