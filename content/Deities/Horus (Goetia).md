# Horus

The [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]’s marginal commentary contrasts a Horus interpretation with the [[Osiris (Goetia)|Osiris]] formula. It names [[Ra Hoor Khuit (Goetia)|Ra Hoor Khuit]] in its discussion of perfected Horus.

This is a commentary relationship; the passage does not supply a Goetic rank or legion count for Horus.
