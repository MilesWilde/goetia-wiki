# Shu

Shu is named in the elemental and symbolic marginalia to the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]. One note pictures Shu supporting the sky; another places the name among correspondences of the firmament.

These are marginal correspondences, not a separate catalogue entry.
