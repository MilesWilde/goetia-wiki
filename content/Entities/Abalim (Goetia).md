# Abalim

Abalim is one of the two attendant kings named in [[Paimon]]'s catalogue entry. The other is [[Labal (Goetia)|Labal]]. They are described as attending [[Paimon]] when he is called alone.

Their twenty-five legions are mentioned jointly. The text does not assign a personal count to Abalim or clearly explain that smaller group's relationship to [[Paimon]]'s total. Abalim has no separate numbered entry or appearance description here.
