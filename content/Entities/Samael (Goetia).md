# Samael

Samael is named in an alternative set of four kings that the translator's footnote attributes to the Rabbins. The set consists of Samael, [[Azazel (Goetia)|Azazel]], [[Azäel (Goetia)|Azäel]], and [[Mahazael (Goetia)|Mahazael]].

The passage gives no individual description, direction, or legion count for Samael. This note records the role in the supplied edition without importing descriptions from other traditions.
