# Halahel

A note accompanying figure 175 attributes a seal to Halahel, a spirit said to be under [[Bael]]. It describes Halahel as mixed good and evil, comparing that character with the spirits of Theurgia-Goetia.

The note explicitly declines to guarantee the attribution's accuracy. Halahel is therefore retained as an uncertain supplementary reference, rather than added to the 72 numbered spirits.
