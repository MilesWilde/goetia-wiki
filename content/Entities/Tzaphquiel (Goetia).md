# Tzaphquiel

Tzaphquiel appears in the circle note's Binah sequence with [[Iehovah (Goetia)|Iehovah]] [[Elohim (Goetia)|Elohim]], the choir Aralim, and Shabbathai, the sphere of Saturn.

The electronic editor's transliteration of a form of the vessel's Hebrew inscription also gives Tzaphqiel. The spelling variation is preserved without adding attributes not stated in these passages.
