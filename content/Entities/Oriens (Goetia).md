# Oriens

Oriens or Uriens appears in a footnote's alternative list of four kings, together with [[Paymon (Goetia)|Paymon]] or [[Paymon (Goetia)|Paymonia]], [[Ariton (Goetia)|Ariton]] or [[Ariton (Goetia)|Egyn]], and [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]] or [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaimon]].

This passage supplies neither a separate description nor an explicit correspondence between each name and the main list's kings or directions. The paired forms are retained as the footnote presents them.
