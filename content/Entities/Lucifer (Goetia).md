# Lucifer

The catalogue describes [[Paimon]] as especially obedient to Lucifer. [[Belial]]'s entry says that [[Belial]] was created next after Lucifer. These references locate Lucifer within the book's relationships, without giving him a numbered catalogue profile.

The electronic editor also suggests Lucifer, [[Leviathan (Goetia)|Leviathan]], [[Satan (Goetia)|Satan]], and [[Belial]] as the preface's Four Great Princes, referring to Abramelin. That identification is an editorial interpretation, distinct from the text's lists of quarter kings.
