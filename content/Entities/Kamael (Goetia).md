# Kamael

Kamael is named in the circle note's Geburah sequence alongside [[Elohim (Goetia)|Elohim]] Gibor, the Seraphim, and Madim, the sphere of Mars. The later [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] writes Camael.

Kamael also appears in the electronic editor's transliteration of one form of the vessel inscription. The prayer's adjacent requests are not treated as literal translations of the name.
