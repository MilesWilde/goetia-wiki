# Gabriel

Gabriel appears in the circle note's Iesod sequence with [[Shaddai (Goetia)|Shaddaï el Chai]], the Cherubim, and Levanah, the sphere of the Moon.

The [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] calls Gabriel a messenger of good tidings. The electronic editor's vessel-inscription transliteration also includes the name.
