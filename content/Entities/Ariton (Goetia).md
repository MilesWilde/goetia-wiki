# Ariton

Ariton or Egyn appears in an alternative list of four kings in the translator's footnote. The list also names [[Oriens (Goetia)|Oriens]] or [[Oriens (Goetia)|Uriens]], [[Paymon (Goetia)|Paymon]] or [[Paymon (Goetia)|Paymonia]], and [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]] or [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaimon]].

No individual appearance, legion count, office profile, or unambiguous directional assignment is supplied for Ariton in this passage.
