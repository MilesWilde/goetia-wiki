# Sakhr

Sakhr or Sacar is the jinni named in the translator's comparison between the brass-vessel narrative and the fisherman story of the Arabian Nights.

This reference belongs to a literary comparison in a note on the vessel. It does not add a seventy-third spirit, a Goetic rank, or a legion count to the numbered catalogue.
