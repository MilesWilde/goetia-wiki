# Zimimay

Zimimay or Ziminiar appears in the main list of four great kings following the catalogue. The text supplies no individual direction or appearance in that list.

The Angelic-language [[Goetia Invocation of the King|king's invocation]] gives Zodimay among the names that may be substituted for [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaimon]]. This reference retains that spelling variation without constructing a new numbered spirit or a complete directional hierarchy.
