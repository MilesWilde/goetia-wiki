# Leviathan

Leviathan is named in the electronic editor's proposed identification of the preface's Four Great Princes. The suggested set is [[Lucifer (Goetia)|Lucifer]], Leviathan, [[Satan (Goetia)|Satan]], and [[Belial]], with a reference to Abramelin.

The original prefatory passage does not name the princes, and this PDF gives Leviathan no numbered entry or appearance description. The editorial identification is distinct from the quarter-king lists.
