# Labal

Labal is one of the two attendant kings named in [[Paimon]]'s catalogue entry. The other is [[Abalim (Goetia)|Abalim]]. They are described as attending [[Paimon]] when he is called alone.

The passage mentions twenty-five legions with the attendants collectively; it does not allocate an individual count to Labal or clearly reconcile that group with [[Paimon]]'s larger total. Labal has no separate numbered entry or appearance description here.
