# Göap

Göap is named among the four great kings following the catalogue. The passage identifies the group with the cardinal quarters without assigning each name a separate direction.

The Angelic-language [[Goetia Invocation of the King|king's invocation]] elsewhere names [[Gäap|Gaap]] as a possible substitution. Compare the numbered spirit [[Gäap]], whose own entry gives different titles. The overlapping spellings are recorded without assuming that the edition has established a single complete hierarchy or an unambiguous identity between every occurrence.
