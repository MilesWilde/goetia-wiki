# Azäel

Azäel is named in an alternative set of four kings that the translator's footnote attributes to the Rabbins. The set consists of [[Samael (Goetia)|Samael]], [[Azazel (Goetia)|Azazel]], Azäel, and [[Mahazael (Goetia)|Mahazael]].

The passage gives no individual description, direction, or legion count for Azäel. The source lists [[Azazel (Goetia)|Azazel]] separately and does not explain the relationship between the two names. This note records the role in the supplied edition without importing descriptions from other traditions.
