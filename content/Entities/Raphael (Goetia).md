# Raphael

The circle note prints Raphaë in its Tiphereth sequence with [[Iehovah (Goetia)|Iehovah]] Eloah va-Daath, the choir Malakim, and Shemesh, the sphere of the Sun. The later [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] writes Raphael in full and asks the angel to attend.

Raphael also appears in the electronic editor's transliteration of a form of the vessel inscription. This note preserves the shortened spelling from the circle note rather than silently correcting the cited text.
