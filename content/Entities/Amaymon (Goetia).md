# Amaymon

Amaymon is one of the four great kings named in the observations following the spirit catalogue. An alternative list also gives the form Amaimon. The [[Goetia Invocation of the King|king's invocation]] explicitly associates Amaimon with the East and portrays him as able to command subordinate spirits.

The quarter-king lists vary between passages. The [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language invocation]] offers substitutions, but this edition does not establish a complete set of directions and subordinate assignments for all four kings.
