# Iophiel

Iophiel is a name in the [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]], where the adjacent petition concerns command over spirits of air, water, fire, earth, and hell. The passage is a prayer, not a reliable literal translation or an independent office catalogue.

The translator flags corrupt spellings in this prayer, and the electronic editor discusses its omissions and defective transmission. This reference does not replace [[Ratziel (Goetia)|Ratziel]] with Iophiel in the separate magical-circle table.
