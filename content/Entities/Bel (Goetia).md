# Bel

Bel is named alongside [[Dragon of Daniel (Goetia)|the Dragon]] in the [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]]'s account of [[Daniel (Goetia)|Daniel]]. The passage credits [[Daniel (Goetia)|Daniel]] with their destruction and invokes the names [[Alpha and Omega (Goetia)|Alpha and Omega]] in recounting that precedent.

Bel is a figure or object of worship in that biblical narrative, not a numbered Goetic spirit. The [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]] repeats the reference.
