# Tzadquiel

The circle note places Tzadquiel in the Chesed sequence, with the divine name [[El (Goetia)|El]], the choir Chaschmalin, and Tzedeq, the sphere of Jupiter.

The electronic editor's transliteration of the vessel inscription also gives Tzadqiel. These spellings refer the reader to the corresponding passages; the surrounding choir and sphere names remain separate terms.
