# Paymon

Paymon or Paymonia belongs to a footnote's alternative list of the four kings governing the quarters. The other names in that list are [[Oriens (Goetia)|Oriens]] or [[Oriens (Goetia)|Uriens]], [[Ariton (Goetia)|Ariton]] or [[Ariton (Goetia)|Egyn]], and [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]] or [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaimon]].

Compare the numbered spirit [[Paimon]]. The footnote does not explicitly map Paymon to that numbered entry, so the names have separate notes here and their possible relationship remains qualified.
