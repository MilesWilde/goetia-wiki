# Pruflas

The electronic editor names Pruflas or Bufas as a Prince and Duke in [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]]'s Pseudomonarchia and explicitly states that this spirit is omitted from the Goetia. The editor also notes the omission in [[Scot (Goetia)|Scot]]'s version of that earlier list.

This PDF supplies no complete appearance or office profile for Pruflas. His presence in the notes documents a difference between catalogues rather than membership among this edition's 72 spirits.
