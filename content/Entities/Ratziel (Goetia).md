# Ratziel

Ratziel is the archangel named in the circle note's Chokmah sequence. The other terms in that row are [[Iah (Goetia)|Iah]], the choir Auphanim, and Masloth, identified with the fixed stars or Zodiac.

The later [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] includes [[Iophiel (Goetia)|Iophiel]], but does not justify silently substituting that name for Ratziel in the circle table. The two names therefore have distinct notes here.
