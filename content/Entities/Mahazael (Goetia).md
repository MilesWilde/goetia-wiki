# Mahazael

Mahazael is named in an alternative set of four kings that the translator's footnote attributes to the Rabbins. The set consists of [[Samael (Goetia)|Samael]], [[Azazel (Goetia)|Azazel]], [[Azäel (Goetia)|Azäel]], and Mahazael.

The passage gives no individual description, direction, or legion count for Mahazael. This note records the role in the supplied edition without importing descriptions from other traditions.
