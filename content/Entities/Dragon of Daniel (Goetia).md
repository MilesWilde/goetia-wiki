# Dragon of Daniel

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] refers to the Dragon together with [[Bel (Goetia)|Bel]] in its account of [[Daniel (Goetia)|Daniel]]'s destruction of them. It gives the creature no individual name or Goetic rank. “Dragon of Daniel” is this wiki's descriptive note title.

The [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]] uses Vovina for the phrase translated as the Dragon. That vocabulary does not supply another named spirit or an alternative proper name.
