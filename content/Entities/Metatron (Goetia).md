# Metatron

The translator's circle note places Metatron in the sequence for Kether, alongside the divine name [[Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh (Goetia)|Ehyeh]], the choir Chaioth ha-Qadesh, and the sphere Rashith ha-Gilgalim or Primum Mobile. These are distinct columns of the correspondence, not additional aliases for the angel.

The later [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] uses the spelling Methrattton alongside the holy hosts. Its orthography and supposed explanations are explicitly criticized in the edition; its wording should not be treated as a reliable literal translation.
