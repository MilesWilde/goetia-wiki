# Satan

The preliminary definition of magic uses the spelling Sathan for the tempter whose influence it blames for the corruption of natural magic into the black art. This is an introductory theological claim, not a numbered spirit description.

The electronic editor later includes Satan with [[Lucifer (Goetia)|Lucifer]], [[Leviathan (Goetia)|Leviathan]], and [[Belial]] in a suggested identification of the preface's Four Great Princes. The preface itself does not name those four; the identification remains attributed to the editor.
