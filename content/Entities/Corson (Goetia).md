# Corson

Corson is one of the four great kings listed after the numbered spirit catalogue, alongside [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]], [[Zimimay (Goetia)|Zimimay]] or [[Zimimay (Goetia)|Ziminiar]], and [[Göap (Goetia)|Göap]]. The passage associates the group with the cardinal quarters.

It does not assign Corson an individual direction, appearance, legion count, or separate office profile. The alternative lists in the footnote are preserved as different lists, without an inferred one-to-one identification.
