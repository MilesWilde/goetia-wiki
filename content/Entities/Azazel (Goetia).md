# Azazel

Azazel is named in an alternative set of four kings that the translator's footnote attributes to the Rabbins. The set consists of [[Samael (Goetia)|Samael]], Azazel, [[Azäel (Goetia)|Azäel]], and [[Mahazael (Goetia)|Mahazael]].

The passage gives no individual description, direction, or legion count for Azazel. The source lists [[Azäel (Goetia)|Azäel]] separately and does not explain the relationship between the two names. This note records the role in the supplied edition without importing descriptions from other traditions.
