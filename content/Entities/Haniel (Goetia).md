# Haniel

Haniel is named in the circle note's Netzach sequence, with [[Iehovah (Goetia)|Iehovah]] [[Zabaoth (Goetia)|Tzabaoth]], the choir Elohim, and Nogah, the sphere of Venus.

The later [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] asks Haniel to minister to the speaker. The electronic editor also identifies Haniel in the vessel's Hebrew-band inscription.
