# Michael

The introduction credits Michael with revealing sacred notes to [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]]. The triangle bears his name, and the [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] calls him general of the heavenly host. [[Belial]]'s catalogue entry mentions Michael among earlier heavenly angels.

In the circle note, the spelling Michaël appears in the Hod sequence with [[Elohim (Goetia)|Elohim]] [[Zabaoth (Goetia)|Tzabaoth]], Beni Elohim, and Kokav, the sphere of Mercury. Michael also appears in the electronic editor's vessel-inscription transliteration. These references describe the roles assigned by this edition.
