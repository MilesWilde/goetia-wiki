# Korah

Kora, also written Korah in the [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]], appears with [[Dathan (Goetia)|Dathan]] and [[Abiram (Goetia)|Abiram]] in the account of the earth opening in judgment. The ritual connects the episode with [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]] and the name [[Primeumaton (Goetia)|Primeumaton]].
