# Moses

Moses appears repeatedly in the conjurations’ accounts of divine names, the Egyptian plagues, and judgment. These episodes support the ritual’s claims of authority.

The [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]] also has its speaker claim the prophet-identity Mosheh. [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]’s marginalia propose [[Ankh-f-n-Khonsu (Goetia)|Ankh-f-n-Khonsu]] as a substitution for that identity; the substitution belongs to the annotation.
