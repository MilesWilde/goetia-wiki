# Mathers

The electronic editor identifies the preface’s D.D.C.F. as S. L. “MacGregor” Mathers, its original translator/editor. The later note questions the translation credit because the British Museum manuscripts were already in English.

The polemical preface and the electronic explanation are different editorial layers; neither is a spirit profile.
