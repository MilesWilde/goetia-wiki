# Ham

The electronic editor, discussing [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]]’s account of [[Gäap]], recounts a story about Cham or Ham, son of [[Noah (Goetia)|Noah]]: he is the first human after the Flood to invoke evil spirits.

This is a report of a comparative source in the later notes, not a numbered Goetic profile.
