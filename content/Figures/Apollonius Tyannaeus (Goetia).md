# Apollonius Tyannaeus

The preliminary definition cites Apollonius Tyannaeus as an example of natural magic. In the electronic notes, the form Apollonius of Tyanæa appears in a discussion of the attribution of Ars Notoria.

These are separate uses of the figure in introductory and comparative editorial material.
