# Abiram

Abiram is named with [[Korah (Goetia)|Korah]] and [[Dathan (Goetia)|Dathan]] in the conjuration’s account of the earth opening in judgment. The episode is repeated in the [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]] and is connected with [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]] and [[Primeumaton (Goetia)|Primeumaton]].
