# A. E. Waite

A. E. Waite is an earlier writer/editor discussed and criticized by [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]] in the preface and introductory essay. The comments concern editions, interpretation, and criticism of magical literature.

These polemics supply editorial context and are not adopted here as objective assessments of his work.
