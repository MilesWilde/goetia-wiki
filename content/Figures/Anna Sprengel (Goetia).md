# Anna Sprengel

Anna Sprengel, styled Soror S.D.A., is a claimed founding authority of the Golden Dawn in the electronic editor’s explanation of the Horos episode. The note discusses a claim to her identity; this reference preserves the attribution without adding an independent biography.
