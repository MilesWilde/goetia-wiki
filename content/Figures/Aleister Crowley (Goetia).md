# Aleister Crowley

The title page names Aleister Crowley as editor of the 1904 publication. He also wrote its introductory interpretation of ceremonial magic. The electronic edition distinguishes later marginalia attributed to him from the main text.

His interpretations are an editorial layer, not interchangeable with the numbered catalogue’s descriptions.
