# Daniel

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] associates Daniel with the destruction of [[Bel (Goetia)|Bel]] and [[Dragon of Daniel (Goetia)|the Dragon]], invoking [[Alpha and Omega (Goetia)|Alpha and Omega]] in its account. [[Bel (Goetia)|Bel]] and [[Dragon of Daniel (Goetia)|the Dragon]] belong to that narrative context, not to the seventy-two numbered spirit profiles.
