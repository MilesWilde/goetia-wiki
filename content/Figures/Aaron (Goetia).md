# Aaron

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] associates Aaron with [[Anaphaxeton (Goetia)|Anaphaxeton]] and wisdom. The sentence continues across the cited pages. His role is one of the biblical precedents attached to divine names.
