# Perdurabo

Perdurabo is the author-persona credited with the [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]] of the conjurations. The preface discusses that version, and its heading repeats the attribution.

These passages do not explicitly resolve the persona’s identity. This source reference keeps the attribution separate from the named editor.
