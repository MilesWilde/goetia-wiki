# Abed-nego

Abed-nego is one of the three children named in the [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]]’s account of deliverance from the furnace. The passage associates their deliverance with [[Emmanuel (Goetia)|Emmanuel]]. The three appear together as a biblical precedent for protection.
