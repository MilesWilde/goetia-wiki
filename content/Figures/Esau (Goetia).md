# Esau

Esau appears as [[Jacob (Goetia)|Jacob]]’s brother in the [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]]’s account of protection from his anger. The passage uses that deliverance as a precedent for the ritual’s invocation of divine authority.
