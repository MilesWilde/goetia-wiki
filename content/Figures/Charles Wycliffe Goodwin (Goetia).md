# Charles Wycliffe Goodwin

Goodwin belongs to the modern publication history of the ancient source behind the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]]. The electronic editor credits him with an **1852 publication of the Greek text and an English translation** of the magical papyrus, spelling his surname “Godwin.” He is a scholar transmitting the text, not an ancient ritual participant. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=89|Preliminary Invocation source note, PDF 89]].

## External historical context

The British Library's bibliography for **Papyrus 46 / PGM V** identifies the author as **Charles Wycliffe Goodwin**, and the publication as *Fragment of a Græco-Egyptian Work Upon Magic* (Cambridge Antiquarian Society, 1852). This note uses that spelling while retaining the PDF's full-name variant as an alias. [British Library, Papyrus 46 bibliography](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002104464).

The date 1852 concerns the scholarly edition. The ancient manuscript and the later adaptation printed by [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]] belong to different periods; see [[Goetia Historical Context]].
