# Swami Vive Ananda

The electronic editor identifies the polemical preface’s S.V.A. as Swami Vive Ananda, also called Madame Horos, Madame Helena, Edith Loleta, and Dis Debar. The account connects this figure with [[Theo Horos (Goetia)|Theo Horos]] and an episode involving [[Mathers (Goetia)|Mathers]] and the claimed identity of [[Anna Sprengel (Goetia)|Anna Sprengel]].

The preface’s supernatural allegations are part of its polemic, not facts asserted by this wiki.
