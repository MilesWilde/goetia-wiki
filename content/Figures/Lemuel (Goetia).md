# Lemuel

The heading to the [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|Angelic-language version]] names Lemuel as [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]]’s son and the recipient of the book. The passage supplies this relationship as part of the version’s framing, without a separate biography.
