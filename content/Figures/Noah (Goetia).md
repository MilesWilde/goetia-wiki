# Noah

Noah is named as [[Ham (Goetia)|Ham]]’s father in the electronic editor’s account of [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]]’s digression about [[Gäap]]. The account places [[Ham (Goetia)|Ham]]’s invocation of evil spirits after the Flood. This passage is comparative editorial material.
