# Ankh-f-n-Khonsu

[[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]’s marginalia offer Ankh-f-n-Khonsu as a substitute for the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]] speaker’s identification as [[Moses (Goetia)|Mosheh]]. This note records the proposed ritual identity; the passage does not supply a biography.
