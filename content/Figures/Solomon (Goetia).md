# Solomon

The book attributes mastery over the spirits and sacred knowledge to Solomon. Its brass-vessel story credits him with binding the seventy-two spirits; the introduction says [[Michael (Goetia)|Michael]] revealed sacred notes to him.

These are the book’s framing claims about its legendary authority. They do not make Solomon a member of its numbered spirit catalogue.
