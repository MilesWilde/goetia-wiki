# Jacob

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] invokes Jacob’s wrestling and his deliverance from his brother [[Esau (Goetia)|Esau]]’s anger. It connects the episode with the name Ioth.

The angel in this passage is unnamed; this note does not identify that angel with a named figure elsewhere in the book.
