# Robert Turner

Robert Turner is a seventeenth-century translator involved in the English transmission of ceremonial magic texts. The electronic editor compares the Goetia's conjurations with his **1655 English *Heptameron*** and also mentions his English version of the *Ars Notoria*. His role here is textual transmission, not a named participant in a recorded Goetia ceremony. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=91|Heptameron comparison, PDF 91]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=92|Ars Notoria, PDF 92]].

## External historical context

The Library of Congress holds the **1655 London volume**, which credits Turner as English translator and includes *Magical Elements of Peter de Abano*, the work also known as the *Heptameron*. This confirms a publication date for a source parallel to the conjurations; it does not make Turner the author of the whole *Lemegeton*. [Library of Congress, 1655 collection](https://www.loc.gov/item/11031418/); [Peterson's Heptameron edition and bibliography](https://www.esotericarchives.com/solomon/heptamer.htm).
