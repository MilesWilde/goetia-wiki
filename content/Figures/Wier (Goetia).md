# Wier

The electronic editor uses Wier’s Pseudomonarchia as a comparative source for the spirit catalogue. Notes discuss differences in names, offices, legion counts, and transmission.

A reading attributed to Wier is kept distinct from the main Goetia description rather than silently replacing it.

## External historical context

**Wier is Johann Weyer.** His *De praestigiis daemonum* first appeared in **1563**; its fifth edition added the *Pseudomonarchia daemonum* in **1577**. The PDF's note on page 91 conflates those dates. [Peterson's edition history](https://www.esotericarchives.com/solomon/weyer.htm); [Indiana University's record of the 1577 edition](https://iucat.iu.edu/iuc/3154182).

His importance here is the publication of a predecessor to the catalogue, not authorship of all the Goetia's conjurations or evidence of attendance at one ceremony. See [[Goetia Historical Context]] for the distinction between publication history and ritual participants.
