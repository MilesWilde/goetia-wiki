# Compare the sources

These links connect reviewed accounts within this wiki. Correspondence does not mean the books agree: shared names, blended descriptions, and uncertain identifications retain their qualifications.

[[Entity Directory|All names]] · [[index|Wiki home]]

## Azael / Azäel

[[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Azael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azael — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Entities/Azäel (Goetia)|Azäel]]

**Shared name.** Same separately named rabbinic rebellious spirit; Goetia lists Azäel distinctly from Azazel. The Goetia attests the name without describing the confinement story given in the dictionary.

## Gabriel / Gabriel

[[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Gabriel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Gabriel — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Entities/Gabriel (Goetia)|Gabriel]]

**Corresponding accounts.** Both sources explicitly identify Gabriel as an angel in ceremonial or cabalistic correspondence lists. the dictionary assigns January and the fourth hour; Goetia places Gabriel in the Iesod/Moon sequence and calls him a messenger. The dictionary assigns a month and an hour; the Goetia gives a different set of ceremonial correspondences.

## Metatron / Metatron

[[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Metatron (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Metatron — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Entities/Metatron (Goetia)|Metatron]]

**Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary names Metatron as one of three superior cabalistic intelligences; Goetia explicitly names Metatron in the Kether correspondence and Methrattton in its explanatory prayer. The dictionary groups Metatron with Acatriel and Sandalphon; the Goetia places him among distinct angelic and divine correspondences.

[[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Sabaoth (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Sabaoth — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Names/Zabaoth (Goetia)|Zabaoth]]

**Corresponding accounts.** Handsome soldier on crocodile with ducal crown and mild disposition is the distinctive Sallos appearance. The dictionary calls him a count and gives no love-related office in its brief entry.

## Zagam / Zagan (Goetia)

[[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Zagam (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Zagam — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Spirits/Zagan|Zagan (Goetia)]]

**Corresponding accounts.** King/president as griffin-winged bull, changing liquids/metals and making fools wise. The transformations differ, and the dictionary gives thirty legions against the Goetia’s thirty-three.

[[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=723|Dictionary PDF 723]] · [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=45|Goetia PDF 45]] · [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=94|Goetia PDF 94]]

## Zepar / Zepar (Goetia)

[[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Zepar (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Zepar — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Spirits/Zepar|Zepar (Goetia)]]

**Corresponding accounts.** Duke in warrior form promoting sexual passions; name and form correspond to Zepar. The dictionary gives twenty-eight legions; the Goetia gives twenty-six. The dictionary only suggests an identification with Vepar.

[[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=724|Dictionary PDF 724]] · [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=31|Goetia PDF 31]]

## Sakhar / Sakhr

[[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Sakhar (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Sakhar — Infernal Dictionary]] · [[Entities/Sakhr (Goetia)|Sakhr]]

**Qualified comparison.** Dictionnaire Sakhar usurps Solomon’s throne and is later subdued by him. Goetia Sakhr/Sacar appears in a comparison with the Arabian Nights fisherman and brass-vessel story. The similar name and Solomon-related spirit context support comparison, but the sources do not explicitly equate their different narratives.
