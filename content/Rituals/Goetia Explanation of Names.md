# Explanation of Certain Names

Despite its title, this section is best read as **prayer arranged beside sacred names and diagram inscriptions**, not as a reliable dictionary. Its clauses ask for divine presence, angelic protection, assistance, and authority over spirits. It also supplies prayer-like expansions beside the inscriptions of the pentagram, hexagram, and triangle. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|Names and prayers, PDF 69–71, printed 53–55]].

## What kinds of names are mixed together?

The list combines [[Goetia Divine Names|names of God]], [[Goetia Angels and Orders|angels and angelic orders]], and terms associated with celestial spheres or the Sephiroth. These are different categories. For example, the prayer calls on [[Michael (Goetia)|Michael]] as a heavenly commander, whereas other entries refer to divine attributes or parts of a cosmological arrangement. Their placement in the same column does not make them all individual spirits. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|Mixed categories, PDF 69–70]].

The triangle explanation asks for authority to restrain the summoned spirit until discharge. This connects it with the main ritual's concern for controlled appearance and departure, rather than introducing a fresh episode after the [[Goetia Licence to Depart|Licence]]. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=71|Triangle explanation, PDF 71]].

## Why some entries do not have biographies

The passage supplies functions within a prayer, not biographies. Some entries are titles, formula words, or uncertain spellings; others are beings also described elsewhere. [[Mathers (Goetia)|Mathers]] notes defective spelling and calls the passage a paraphrased prayer; [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]] rejects its glosses as literal meanings. The wiki therefore distinguishes “this text associates a prayer with this word” from “this word literally means that prayer.” [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|Translator and editor notes, PDF 69]].

The electronic editor locates the material on the last leaf of Sloane MS 2731 and discusses its association with *Ars Nova*, suggesting a use connected with preparing the circle. That is a later editorial explanation, not an explicit stage mandated by the preceding dismissal. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|Electronic editor's note, PDF 96]].
