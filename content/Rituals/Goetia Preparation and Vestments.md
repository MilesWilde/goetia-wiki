# Preparation and Vestments

The section headed “The Other Magical Requisites” describes the operator's ceremonial clothing, equipment, washing, and prayers. Its purpose is to establish purity, protection, and the right to speak with divine authority before the summons begins. The clothes are interpreted as spiritual armour, rather than ordinary costume. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=60|Requisites and prayers, PDF 60, printed 44]].

## Who is involved?

An unnamed human operator addresses [[God (Goetia)|God]]. The washing prayer invokes the penitential language attributed to [[David (Goetia)|David]]; the vestment prayer appeals to the Most High and sacred names. [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] appears as the supposed authority behind the prescribed practice. Neither David nor Solomon is presented as a physically present assistant to this operator. No particular catalogue spirit has yet been selected in these prayers. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=60|PDF 60]].

## Historical layer

This belongs to the English ritual framework discussed in [[Goetia Historical Context]], not to a securely dated ceremony in biblical history. The electronic editor compares the washing words with Psalm 51 and Christian liturgical usage, and the vestment prayer with the *Key of Solomon* and *Heptameron*. Those comparisons explain why Christian prayer and ceremonial magic appear together in this text. They do not establish that an institution authorised the ritual. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|Electronic editor's comparisons, PDF 96, printed 80]].

The [[Goetia Angelic-Language Conjurations|later angelic-language section]] supplies a parallel version of this preparation, including the washing prayer in Latin. It is a second presentation of the material, not evidence of a second historical event. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=75|Parallel prayers, PDF 75, printed 59]].

## The protective objects

|---|---|---|

These three figures precede the clothing section. The text assigns each a different function: the hexagram is displayed to command an obedient human appearance, the pentagram is worn for protection and authority, and the ring or disc is held before the face against the spirit's dangerous breath. They are illustrated on [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=54|PDF 54]] and explained on [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=55|PDF 55]]. The [[Goetia Illustrations|illustration gallery]] also shows the circle, triangle, vessel, and secret seal.
