# Conjuration of Fire

This formula addresses **fire as an instrument of compulsion**. The operator calls on the power of the creator to make the fire punish a spirit that still refuses to appear. It is not an introduction to an otherwise unidentified fire spirit. The chosen spirit remains N. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=65|Conjuration of the Fire, PDF 65, printed 49]].

## What the material imagery means

The preceding rubric places the spirit's seal or written name in a container. The subsequent threat treats that representation as connected with the spirit itself: destruction of the image or name is presented as punishment of its owner. The text moves from spoken authority to a threatened action against a material substitute. This is an interpretation of the sequence, not independent evidence that the action could affect a spirit. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=65|Rubric and formula, PDF 65]].

The speaker explicitly claims to be the servant of [[God (Goetia)|God]], using Iehovah among the divine names. The demands still include peaceful appearance and freedom from injury to the operator. Fire serves the claimed divine jurisdiction; it does not replace that authority. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=65|PDF 65]].

## Place in the book

This is one of the later, conditional coercive stages. It follows the [[Goetia Spirits Chain|Spirits' Chain]] and leads to the [[Goetia Greater Curse|Greater Curse]] if refusal continues. The printed sequence is an early modern ritual script transmitted through this edition, not a dated account of an actual burning or apparition. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=65|Transition, PDF 65–66]]; [[Goetia Historical Context]].
