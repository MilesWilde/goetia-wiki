# Address and Welcome

These short sections govern the encounter **after the spirit is understood to have appeared**. The Address asserts the operator's authority; the Welcome acknowledges the spirit's arrival and introduces the requests. They follow a successful summons, whether or not the earlier escalation was necessary. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=66|Transition upon appearance, PDF 66]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=67|Address and Welcome, PDF 67, printed 51]].

## The Address

The operator presents the pentacle of [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] as a sign of authority and uses the title [[Octinomos (Goetia)|Octinomos]]. Here Octinomos is a designation claimed by the speaker, not the name of another spirit entering the scene. The formula situates that speaker under divine protection and requires obedience. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=67|Address, PDF 67]].

A note disputes the treatment of the short words following this address as divine names. The disagreement is a reminder that the edition preserves interpretations and textual difficulties alongside the ritual wording. Those words should not automatically be turned into separate character biographies. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=67|Footnotes to the Address, PDF 67]].

## The Welcome

The greeting becomes courteous, using an appropriate rank such as king. It recognises the spirit's compliance while requiring it to remain visible until the operator's business is finished and leave is granted. The following direction says that the operator now states their desires and requests: the book does not supply one fixed objective for every spirit. The [[Goetia Offices and Motifs|catalogue's offices]] help explain why a particular spirit might be chosen. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=67|Welcome and concluding rubric, PDF 67]].

The participants are the anonymous operator and selected spirit. Solomon supplies the traditional authority of the emblem; he is not a witness to an identifiable performance. The early modern text and its later editions are discussed in [[Goetia Historical Context]]. The electronic editor also identifies a *Heptameron* parallel for the Address. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|Editorial comparison, PDF 96]].
