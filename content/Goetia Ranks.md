# Goetia ranks

This index follows **the titles in each numbered description**. It preserves dual ranks instead of assigning each spirit to a single tier. The later classified table appears on [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=50|PDF page 50, printed page 36]].

**[[Ipos]]** is an Earl and Prince in his entry; **[[Murmur]]** is a Duke and Earl. The later table omits both from its Earls list. They remain in both of their catalogue groups here. These are source disagreements, not extra spirits. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=33|Ipos, PDF 33]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=43|Murmur, PDF 43]].

| Rank group | Profiles | Seal material in the classified table |
|---|---|---|
| [[Goetia Ranks#Kings\|Kings]] | 9 | Gold |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Dukes\|Dukes]] | 23 | Copper |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Princes and Prelates\|Princes and Prelates]] | 7 | Tin |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Marquises\|Marquises]] | 15 | Silver |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Presidents\|Presidents]] | 14 | Mercury |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Earls and Counts\|Earls and Counts]] | 12 | Copper and silver in equal portions |
| [[Goetia Ranks#Knights\|Knights]] | 1 | Lead |

The groups contain **72 distinct spirits and 81 rank memberships**. Prince and Prelate are treated as interchangeable in the table's note; Earl and Count are alternative titles for the same group.

The table's note proposes **iron for Earls** and a **mixture for Presidents** as an alternative to its own listed metals. Planetary symbols are handwritten additions from [[Aleister Crowley (Goetia)|Crowley]]'s copy according to the electronic editor. Neither proposal changes a spirit's numbered identity. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=50|PDF 50–51]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=94|editorial note, PDF 94]].
