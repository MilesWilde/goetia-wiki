# Beleth

The catalogue presents Beleth as its thirteenth spirit, a mighty and terrible king whose office is to cause love between men and women.

## Appearance

He rides a pale horse, preceded by trumpets and other musical instruments. His first appearance is furious. A footnote describes flaming breath, explaining the protective ring motif in the accompanying ceremonial account.

## Offices and powers

The text attributes to Beleth the production of love among men and women until the exorcist’s desire is fulfilled. It gives him eighty-five legions and places him in the Order of Powers.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry supplies Bileth and Bilet as alternative names. Its ceremonial imagery includes a hazel wand, an external triangle, a silver ring, and respectful reception appropriate to a king; the ring is compared with the treatment of [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]]. The later vessel narrative names Bileth among four chiefs with [[Belial]], [[Asmoday]], and [[Gäap|Gaap]]. The editorial notes report that the Pseudomonarchia associates Beleth with hope of returning to the Seventh Throne, while questioning that hope. That comparison belongs to the later notes rather than the numbered description.
