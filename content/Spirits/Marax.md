# Marax

The catalogue presents Marax as its twenty-first spirit, a great earl and president associated with astronomy, the liberal sciences, and knowledgeable familiars.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 21 |
| Rank(s) | Earl; President |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Morax |
| Legions | 30 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears as a great bull with a man’s face. The description presents this as a single combined form. It gives no separate mount, clothing, voice, or subsequent human transformation, and the man’s face does not receive an independent identity.

## Offices and powers

Marax makes people knowledgeable in astronomy and all the other liberal sciences. He can also give good, wise familiars who know the virtues of herbs and precious stones. In the wording of the entry, that natural knowledge is attributed to the familiars he provides. Thirty legions of spirits fall under his government.

## Relationships and textual notes

Both Earl and President are stated as ranks in the same entry. A footnote records Morax in some codices while expressing the writer’s preference for Marax as the correct spelling. That preference does not remove the alternative attestation. The seal is associated with the catalogue’s recurring worn-character motif.
