# Haures

According to the *Goëtia*, Haures answers about events and creation, burns enemies, and protects against other spirits’ temptation.

## Appearance

Haures first appears as a powerful, terrifying leopard. He subsequently takes human shape with flaming eyes and a dreadful face. The catalogue identifies him as a great Duke.

## Offices and powers

He answers questions about past, present, and future events, creation, divinity, and the fall of himself and other spirits. The account qualifies his truthfulness with a warning about deception under improper constraint. It also credits him with burning and destroying enemies and preventing temptation by other spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

Hauras, Havres, and Flauros are explicit alternatives in the heading. The main description assigns thirty-six legions. The edition’s later note, filed under Flauros, remarks that this is sixteen more than in the Pseudomonarchia. The warning about unreliable answers and the destructive powers both belong to the catalogue’s own characterization.
