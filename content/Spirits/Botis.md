# Botis

The catalogue presents Botis as its seventeenth spirit, simultaneously a great president and an earl, with offices in knowledge and reconciliation.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 17 |
| Rank(s) | President; Earl |
| Aliases attested in this edition | None stated |
| Legions | 60 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

His first appearance is an ugly viper. On the magician’s command, he changes to human shape with great teeth and two horns. In this human appearance he carries a bright, sharp sword. The viper and armed humanoid are successive forms of the same spirit.

## Offices and powers

Botis tells things past and things to come. He reconciles friends and foes, extending his conciliatory office beyond existing friendships. He rules sixty legions of spirits. The entry does not assign a separate destructive office to the sword that forms part of his appearance.

## Relationships and textual notes

The dual rank is explicit in the individual description and is repeated by his inclusion in both the presidents’ and earls’ sections of the later classified list. Neither rank replaces the other. His seal accompanies the account, while a former angelic order and a named personal superior are not specified there.
