# Buné

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Buné as a spirit who moves the dead, gathers spirits at sepulchres, grants wealth and eloquence, and answers questions.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 26 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Bune, Bimé, Bim |
| Legions | 30 |

## Appearance

Buné appears as a dragon with three heads: those of a dog, a gryphon, and a human. His voice is described as high and pleasing. Graves and the gathering of subordinate spirits provide a second set of motifs alongside the composite dragon.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue credits him with changing the location of the dead and assembling the spirits under his authority at sepulchres. He grants wealth, wisdom, and eloquence, and gives truthful answers to questions. These offices connect burial places and spiritual attendants with material riches and articulate speech.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry prints two seals and prefers the second. The later edition notes explicitly disagree: the version in Sloane 3825 prefers the more elaborate first seal. A separate editorial discussion identifies Buné as an exception to the usually minor differences between paired seals, because his two designs differ substantially. Bimé appears in the heading; the body gives the shorter Bim.
