# Ipos

The catalogue describes Ipos as its twenty-second spirit, an earl and mighty prince associated with knowledge of events, wit, and boldness.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 22 |
| Rank(s) | Earl; Prince |
| Aliases attested in this edition | None stated |
| Legions | 36 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears in the form of an angel with a lion’s head, a goose’s foot, and a hare’s tail. These animal elements are combined in the angelic figure. The entry gives no account of a separate transformation into an ordinary human shape.

## Offices and powers

Ipos knows things past, present, and to come. He makes people witty and bold. The text assigns thirty-six legions of spirits to his government without giving individual tasks or names to those followers.

## Relationships and textual notes

The individual entry explicitly assigns both Earl and Prince. The later classified list includes Ipos among princes and prelates but omits him from the earls. The editorial appendix’s decan table instead marks number twenty-two with the symbol used for Earl. These differences remain visible rather than being reconciled into one exclusive rank. His angelic appearance does not by itself identify a former angelic order.

## Compare the other source

[[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Ipes (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ipes — Infernal Dictionary]]. **Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary also calls him Ayperos; angel/lion, goose feet, hare tail, knowledge, boldness and thirty-six legions. The dictionary repeats similar traits under Valafar, creating a discrepancy rather than establishing that the names are interchangeable.
