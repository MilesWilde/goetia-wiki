# Räum

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Räum as a spirit who takes royal treasure, destroys cities and dignities, reveals events, and causes love between friends and enemies.

| Number | 40 |
| Rank(s) | Earl |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Raum |
| Legions | 30 |
| Former angelic order | Thrones |

## Appearance

Räum initially appears as a crow, then assumes human shape. The entry does not add clothing, weapons, or a mount. Crow and human are successive forms of the same spirit in this account.

## Offices and powers

He steals treasure from kings’ houses and carries it to a designated place. He destroys cities and human dignities, reveals past, present, and future events, and causes love between friends and foes. The text joins destructive and conciliatory offices without resolving their contrast.

## Relationships and textual notes

Räum formerly belonged to the Thrones. His authority extends over thirty legions, and the catalogue gives him the title of great Earl. The names table prints the unaccented form Raum; the main entry and classified list retain Räum. His treasure office concerns taking wealth from royal houses rather than discovering an unspecified buried hoard. The destruction of dignities is stated separately from the destruction of cities, making loss of status a distinct element of the description. His seal accompanies the entry.
