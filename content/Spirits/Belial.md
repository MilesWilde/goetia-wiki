# Belial

According to the *Goëtia*, Belial grants appointments and senatorships, favour from friends and foes, and excellent familiars.

## Appearance

Belial appears as two beautiful angels seated in a chariot of fire and speaks with an agreeable voice. The entry says he was created immediately after [[Lucifer (Goetia)|Lucifer]] and attributes to him a claim to have fallen first among the worthier angels who preceded [[Michael (Goetia)|Michael]] and other heavenly angels.

## Offices and powers

He distributes appointments and senatorships, obtains favour from friends and enemies, and gives excellent familiars. The account portrays his truthfulness as unstable and dependent on conditions and divine constraint.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue prints eighty legions. Its footnotes successively suggest thirty, claim fifty for 1898, and return to eighty in a later annotation. The editorial notes confirm eighty in the Pseudomonarchia. The vessel narrative names Belial among four chief spirits; after release he alone enters an image and answers its worshippers, while the others return to their former places.
