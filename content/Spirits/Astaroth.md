# Astaroth

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Astaroth as a spirit who reveals secrets and events, explains the spirits’ fall, and teaches liberal sciences.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 29 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Asteroth |
| Legions | 40 |

## Appearance

Astaroth appears as a harmful angel riding an infernal beast resembling a dragon. He carries a viper in his right hand. His breath is described as dangerous, and the account associates a protective magical ring with this danger. Angel, dragon-like mount, serpent, and ring form a distinctive cluster of imagery.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue credits him with truthful answers about past, present, and future events and with the disclosure of every secret. He can explain how the spirits fell and give the reason for his own fall. He also makes people highly knowledgeable in the liberal sciences.

## Relationships and textual notes

Although the entry describes an angelic appearance and a personal fall, it does not name a former angelic order. Its warning about breath is distinct from its promises of knowledge. The table accompanying the Hebrew names uses the spelling Asteroth; the main catalogue and rank list use Astaroth. The description includes a seal associated with his appearance and obedience.
