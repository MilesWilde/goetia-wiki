# Oriax

According to the *Goëtia*, Oriax teaches stellar and planetary virtues, transforms people, and grants honours and favour.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 59 |
| Rank(s) | Marquis |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Orias |
| Legions | 30 |

## Appearance

Oriax appears as a lion, with a footnote offering the alternative of a lion’s face. He rides a powerful horse and holds two large hissing serpents in his right hand. A serpent’s tail is also mentioned, with its owner ambiguous in the English sentence.

## Offices and powers

He teaches the virtues of the stars and the mansions and virtues of the planets. He transforms people, grants dignities and prelacies, confirms those honours, and obtains favour from friends and enemies.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue gives Oriax and Orias; the rank table uses Orias. An editorial note explicitly asks whether the serpent’s tail belongs to the horse or the Marquis. The added Latin comparison favours the Marquis. This ambiguity remains separate from the clearly stated pair of serpents carried in his hand.
