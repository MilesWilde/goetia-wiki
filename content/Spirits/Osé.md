# Osé

According to the *Goëtia*, Osé teaches liberal sciences, answers divine and secret questions, and changes a person’s form and self-perception.

| Legions | 3 in main text; footnote suggests 30 |

## Appearance

Osé first appears as a leopard and after a short interval assumes a man’s shape. The entry identifies him as a great President.

## Offices and powers

He makes people skilled in the liberal sciences and answers questions about divine and secret things. He can change a person into a chosen shape. The account adds that the changed person becomes convinced that they truly are the creature or thing whose shape they have received.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading uses Osé and Voso; the body adds Oso, and the rank list prints Ose. The printed legion count is 3 followed by a superscript footnote marker, not 31. That footnote proposes 30. The later editorial notes state that the Pseudomonarchia gives no count and characterize the Goëtia’s number as a compiler’s guess.
