# Alloces

According to the *Goëtia*, Alloces teaches astronomy and liberal sciences and provides good familiar spirits.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |

| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Alocas |
| Legions | 36 |

## Appearance

Alloces appears as a soldier or warrior riding a great horse. His face resembles a lion’s and is very red, with flaming eyes. His voice is described as hoarse and unusually forceful.

## Offices and powers

His teaching covers astronomy and all the liberal sciences. The entry also credits him with bringing good familiars. These are the explicit offices given alongside his command of thirty-six legions.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry names Alloces and Alocas as alternatives. A footnote offers “warrior” for the main text’s soldier. Another preserves the manuscript’s unusual description of his speech as very big; the edition’s added Latin gloss interprets it as speaking harshly. The horseback warrior and lion face are visual details, while his assigned services concern learning and familiar spirits.
