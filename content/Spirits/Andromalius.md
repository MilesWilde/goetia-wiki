# Andromalius

According to the *Goëtia*, Andromalius recovers thieves and stolen goods, exposes wrongdoing, punishes offenders, and finds hidden treasure.

## Appearance

Andromalius appears as a man holding a great serpent in his hand. The catalogue calls him a great and mighty Earl and places him last in its numbered sequence of seventy-two spirits.

## Offices and powers

His office includes bringing back both a thief and the stolen goods. He exposes wickedness and secret dealing, punishes thieves and other wrongdoers, and discovers concealed treasure. The recovery of property and the return of the person who stole it are both stated in the description.

## Relationships and textual notes

The serpent is the defining object of his appearance; his listed functions concern discovery, recovery, and punishment. The entry gives him thirty-six legions. The edition’s later notes group Andromalius with [[Seere]] and [[Dantalion]] as absent from the Pseudomonarchia. This page preserves the catalogue’s Earl rank and its distinction between stolen goods and separately hidden treasure.
