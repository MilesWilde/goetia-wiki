# Amdusias

According to the *Goëtia*, Amdusias produces instrumental music, bends trees, and gives excellent familiars.

| Field | Catalogue record |

| Rank(s) | Duke |

| Legions | 29 |

## Appearance

Amdusias first appears as a unicorn and subsequently takes human shape. Trumpets and other musical instruments are heard in connection with that manifestation; the text notes that the sound does not begin immediately.

## Offices and powers

The main entry says that he can cause trees to bend or incline according to the exorcist’s will. It also credits him with providing excellent familiars. His government is given as twenty-nine legions.

## Relationships and textual notes

Amdukias is the alternative name in the numbered heading. The edition’s later note treats the tree passage as a mistranslation or misunderstood Latin idiom in the English Pseudomonarchia tradition. It suggests that the underlying phrase about trees bending the knee probably means their being subject to the exorcist. This editorial interpretation is distinguished from the catalogue’s literal bending-tree description.
