# Leraje

The catalogue describes Leraje as its fourteenth spirit, a powerful marquis associated with battles, contests, and the worsening of wounds made by arrows.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 14 |
| Rank(s) | Marquis |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Leraikha; Leraie; Lerajé |
| Legions | 30 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears as an archer dressed in green, carrying a bow and quiver. The equipment belongs directly to the described figure. The text also connects him with Sagittar, but does not expand that reference into a separate description of his body or retinue.

## Offices and powers

Leraje causes great battles and contests. He makes wounds inflicted by archers’ arrows putrefy. The wound effect is specifically linked to arrows in the text, rather than described as a general power over every injury. He governs thirty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading supplies Leraikha alongside Leraje, while the opening sentence gives Leraie as another spelling. The later classified rank list prints Lerajé with an accent. These forms are retained as edition-attested variants of number fourteen. His visual archer motif and his stated arrow-wound office reinforce one another within the account without establishing additional powers.
