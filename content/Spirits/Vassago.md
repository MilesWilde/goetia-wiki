# Vassago

The catalogue presents Vassago as its third spirit, a mighty prince described as good-natured and concerned with knowledge of events and concealed things.

## Appearance

The individual entry gives no bodily form, clothing, animal companion, or distinctive voice. Its identifying description emphasizes his nature and office instead. The accompanying seal supplies the entry’s visual sign.

## Offices and powers

Vassago declares things past and things to come. He also discovers things that are hidden or lost. These are stated as general offices, without an account of how a discovery is communicated. His government extends to twenty-six legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The text says that he is of the same nature as [[Agares]], but does not explain how that comparison relates to its separate description of Vassago as good-natured. The later editorial notes say that Vassago is absent from the Pseudomonarchia and report [[A. E. Waite (Goetia)|Waite]]’s discussion of his use in crystallomancy. Those are editorial comparisons, not additional offices in the numbered entry.
