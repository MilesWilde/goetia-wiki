# Gremory

According to the *Goëtia*, Gremory reveals events and concealed treasure and obtains the love of women.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 56 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Gamori |
| Legions | 26 |

## Appearance

Gremory appears as a beautiful woman riding a great camel. A duchess’s crown is tied around her waist. The catalogue nevertheless uses masculine pronouns for the spirit and calls Gremory a strong and powerful Duke.

## Offices and powers

The entry credits Gremory with telling of past, present, and future things. It also specifies knowledge of hidden treasures and what contains them. A further office is procuring the love of women, expressly including both younger and older women.

## Relationships and textual notes

Gamori is the alternative name supplied in the same numbered entry. The feminine manifestation, ducal rank, and crown at the waist are all details of this edition’s wording. Treasure discovery here includes information about the treasure’s enclosure or location; the text’s separate love-granting claim is specifically framed around women.
