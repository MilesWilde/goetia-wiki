# Shax

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Shax as a spirit who removes faculties, steals royal money, fetches things, reveals unguarded hidden objects, and sometimes gives familiars.

## Appearance

Shax appears as a stock-dove and speaks in a voice that is both hoarse and subtle. The triangle is a recurring ceremonial motif in the entry’s account of deception and compelled reliability.

## Offices and powers

He removes sight, hearing, or understanding from a person. He steals money from kings’ houses, with a stated return after 1,200 years, and can fetch horses or other things. He reveals hidden things unless wicked spirits guard them. He sometimes supplies good familiars; the qualification belongs to the main text.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue warns that Shax deceives and lies outside the specified constraint. Its promises should therefore be read alongside its explicit limitations, particularly the exception for hidden things protected by spirits. Shaz and Shass are given as alternate names. He is a great Marquis with thirty legions and an individual seal. The 1,200-year interval concerns the return of stolen money here; unlike the entries for [[Marchosias]] and [[Phenex]], it is not a hope of return to a throne.
