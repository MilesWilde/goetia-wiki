# Furfur

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Furfur as a spirit who causes love, raises lightning and storms, and answers questions about secret and divine matters.

## Appearance

Furfur first appears as a hart with a fiery tail. The catalogue also gives him an angelic form and describes his speaking voice as hoarse. A triangle forms part of the account’s imagery of compelled appearance and speech.

## Offices and powers

He promotes love between a man and a woman. The text also credits him with lightning, thunder, violent gusts, and great storms. He can give truthful answers about secret and divine matters. The weather powers are stated explicitly, rather than inferred from the fire in his initial appearance.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry makes truthfulness conditional: it says Furfur ordinarily does not speak the truth, then associates his angelic form and truthful answers with constraint. Its warning and promises therefore occur within the same account. No individual superior, named companion, or former angelic order is specified. The catalogue calls him a great and mighty Earl and places twenty-six legions under his authority; his seal is printed with the entry.
