# Crocell

According to the *Goëtia*, Crocell teaches geometry and liberal sciences, produces the sound of rushing water, warms water, and discovers baths.

## Appearance

The catalogue gives Crocell the form of an angel. His speech concerns hidden things and is described as mystical. The entry associates him with the sound of great rushing waters, even when no water is present to make that sound.

## Offices and powers

Crocell teaches geometry and the liberal sciences. He can produce a great noise resembling many rushing waters, warm water, and discover baths. These are distinct claims: the noise can occur without actual water, while warming water and finding baths concern water itself.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry attributes Crocell’s account of his former angelic standing to his own declaration to [[Solomon (Goetia)|King Solomon]]. It names that order both as Potestates and as Powers. The name Crokel is presented as an alternative to Crocell within the same numbered entry.
