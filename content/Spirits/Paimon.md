# Paimon

The catalogue presents Paimon as its ninth spirit, a great king obedient to [[Lucifer (Goetia)|Lucifer]], with offices in knowledge, dignity, subjection, and familiar service.

## Appearance

He is a crowned man seated on a dromedary. A host of human-like spirits precedes him with trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments. His first arrival includes roaring and speech the magician finds difficult to understand.

## Offices and powers

Paimon teaches arts, sciences, and secrets, including the nature of the earth, its support in the waters, and the nature and location of mind. He grants and confirms dignity, makes people subject to the magician, and provides good familiars able to teach arts.

## Relationships and textual notes

The account associates him with the West and the Order of Dominations, glossed as Dominions. His two hundred legions include Angels and Potentates. It also names attendant kings [[Labal (Goetia)|Labal]] and [[Abalim (Goetia)|Abalim]], other Potentates, and twenty-five legions. The passage does not clearly explain how that smaller group relates to the total. It says their subordinate spirits are not always present with them. These details belong to the book’s ceremonial account of his retinue.
