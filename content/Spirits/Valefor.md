# Valefor

The catalogue describes Valefor as its sixth spirit, a mighty duke who can serve as a familiar but is also said to encourage theft.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 6 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | None stated |
| Legions | 10 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears as a lion with an ass’s head and makes a bellowing sound. The description combines the lion’s body and the ass’s head in one figure. It does not describe a later human form, a rider, or a separate animal companion.

## Offices and powers

The entry calls Valefor a good familiar and immediately qualifies that description: he tempts the people for whom he acts as a familiar to steal. Familiar service and temptation are therefore presented together. His rule encompasses ten legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The positive description of his service does not erase the stated temptation to theft; both claims belong to the same short account. The text says that his seal is worn whether or not the magician intends to take him as a familiar. It gives no named superior or former angelic order in the individual entry.
