# Decarbia

According to the *Goëtia*, Decarbia reveals the virtues of birds and precious stones and produces lifelike bird appearances.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 69 |
| Rank(s) | Marquis |

| Legions | 30 |

## Appearance

Decarbia first appears as a star within a pentacle and later assumes a man’s image. The description identifies him as a great Marquis.

## Offices and powers

He reveals the virtues of birds and precious stones. He also makes likenesses of every kind of bird appear and fly before the exorcist. These bird forms sing and drink as natural birds do, according to the entry.

## Relationships and textual notes

This edition spells the name Decarbia in the numbered description, the classified rank list, and the table of Hebrew names. That spelling is retained as the page’s primary name. The text calls the produced birds similitudes, so their resemblance and behaviour are preserved without turning the claim into a statement that he creates ordinary living animals. His rule is given as thirty legions of spirits.
