# Bathin

The catalogue describes Bathin as its eighteenth spirit, a mighty and strong duke associated with knowledge of natural substances and sudden travel.

## Appearance

He appears as a strong man with a serpent’s tail, seated on a pale-coloured mount. The main text tentatively identifies that mount as a horse, marking the word with brackets and a question mark. The serpent tail belongs to Bathin’s body rather than to a separate companion.

## Offices and powers

Bathin knows the virtues of herbs and precious stones. He can transport people suddenly from one country to another. The account gives this as an office affecting human travellers, separately from his own mounted appearance. He rules thirty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The footnote preserves a disagreement about the mount: the translator says the word is absent or indistinct in some older codices and tentatively reads horse; the editor says the quartos read ass. The wiki retains that uncertainty rather than selecting one reading. His seal is linked to the catalogue’s recurring worn-character motif.
