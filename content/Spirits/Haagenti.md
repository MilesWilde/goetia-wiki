# Haagenti

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Haagenti as a spirit who makes people wise, turns metals into gold, and changes water and wine into each other.

| --- | --- |
| Number | 48 |
| Rank(s) | President |

| Legions | 33 |

## Appearance

Haagenti initially appears as a mighty bull with the wings of a gryphon. He subsequently takes human form. The catalogue’s physical description is brief, with the winged bull as its principal visual motif.

## Offices and powers

He makes people wise and instructs them in various matters. He also transforms every metal into gold and changes wine into water or water into wine. These are explicitly attributed transformations of materials, separate from his own change of appearance.

## Relationships and textual notes

The main entry spells the name Haagenti, while the classified list of Presidents prints Häagenti. Both refer to numbered spirit forty-eight. He commands thirty-three legions, and the catalogue gives him a separate seal. The instruction he provides is left general: the entry does not identify particular mathematical or liberal disciplines. Likewise, the described liquid transformations concern water and wine specifically. The source combines wisdom and material transformation without assigning Haagenti a named superior or a former angelic order.
