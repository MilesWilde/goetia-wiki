# Furcas

According to the *Goëtia*, Furcas teaches philosophy, astrology, rhetoric, logic, palm divination, and fire divination.

## Appearance

Furcas appears as a cruel old man with a long beard and a grey or white-haired head. He rides a pale horse and carries a sharp weapon in one hand. The passage does not identify a more specific weapon type.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue assigns Furcas instruction in philosophy, astrology, rhetoric, and logic. It also includes cheiromancy and pyromancy: divination associated with the hand and with fire. The text describes his teaching as comprehensive within each of these named arts.

## Relationships and textual notes

Furcas is explicitly a Knight in his numbered description. The later classified list also places him under Knights and lists no other spirit in that category. His mounted, armed appearance accompanies a teaching office; the entry’s stated powers concern these intellectual and divinatory arts.
