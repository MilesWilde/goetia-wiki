# Seere

According to the *Goëtia*, Seere moves and carries things rapidly, brings events about suddenly, and reveals theft and hidden treasure.

| Legions | Not stated in catalogue entry |

## Appearance

Seere appears as a beautiful man riding a winged horse. The entry presents him as a mighty Prince with an agreeable disposition and a willingness to fulfil requests.

## Offices and powers

He can go and come rapidly, carry things to and from a desired place, and bring many events about suddenly. The catalogue describes his passage over the earth as nearly instantaneous. He also gives truthful information about theft, concealed treasure, and other matters.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry explicitly places Seere under [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]], King of the East. Its heading prints Sear, its body prints Sea, and both include Seir; the Hebrew-name table uses Seeré. No legion count appears in this description. The later editorial notes group Seere with [[Dantalion]] and [[Andromalius]] as absent from the Pseudomonarchia.
