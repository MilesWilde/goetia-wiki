# Volac

According to the *Goëtia*, Volac reveals hidden treasure and the locations of serpents, and brings serpents to the questioner.

## Appearance

Volac appears as a child with angel’s wings, riding a dragon with two heads. The catalogue calls him a mighty and great President. The child, wings, and double-headed mount are the distinctive elements of this manifestation.

## Offices and powers

He gives truthful answers about concealed treasure and identifies places where serpents can be found. The passage adds that he brings the serpents to the exorcist without the latter having to use force or strength.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading supplies Volac, Valak, Valu, and Ualuc; its opening sentence repeats the first three. The classified rank list instead spells the name Valac. These are recorded as variants within this edition. The serpents are part of his assigned office as well as a separate motif from the dragon he rides.
