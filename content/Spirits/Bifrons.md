# Bifrons

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Bifrons as a spirit who teaches sciences and material properties, relocates dead bodies, and produces apparent grave lights.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 46 |
| Rank(s) | Earl |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Bifröus, Bifrovs |
| Legions | 6 in the main text; note proposes 60 and reports [[Wier (Goetia)\|Wier]]’s 26 |

## Appearance

Bifrons first appears as a monster, then takes human shape. The monster’s anatomy is not specified. Graves, relocated bodies, and lights resembling candles provide the entry’s more distinctive imagery.

## Offices and powers

He teaches astrology, geometry, other arts and sciences, and the virtues of precious stones and woods. The catalogue also credits him with changing the location of dead bodies and producing what seem to be candles on their graves. It does not explain the material nature of those lights.

## Relationships and textual notes

The printed entry gives six legions, followed by a footnote marker. The note suggests sixty instead, while an added annotation reports twenty-six in [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]]. These are three textual readings, not a combined total of sixty-one. Bifröus and Bifrovs appear as alternate names. His title is Earl. The account describes movement of corpses rather than an explicit resurrection or dialogue with the dead, and supplies his individual seal alongside the description.
