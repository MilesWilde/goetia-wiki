# Andras

According to the *Goëtia*, Andras sows discord and is described as dangerous to the exorcist and companions.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 63 |
| Rank(s) | Marquis |

| Legions | 30 |

## Appearance

Andras appears as an angel with a head resembling a black night raven. He rides a strong black wolf and raises a bright, sharp sword in his hand. The entry calls him a great Marquis.

## Offices and powers

His specified office is to sow discord. The text also warns that he can kill the exorcist and the exorcist’s companions if sufficient care is not taken. That danger is presented directly alongside his discord-producing office.

## Relationships and textual notes

The bird identification here follows this edition’s wording: a black night raven. The angelic body describes his visible form; the entry does not assign it the status of a former angelic order. The wolf and sword reinforce the threatening appearance, while the actual office stated in the catalogue concerns causing discord. His command is given as thirty legions.
