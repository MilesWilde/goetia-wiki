# Forneus

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Forneus as a spirit who teaches rhetoric and languages, establishes a good reputation, and brings affection from friends and foes.

## Appearance

Forneus appears as a great sea-monster. The catalogue gives no more precise anatomy, mount, clothing, or alternate human form. His marine appearance is therefore a visual description, while the offices attributed to him concern learning and social standing.

## Offices and powers

He teaches rhetoric and makes people accomplished in it. He also grants the knowledge and understanding of languages, establishes a good name, and makes a person beloved by enemies as well as friends. Reputation, communication, and affection are the entry’s closely connected benefits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The twenty-nine legions under Forneus are said to consist partly of Thrones and partly of Angels. This statement describes his subordinate spirits; the entry does not expressly assign Forneus himself to either former order. His title is Marquis. The sea-monster form is not accompanied by a stated power over water or storms, so the catalogue’s marine imagery should remain distinct from his listed offices. An individual seal accompanies the account.
