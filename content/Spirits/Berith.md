# Berith

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Berith as a spirit who answers questions about time, turns metals to gold, and grants or confirms dignities.

| Rank(s) | Duke |

| Legions | 26 |

## Appearance

Berith takes the form of a soldier in red clothing, riding a red horse and wearing a golden crown. His voice is clear and subtle. The repeated red colouring, military dress, horse, and crown are the main material motifs.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue attributes truthful answers about past, present, and future events to him. It also says he can transform every metal into gold and confer or confirm human dignities. These are separate offices: knowledge, metal transformation, and the granting or maintenance of status.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry later describes Berith as a great liar who should not be trusted. A footnote draws attention to the contradiction with its earlier promise of truthful answers; the edition leaves both claims in place. The alternate names are explicitly described as names given by people of later times. A reference to a ring connects his ceremonial description with [[Beleth]], but the text does not identify the two spirits as one.
