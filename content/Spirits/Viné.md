# Viné

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Viné as a spirit who reveals hidden things and practitioners of magic, builds towers, destroys stone walls, and raises storms.

## Appearance

The main catalogue describes a lion riding a black horse and carrying a viper. A footnote records manuscripts that instead specify a lion’s head. The alternatives change the creature’s bodily description without changing the black mount or carried serpent.

## Offices and powers

Viné reveals hidden things, witches, wizards, and events of past, present, and future. He builds towers, overthrows great stone walls, and makes waters stormy. His listed offices therefore include discovery, construction, demolition, and disturbances of water.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue gives him two ranks, King and Earl, and the classified list includes him in both groups. It explicitly uses him as an example of a spirit with two titles. Vinea is an alternate name in the opening sentence, while the heading uses the unaccented Vine. He commands thirty-six legions. The image of a lion carrying a viper should be retained with the manuscript qualification, rather than silently replaced by a single harmonized human or animal form.
