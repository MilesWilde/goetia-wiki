# Sabnock

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Sabnock as a spirit who builds fortified places, equips them with armour, causes enduring infected sores, and supplies familiars.

| Number | 43 |

| Aliases attested in this edition | Savnok |
| Legions | 50 |

## Appearance

Sabnock appears as an armed soldier with a lion’s head, riding a pale horse. Military equipment, the lion head, and the light-coloured mount are specified features; the entry does not describe a separate later form.

## Offices and powers

He builds high towers, castles, and cities, and supplies them with armour. He also afflicts people for many days with wounds and rotting sores full of worms. Alongside construction and bodily harm, the catalogue credits him with providing good familiars.

## Relationships and textual notes

The opening sentence situates Sabnock within the spirits that [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] commanded into the brass vessel. Savnok is the alternate name given there. He is called a mighty, great, and strong Marquis, with fifty legions under his command. His wound-causing office resembles the imagery in [[Vepar]], but Sabnock’s entry specifies many days of affliction rather than [[Vepar]]’s stated death within three days. The catalogue provides a separate seal for Sabnock and names no individual superior in his entry.
