# Amon

The catalogue presents Amon as its seventh spirit, a powerful and stern marquis associated with knowledge of events and the making or settling of disputes.

| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

His initial appearance is a wolf with a serpent’s tail, emitting flames from its mouth. On command he takes a human shape with a raven-like head furnished with dog’s teeth; the text also gives the simpler alternative of a man with a raven’s head. These are variant descriptions within the entry.

## Offices and powers

Amon tells things past and to come. He brings about feuds and also reconciles controversies between friends. The catalogue consequently gives him both disruptive and conciliatory influence over relationships, without explaining a division between those offices. He governs forty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The fire, serpent tail, raven head, and dog’s teeth describe his manifestations rather than separately stated offices. The account calls for his seal as the familiar ceremonial identifying sign used elsewhere in the catalogue. It does not assign a former angelic order or name a personal superior.
