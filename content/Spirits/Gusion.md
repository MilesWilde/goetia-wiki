# Gusion

The catalogue presents Gusion as its eleventh spirit, a great and strong duke concerned with answers, reconciliation, and the granting of honour.

## Appearance

His appearance is compared to a Xenopilus. The entry supplies no explanation of that term and does not describe the figure’s anatomy, clothing, voice, or companions. Keeping the source’s term preserves an uncertainty in the description rather than replacing it with a particular animal or human type.

## Offices and powers

Gusion tells things past, present, and to come. He shows the meaning and resolution of questions put to him. He also conciliates and reconciles friendships and gives honour and dignity to people. His rule extends over forty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

Knowledge, social reconciliation, and dignity are separately stated offices of the same spirit. His seal is linked to the catalogue’s recurring instruction that the character be worn. No former angelic order is specified in this individual description. The unexplained appearance term should not be treated as an additional named entity on the strength of this passage alone.
