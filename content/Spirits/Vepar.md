# Vepar

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Vepar as a spirit who governs waters and armed shipping, raises storms, and causes fatal putrefying wounds.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 42 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Vephar |
| Legions | 29 |

## Appearance

Vepar appears as a mermaid. The catalogue gives no additional anatomy, clothing, or later human form. Water, ships carrying military supplies, and worm-filled wounds are the other concrete images associated with the entry.

## Offices and powers

He governs waters and guides ships carrying weapons, armour, and ammunition. He can make the sea stormy and cause it to appear full of ships. He also brings death within three days through putrefying wounds or sores in which worms breed. The description states both a maritime office and a separate power of bodily affliction.

## Relationships and textual notes

A footnote records a shorter manuscript wording that combines the guidance of waters and armour-laden ships. It does not remove the main account’s wider list of supplies. The appearance of many ships is phrased as an appearance, rather than explicitly as the creation of a real fleet. Vephar is an alternate name given in the catalogue. Vepar is a great and strong Duke commanding twenty-nine legions, with his own printed seal.
