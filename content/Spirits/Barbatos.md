# Barbatos

The catalogue describes Barbatos as its eighth spirit, a great duke whose offices concern animal communication, concealed treasure, knowledge of events, and reconciliation.

## Appearance

He appears when the sun is in Sagittary, accompanied by four noble kings and their large companies of troops. The account describes this retinue but does not specify Barbatos’s own bodily form. The four accompanying kings are unnamed.

## Offices and powers

Barbatos grants understanding of birdsong and other creatures’ voices, including the barking of dogs. He opens hidden treasures protected by magicians’ enchantments. He knows things past and to come and conciliates friends and people in power. His authority extends over thirty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry places him in the Order of Virtues and says that he still retains some part of it; it does not explain what that retained portion consists of. The four kings and their troops belong to his described appearance, without individual names or offices. His character is called a seal of obedience.
