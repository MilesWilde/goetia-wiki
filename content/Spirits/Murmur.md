# Murmur

According to the *Goëtia*, Murmur teaches philosophy and brings deceased souls to answer questions.

## Appearance

Murmur appears as a warrior riding a gryphon and wearing a ducal crown. Ministers precede him sounding great trumpets. The crown, mount, and heralding attendants give the entry a distinctly ceremonial military appearance.

## Offices and powers

He teaches philosophy with the completeness claimed by the catalogue. His other office is to compel the souls of deceased people to appear and answer questions. The passage presents this as questioning the dead, rather than as a separate claim to restore them to life.

## Relationships and textual notes

The main entry gives him both Duke and Earl, and names Murmus and Murmux as alternatives. The later classified list includes Murmur among Dukes but omits him from its Earls. This page retains the two ranks explicitly stated in his description. His earlier standing is described as partly Thrones and partly Angels.
