# Ronové

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Ronové as a spirit who teaches rhetoric and languages, supplies servants, and obtains favour from friends and enemies.

## Appearance

Ronové is described only as a monster. The catalogue supplies no animal species, clothing, equipment, colour, or sequence of changing forms. His individual seal accompanies the entry, but the prose leaves the body’s details undefined.

## Offices and powers

His principal teaching office is rhetoric. The text also credits him with knowledge of languages, good servants, and favours from friends or enemies. Its brief list therefore combines skilled speech, practical service, and influence over social relationships. The promise concerning enemies is favour rather than an explicit power to cause romantic love.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue names Ronové both Marquis and Great Earl. The edition’s classified list preserves this dual title by placing him in both rank groups. The entry does not identify any named superior, companion, or former angelic order. The nineteen legions belong to his command; they are distinct from the good servants listed among the benefits he supplies.
