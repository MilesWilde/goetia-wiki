# Aim

The catalogue presents Aim as its twenty-third spirit, a great and strong duke whose offices combine destructive fire, wit, and answers about private matters.

| --- | --- |

| Rank(s) | Duke |

| Legions | 26 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He has a handsome human body and three heads: a serpent’s head, a human head bearing two stars on the forehead, and a calf’s head. He rides a viper and carries a firebrand. The serpent head and the viper mount are distinct elements in this composite appearance.

## Offices and powers

With the firebrand he sets cities, castles, and great places on fire. He makes a person witty in many ways and gives true answers about private matters. His government comprises twenty-six legions of inferior spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The firebrand is both a visual object and the instrument linked to an explicitly destructive office. The stars are placed on the human forehead in the description, without a further explanation of their meaning. The numbered entry provides no former angelic order or named superior. His seal accompanies the account as the character identified with this spirit.
