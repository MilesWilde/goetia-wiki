# Glasya-Labolas

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Glasya-Labolas as a spirit who teaches arts and sciences, reveals past and future, causes bloodshed and affection, and grants invisibility.

## Appearance

The catalogue describes a dog with the wings of a gryphon. It specifies no later human form. The combination of an ordinary animal body and a mythical wing type is the entry’s principal visual motif; the printed page also supplies an individual seal.

## Offices and powers

Glasya-Labolas is credited with teaching every art and science immediately, and with knowledge of past and future events. The same entry makes him a cause of bloodshed and manslaughter. It also attributes to him the ability to cause affection among both friends and enemies and to make a person invisible.

## Relationships and textual notes

The main entry gives him both presidential and comital rank, and the classified list repeats him under both Presidents and Earls. Its brief description places instruction, violence, affection, and concealment together without explaining how these offices relate. It names no particular superior or individual companion.
