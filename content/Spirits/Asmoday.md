# Asmoday

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Asmoday as a spirit who teaches mathematical arts and crafts, gives answers and the Ring of Virtues, and reveals and guards treasure.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 32 |
| Rank(s) | King |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Asmodai |
| Legions | 72 |

## Appearance

Asmoday has the heads of a bull, a human, and a ram, a serpent’s tail, goose-like webbed feet, and flames issuing from his mouth. He rides an infernal dragon and carries a lance with a banner.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue credits him with the Ring of Virtues, complete instruction in arithmetic, astronomy, geometry, and handicrafts, and full truthful answers. He reveals treasure locations and guards them. Its main reading also promises invincibility; the edition notes argue that this is a copying error for invisibility, citing [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]] and [[Scot (Goetia)|Scot]].

## Relationships and textual notes

He is presented as foremost under [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]], with seventy-two inferior legions within that ruler’s forces. The vessel narrative also names him among four chief spirits. The later notes identify mistranslation and copying errors in the passage about the exorcist’s posture and head covering. They interpret the underlying passage as a warning about strength and caution, rather than accepting every ceremonial detail of the catalogue wording.
