# Zepar

The catalogue describes Zepar as its sixteenth spirit, a great duke whose offices concern love between women and men and the prevention of fertility.

## Appearance

He appears in red clothing and armour, resembling a soldier. The description offers these costume and military motifs without identifying a weapon, mount, distinctive voice, or alternate bodily form. The soldier-like appearance does not alter his stated rank as a duke.

## Offices and powers

Zepar causes women to love men and brings them together in love. The text then says that he also makes them barren; the pronoun follows the reference to women. The account places these effects alongside one another without explaining the relationship between them. He governs twenty-six legions of inferior spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

Love and barrenness are both explicit claims in the catalogue, so the latter should not be omitted when summarizing his otherwise amorous office. The entry associates obedience with the sight of his seal. It provides no former angelic order, named superior, or additional personal alias in the individual description.
