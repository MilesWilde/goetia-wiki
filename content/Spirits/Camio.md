# Camio

According to the *Goëtia*, Camio explains animal voices and waters, disputes well, and answers about future events.

## Appearance

Camio first appears as a thrush, then assumes a man’s shape and carries a sharp sword. The entry says that he seems to answer in burning ashes or coals of fire. It also describes him as a skilled disputant.

## Offices and powers

His office gives people understanding of birds, the lowing of cattle, the barking of dogs, and the sounds of other creatures. It extends this understanding to the voice of waters. He also gives truthful answers concerning things to come.

## Relationships and textual notes

The numbered description uses Camio and Caïm for the same spirit, while the classified list uses Caïm. The bird specified by this edition is a thrush. His former order is Angels, contrasted in the entry with his present government of thirty legions of infernal spirits.
