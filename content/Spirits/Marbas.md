# Marbas

The catalogue presents Marbas as its fifth spirit, a great president associated with secret knowledge, disease and healing, mechanical arts, and changes of human form.

| Rank(s) | President |

| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

His first form is a great lion. At the master’s request, he assumes a human shape. The entry gives these appearances in sequence and supplies no further description of the human body, clothing, or voice. The lion is the spirit’s own initial form.

## Offices and powers

Marbas answers truthfully about hidden or secret matters. He both causes diseases and cures them, so the catalogue assigns opposite effects within the same field to one spirit. He grants substantial wisdom and knowledge of mechanical arts and can change people into other shapes. He governs thirty-six legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The transformation office concerns people, separately from his own change between lion and human appearances. The numbered account does not assign him a former angelic order or name a superior spirit. It connects his seal with the previously described practice of wearing the spirit’s character.
