# Samigina

The catalogue describes Samigina as its fourth spirit, a great marquis whose offices combine instruction in the liberal sciences with knowledge of the dead.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 4 |
| Rank(s) | Marquis |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Gamigin |
| Legions | 30 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He first appears as a small horse or ass. At the master’s request, he changes into human shape. His speech has a hoarse sound. The two possible animal forms are alternatives within this entry, followed by the human appearance; the book does not describe them as separate spirits.

## Offices and powers

Samigina teaches all the liberal sciences. He also gives an account of dead souls who died in sin. The wording concerns reporting on those souls rather than expressly transporting or restoring them. He rules thirty legions of inferiors.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading gives both Samigina and Gamigin, while the body of the entry uses Samigina. The later table accompanying the Rudd Hebrew names labels number four Gamigin. The catalogue associates his seal with the magician’s worn ceremonial sign. These variant labels refer to the same numbered catalogue spirit in this edition.
