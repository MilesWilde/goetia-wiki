# Halphas

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Halphas as a spirit who builds and equips towers and sends warriors to appointed places.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 38 |
| Rank(s) | Earl |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Halphus, Malthus, Malthas |
| Legions | 26 |

## Appearance

The catalogue describes a stock-dove and a hoarse voice. A later annotator’s footnote reports a stork in [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]] instead. These are alternate descriptions preserved by the edition, rather than two successive forms in the main account.

## Offices and powers

Halphas builds towers, supplies them with ammunition and weapons, and sends fighting men to designated locations. A footnote clarifies that the expression used for these men means warriors or men-at-arms. The entry’s construction and provisioning offices are explicitly military.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading spells the primary name Halphus, while the opening sentence and rank list use Halphas. Malthus and Malthas are also given, with Malthas appearing in the names table. An editorial aside connects Malthus with Shelley and claims he is in heaven; it is separate from the catalogue’s description and does not establish a former angelic order. Halphas commands twenty-six legions and has his own seal. He is a separate numbered spirit from the following [[Malphas]].
