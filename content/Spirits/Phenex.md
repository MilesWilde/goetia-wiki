# Phenex

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Phenex as a spirit who speaks about the sciences and excels in poetry, appearing first as a phoenix with a child’s voice.

## Appearance

Phenex appears as the bird phoenix, with the voice of a child. He sings sweet notes before taking human shape. His account therefore joins a fabulous bird, youthful speech, song, and a later human appearance.

## Offices and powers

In human form he speaks remarkably about the sciences. The catalogue describes him as an excellent poet and willing to fulfil requests. It does not enumerate further particular services under that general promise. Scientific discourse and poetry are the named accomplishments.

## Relationships and textual notes

The narrative says Phenex told [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] that he hoped to return to the Seventh Throne after another 1,200 years. This is presented as the spirit’s hope, not as a confirmed future event. The same duration and destination also occur in the account of [[Marchosias]], though the entries remain separate. Phenex is a great Marquis commanding twenty legions. The parenthetical name Pheynix occurs in his opening sentence, and his individual seal is printed beside the description.
