# Orobas

According to the *Goëtia*, Orobas reveals events and divine matters, gives dignities and favour, and protects against other spirits’ temptation.

## Appearance

Orobas initially appears as a horse and subsequently takes a man’s image. The catalogue calls him a great and mighty Prince and emphasizes his faithfulness to the person addressing him.

## Offices and powers

He discloses past, present, and future matters, including truthful answers about divinity and the creation of the world. He grants dignities and prelacies and obtains the favour of both friends and enemies. The entry also says that he prevents other spirits from tempting the exorcist.

## Relationships and textual notes

The description combines a horse-to-human appearance with offices concerning knowledge, social standing, and spiritual protection. Its assurance of faithfulness belongs to the book’s characterization of Orobas. The protective claim specifically concerns temptation by another spirit, while the separate favour-granting claim covers human friends and foes.
