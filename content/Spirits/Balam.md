# Balam

According to the *Goëtia*, Balam answers about past, present, and future, grants invisibility, and makes people witty.

## Appearance

Balam has three heads, resembling a bull, a man, and a ram. He also has a serpent’s tail and flaming eyes. His mount is a furious bear, and he carries a goshawk on his fist. His voice is hoarse.

## Offices and powers

The catalogue credits Balam with truthful answers concerning past, present, and future events. He can make people invisible and make them witty. These human benefits accompany the elaborate animal imagery of his own manifestation.

## Relationships and textual notes

Balam and Balaam are given as two names for the same fifty-first spirit. The text calls him a terrible, great, and powerful King. The bear, bull, ram, serpent, and goshawk belong to his described appearance; the entry assigns his offices to knowledge, invisibility, and wit rather than to command over those animals.
