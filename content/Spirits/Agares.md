# Agares

The catalogue describes Agares as its second spirit, a duke under the power of the East whose offices include languages, movement, and the destruction of dignities.

## Appearance

He appears as a fair old man riding a crocodile and carrying a goshawk on his fist. Despite the animal mount and the destructive powers later attributed to him, the description calls his appearance mild. The crocodile and bird are visual companions; neither receives a personal name.

## Offices and powers

Agares makes those who stand still run and brings back runaways. He teaches all languages or tongues immediately. The text also assigns him the ability to destroy both spiritual and temporal dignities and to cause earthquakes. He governs thirty-one legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading uses Agares, while the opening sentence supplies Agreas as an alternative. He is said to have belonged to the angelic Order of Virtues. The next entry describes [[Vassago]] as sharing his nature without specifying which qualities are shared. His seal is associated with a worn lamen.
