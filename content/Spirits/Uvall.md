# Uvall

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Uvall as a spirit who procures women’s love, reveals events, and creates friendship between friends and enemies.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 47 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Vual, Voval |
| Legions | 37 |
| Former angelic order | Potestates or Powers |

## Appearance

Uvall first appears as a mighty dromedary, then assumes human shape. He speaks the Egyptian tongue imperfectly according to the main catalogue. The description does not give him a rider, carried object, or weapon.

## Offices and powers

His offices are obtaining the love of women, telling past, present, and future events, and creating friendship between friends and foes. The entry thus places romantic attraction and broader reconciliation alongside knowledge of events.

## Relationships and textual notes

The catalogue says Uvall formerly belonged to the Potestates, also called Powers, and assigns him thirty-seven legions. Vual and Voval appear as alternate names in the heading and opening sentence; the rank list uses Vual. An editorial footnote claims that he can now converse in sound colloquial Coptic. That later claim belongs to the annotation and differs from the main text’s imperfect Egyptian speech. The source calls him a great, mighty, and strong Duke and includes an individual seal.
