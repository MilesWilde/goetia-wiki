# Dantalion

According to the *Goëtia*, Dantalion teaches arts and sciences, reads and changes thoughts, causes love, and shows distant people in visions.

## Appearance

Dantalion appears as a man with many faces, including both men’s and women’s countenances. He holds a book in his right hand. The catalogue calls him a great and mighty Duke.

## Offices and powers

He teaches all arts and sciences and reveals other people’s secret counsels. The entry credits him with knowing the thoughts of men and women and changing those thoughts at will. He can also cause love and show a person’s likeness in a vision regardless of where that person is in the world.

## Relationships and textual notes

The book and multiple faces belong to his appearance, while learning, concealed intentions, altered thoughts, love, and distant visions are his specified offices. The edition’s later notes identify Dantalion, [[Seere]], and [[Andromalius]] as spirits absent from the Pseudomonarchia. His numbered entry assigns thirty-six legions.
