# Foras

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Foras as a spirit who teaches logic and ethics, grants longevity and eloquence, reveals treasure, and recovers lost things.

## Appearance

Foras appears as a strong man in human form. The catalogue names no mount, animal feature, weapon, or other item of clothing. His physical strength becomes relevant in a footnote discussing a disputed reading of one of his powers.

## Offices and powers

He teaches logic and ethics in all their parts. The main text also credits him with making people invisible, long-lived, and eloquent. He discovers treasures and recovers lost things. These offices combine intellectual instruction with prolonged life, concealed presence, and the retrieval of hidden or missing property.

## Relationships and textual notes

The accompanying note reports that one or two manuscripts read invincible where the majority read invisible. It suggests that his strong human appearance could support the minority reading, while speculating that such invincibility might be mental rather than physical. That interpretation belongs to the annotation, not the catalogue’s direct statement. The entry continues onto the next printed page, where the legion count is given as twenty-nine.
