# Marchosias

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Marchosias as a strong fighter associated with a fiery winged wolf and a hoped-for return to the Seventh Throne.

## Appearance

The main catalogue gives Marchosias a wolf’s body, gryphon’s wings, and a serpent’s tail, with fire coming from his mouth. He subsequently assumes human shape. A translator’s footnote reports that one poorly written manuscript could read ox instead of wolf; an editorial addition claims an ox-like appearance in personal experience.

## Offices and powers

His explicitly stated practical ability is strength in fighting. The entry is otherwise concerned with appearance, command, former order, and his expectation of restoration rather than a broad list of teaching or divinatory offices.

## Relationships and textual notes

Marchosias formerly belonged to the Dominations. He is said to have told [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]], called his chief, that he hoped to return to the Seventh Throne after 1,200 years. The later edition notes report that [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]] calls this hope mistaken. They also give the Pseudomonarchia description as a fierce she-wolf whose mouth emits an unspecified substance, preserving a difference from the catalogue’s definite fire.
