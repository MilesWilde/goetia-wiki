# Amy

According to the *Goëtia*, Amy teaches astrology and liberal sciences, gives familiars, and reveals treasure guarded by spirits.

| Field | Catalogue record |

| Legions | 36 |

## Appearance

Amy initially appears as a flaming fire, then takes the shape of a man. The catalogue calls Amy a great President and names Avnas as an alternative.

## Offices and powers

The entry assigns Amy instruction in astrology and all the liberal sciences. He also provides good familiars and reveals treasure kept by spirits. The treasure claim specifically involves spirit guardianship, while the familiar claim concerns spirits supplied as helpers.

## Relationships and textual notes

The main text uses an unusual phrase for making someone exceptionally knowledgeable. An editorial footnote notes that the corresponding Latin describes excellence in astrology and treats the added English word “knowing” as a probable interpolation. Another footnote explains the archaic verb “bewray” as revealing or disclosing. These annotations clarify the wording of the teaching and treasure offices.
