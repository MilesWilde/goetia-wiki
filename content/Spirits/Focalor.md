# Focalor

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Focalor as a spirit who rules winds and seas, drowns people, and overturns warships, with restraint also described.

| Legions | 30 in the main text; 3 in several manuscripts |

## Appearance

Focalor appears as a man with the wings of a gryphon. The catalogue gives him no further animal features, carried weapon, or mount. His winged human appearance accompanies offices centred on wind, sea, and shipping.

## Offices and powers

He kills people, drowns them in water, and overthrows ships of war. The entry expressly attributes power over both winds and seas to him. It also says that he will refrain from harming a person or thing when directed to do so; restraint is part of the same account as destruction.

## Relationships and textual notes

Focalor hopes to return to the Seventh Throne after 1,000 years. A footnote reports that [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]] regards this hope as mistaken. The main text assigns thirty legions, while another note records three in several manuscripts and prefers thirty; a further annotation identifies three as a mistranslation of [[Wier (Goetia)|Wier]]’s Latin. The superscript footnote after 30 is not part of the number. Forcalor and Furcalor are alternate names in the opening sentence.
