# Bael

The catalogue presents Bael as its first principal spirit, a king ruling in the East whose stated office is to make a person invisible.

## Appearance

Bael appears as a cat, a toad, or a man. The description also allows all three forms to appear together, rather than choosing one permanent body. His voice is hoarse. These animal and human forms belong to the account of his appearance; the entry does not give them separate names.

## Offices and powers

The power specifically assigned to Bael is invisibility. The entry couples this office with his authority over sixty-six legions of infernal spirits, but does not distribute different tasks among those legions.

## Relationships and textual notes

His eastern rulership is stated directly in the individual entry. The book connects his homage with his character being worn as a lamen. An editorial footnote jokes about the simultaneous forms as a confusion of persons; it does not establish additional named entities.
