# Purson

The catalogue describes Purson as its twentieth spirit, a great king concerned with treasure, knowledge of hidden things and events, and good familiars.

| Rank(s) | King |

| Legions | 22 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated; the legions include Virtues and Thrones |

## Appearance

He appears as a comely man with a lion’s face, carrying a fierce viper and riding a bear. Many sounding trumpets precede him. The entry also says that he can take either a human or an aerial body, without explaining the relationship between that distinction and his lion-faced appearance.

## Offices and powers

Purson knows hidden things, discovers treasure, and tells things past, present, and to come. He answers truthfully about earthly matters, secret and divine things, and the creation of the world. He brings forth good familiars and governs twenty-two legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The legions are said to belong partly to the Order of Virtues and partly to the Order of Thrones. That statement describes his followers, not an explicit former angelic order for Purson himself. The text calls his sign a mark, seal, or character and associates it with obedience.
