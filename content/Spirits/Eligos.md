# Eligos

The catalogue presents Eligos as its fifteenth spirit, a great duke who reveals concealed things and future events and brings the love of powerful people.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 15 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |

| Legions | 60 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears as a goodly knight carrying a lance, an ensign, and a serpent. The lance and ensign establish a military visual setting; the serpent is carried with them. The passage gives no named mount or accompanying troop for this appearance.

## Offices and powers

Eligos discovers hidden things and knows things to come. His knowledge includes wars and how soldiers will meet. He also causes the love of lords and other great persons. Sixty legions of spirits are assigned to his government.

## Relationships and textual notes

Although he appears as a knight, his formal catalogue rank is Duke. The entry attributes knowledge of warfare to him; it does not expressly say that he causes wars. Likewise, the love of lords is a stated influence, without a separate promise of a title or office. His seal accompanies the description as the spirit’s identifying character.
