# Gäap

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Gäap as a spirit who teaches sciences, changes understanding and affection, releases familiars, answers questions, and transports people.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 33 |
| Rank(s) | President, Prince |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Gaap, Gaäp |
| Legions | 66 |
| Former angelic order | Potentates |

## Appearance

Gäap appears in human form, leading four mighty kings. The catalogue associates his appearance with the sun occupying unspecified southern signs.

## Offices and powers

He can make people insensible or ignorant, but also instruct them in philosophy and the liberal sciences. He causes love or hatred, teaches the consecration of things belonging to [[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]]’s dominion, frees familiars from other magicians’ custody, gives truthful answers about past, present, and future, and rapidly carries people between kingdoms.

## Relationships and textual notes

[[Amaymon (Goetia)|Amaymon]] is his king; his former order is the Potentates. Gaap is the spelling in the vessel narrative and names table. The later note uses Gaäp and discusses the Pseudomonarchia’s account of [[Ham (Goetia)|Ham]]. It warns that an ambiguous passage about constraint could concern Gäap or [[Beleth]].
