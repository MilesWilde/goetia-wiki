# Sitri

The catalogue describes Sitri as its twelfth spirit, a great prince whose stated office is to provoke love or desire between men and women.

## Appearance

His initial form combines a leopard’s head with the wings of a gryphon. At the command of the master of the exorcism, he assumes a very beautiful human shape. The entry presents the winged animal-headed form and the human form in sequence, without further anatomical details.

## Offices and powers

Sitri inflames men with love for women and women with love for men. The text also attributes to him the power to cause them to show themselves naked when desired. These claims concern influence over other people; the account does not describe a separate teaching or divinatory office. He governs sixty legions of spirits.

## Relationships and textual notes

The description distinguishes his own change of appearance from the effects assigned to his office. His seal is associated with a worn lamen, as in other entries. The individual account provides no named superior, attendant spirit, or former angelic order.
