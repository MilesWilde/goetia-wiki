# Stolas

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Stolas as a spirit who teaches astronomy and the properties of herbs and precious stones.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 36 |
| Rank(s) | Prince |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Stolos |
| Legions | 26 |

## Appearance

Stolas first appears as a mighty raven and afterward takes a human form. These are the only bodily forms described in this edition’s catalogue entry. It gives no clothing, mount, weapon, or other carried object.

## Offices and powers

He teaches astronomy and the virtues of herbs and precious stones. Here virtues identifies the properties attributed to those materials by the text. The entry presents these subjects as knowledge he teaches; it does not separately describe a healing act, a treasure-finding service, or control over weather.

## Relationships and textual notes

The heading supplies Stolos as an alternative name, although the opening sentence repeats Stolas in both positions. The classified list places him among Princes and Prelates. The catalogue calls him a great and powerful Prince and assigns him twenty-six legions. Its account names no individual superior or former angelic order. The raven, human transformation, celestial learning, herbs, and stones are the concrete source elements available for reference, alongside his printed seal.
