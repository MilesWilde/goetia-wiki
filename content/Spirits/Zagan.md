# Zagan

According to the *Goëtia*, Zagan grants wit and wisdom, changes liquids, and converts metals into coin.

## Appearance

Zagan first appears as a bull with gryphon’s wings and later assumes human shape. The catalogue gives him the two ranks of King and President.

## Offices and powers

He makes people witty and can make fools wise. The main account says that he turns wine into water and blood into wine. It also credits him with changing metals into coin of the dominion to which the metal belongs.

## Relationships and textual notes

The edition’s later notes distinguish this account from the Pseudomonarchia. They report water becoming wine, blood becoming oil, and the reverse transformations in that earlier text, attributing the replacement of oil with wine to its English translator. These are editorial comparisons, rather than additional liquid transformations silently merged into the catalogue. The coin-making and wisdom claims occur in the main description itself.
