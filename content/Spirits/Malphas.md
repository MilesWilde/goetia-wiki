# Malphas

The *Goetia* catalogue describes Malphas as a spirit who builds houses and towers, reveals enemies’ thoughts and deeds, and gives familiars.

## Appearance

Malphas first appears as a crow, then takes human form. In that form he speaks with a hoarse voice. The catalogue gives no weapons, clothing, or mount; its principal images are the crow and the buildings associated with his office.

## Offices and powers

He builds houses and high towers. He also reveals enemies’ desires, thoughts, and completed actions, and supplies good familiars. His knowledge of opponents therefore covers their intentions as well as what they have already done.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry warns that Malphas willingly accepts a sacrifice but deceives the person who offers it. This warning qualifies the relationship described by the catalogue without erasing its listed promises. He is a mighty President commanding forty legions, and the text includes his individual seal. Despite the adjacent position and similar name, he is distinct from [[Halphas]], whose entry concerns armed towers and the dispatch of warriors. Malphas’s own account explicitly includes houses, enemy intelligence, and familiars.
