# Naberius

The catalogue describes Naberius as its twenty-fourth spirit, a valiant marquis associated with learning, rhetoric, and the restoration of social standing.

## Appearance

He appears as a black crane fluttering around the circle. His speech is hoarse. The circle supplies a ceremonial setting for his movement, while the crane is the spirit’s stated bodily form. The entry does not describe a human transformation or add other animal heads to this appearance.

## Offices and powers

Naberius makes people skilled in all arts and sciences, with particular emphasis on rhetoric. He also restores lost dignities and honours. The restoration concerns standing previously lost, rather than an express promise to create any new title. Nineteen legions of spirits are placed under his government.

## Relationships and textual notes

The account gives no alternative personal name, former angelic order, or named attendant. Its stress on rhetoric makes that subject a particular specialty within the broader teaching office. The seal appears with the catalogue’s recurring reference to a worn character. The bird form and hoarse voice are descriptive motifs rather than independently stated powers.
