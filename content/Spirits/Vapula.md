# Vapula

According to the *Goëtia*, Vapula teaches handicrafts, professions, philosophy, and other sciences.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 60 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |

| Legions | 36 |

## Appearance

Vapula appears as a lion with the wings of a gryphon. The entry presents this winged animal form directly and calls the spirit a great, mighty, and strong Duke.

## Offices and powers

His office is to make people knowledgeable in handicrafts and professions. The same sentence extends that instruction to philosophy and other sciences. The range therefore includes practical skilled work as well as learned subjects.

## Relationships and textual notes

Naphula is supplied as an alternative name in both the heading and the body of the numbered entry. The lion and gryphon wings identify the manifestation, while the stated benefit is instruction in crafts and learning. The description gives thirty-six legions and a seal, but its account of the office remains brief and does not subdivide the crafts or professions into named trades.
