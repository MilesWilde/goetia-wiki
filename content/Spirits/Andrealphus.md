# Andrealphus

According to the *Goëtia*, Andrealphus teaches geometry, measurement, and astronomy, and gives a person the likeness of a bird.

| Field | Catalogue record |

| Legions | 30 |

## Appearance

Andrealphus initially appears as a peacock accompanied by great noises. After a time he takes human shape. The entry calls him a mighty Marquis.

## Offices and powers

He teaches geometry and makes people highly capable in it. His instruction also extends to mensuration, meaning measurement, and astronomy. The catalogue separately says that he can transform a person into the likeness of a bird.

## Relationships and textual notes

The numbered description spells the name Andrealphus, while the classified list and the table accompanying the Hebrew names print Andrealphas. Those forms are kept as edition variants. His own peacock appearance and his ability to give a person a bird’s likeness are distinct claims in the entry: the latter belongs to his office, alongside the mathematical and astronomical teaching. He governs thirty legions of infernal spirits.
