# Buer

The catalogue describes Buer as its tenth spirit, a great president whose offices bring together philosophical instruction, knowledge of plants, healing, and familiars.

## Appearance

The text says that Buer appears in Sagittary and that this is his shape when the sun is there. The wording gives an astrological association without spelling out the anatomy of the form. This entry supplies no separate clothing, mount, or voice description.

## Offices and powers

Buer teaches moral and natural philosophy, logic, and the virtues of all herbs and plants. He heals human distempers and gives good familiars. Fifty legions of spirits are placed under his government. The plant knowledge and healing are both explicit offices, rather than inferred from his appearance.

## Relationships and textual notes

The entry calls his sign a character of obedience. A later editorial note reports an omitted handwritten remark about invocation timing found near [[Paimon]], Buer, and [[Gusion]]. Because its attachment was unclear, the editor supplies no definite attribution to Buer. That uncertain marginal note does not resolve the individual entry’s compressed wording about Sagittary.
