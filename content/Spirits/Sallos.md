# Sallos

The catalogue presents Sallos as its nineteenth spirit, a great and mighty duke whose stated office is to cause love between women and men.

| Field | Catalogue record |
| --- | --- |
| Number | 19 |
| Rank(s) | Duke |
| Aliases attested in this edition | Saleos |
| Legions | 30 |
| Former angelic order | Not stated |

## Appearance

He appears as a gallant soldier riding a crocodile and wearing a ducal crown. Despite the soldier’s form, his manner is peaceful. The crocodile is his mount, and the crown visibly corresponds to the ducal rank assigned in the description.

## Offices and powers

Sallos causes women to love men and men to love women. The entry expresses the office in both directions and places thirty legions of spirits under his government. It does not distinguish different tasks among those legions or assign a separate martial power to the soldier-like appearance.

## Relationships and textual notes

Saleos is supplied as an alternative name in the opening sentence. The peaceful appearance and military clothing coexist in the source rather than requiring one to be replaced. His seal accompanies the numbered account. The individual entry does not identify a former angelic order or name any attending or superior spirit.
