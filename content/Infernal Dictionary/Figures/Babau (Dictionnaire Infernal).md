# Babau

Babau is a southern French bogeyman used to frighten children. The dictionary describes him as an ogre or phantom who threatens to eat disobedient children, and compares him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Croquemitaine (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Croquemitaine]] and Pier-Jan Claes.
