# Wayland

Wayland is the extraordinary smith called Veland or Vanlund in the dictionary’s account. In one version he is the son of the giant [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Wade (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Wade]] and learns from Mimer and two dwarfs. Captured and hamstrung by a king, he takes a violent revenge and escapes with wings he has made.

The dictionary separately summarizes an Edda version in which Wayland is the third son of an elf king. He and his brothers marry valkyries who later disappear; his capture and revenge follow. The source expressly presents these as differing versions.
