# Elingsor

Elingsor is the magician of the Percival poem as named in this edition. He constructs an enchanted mountain palace containing a bed that evades anyone trying to climb onto it and shoots arrows at a person who succeeds.

The dictionary places the tale in the traditions of southern Italy and Sicily.
