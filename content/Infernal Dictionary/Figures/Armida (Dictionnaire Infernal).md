# Armida

Armida is the enchantress whom the dictionary connects with Tasso and traditions of the First Crusade. Raised in magic by her uncle Hidraote, she is sent to distract the crusading leaders.

The dictionary’s version ends with her death during the siege of Jerusalem; this should not be replaced by the ending of a different literary retelling.
