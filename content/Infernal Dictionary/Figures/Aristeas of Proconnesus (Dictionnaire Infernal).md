# Aristeas of Proconnesus

The dictionary gathers stories of Aristeas leaving his body and travelling in another form, with stag and raven variants. An apparent death is followed by disappearance of the body and later reappearances.

It reports very different intervals between appearances, including seven years and several centuries, and compares the figure with a revenant or demigod. The variants should remain visible rather than becoming one fixed chronology.
