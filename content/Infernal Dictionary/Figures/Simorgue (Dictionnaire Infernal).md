# Simorgue

Simorgue is a gigantic, rational, speaking bird living in the mountains of Qaf. The dictionary describes it as a fairy under a bird’s form, consuming the mountains’ produce. It connects Simorgue with traditions about Solomon and repeated cycles of the world and compares the bird with the Arab Anka and the rabbinic Jukhneh. Those comparisons belong to the dictionary’s account.
