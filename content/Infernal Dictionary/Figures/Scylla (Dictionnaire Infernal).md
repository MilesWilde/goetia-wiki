# Scylla

Scylla is a nymph loved by Glaucus. When he asks [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Circe (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Circe]] for a love potion, [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Circe (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Circe]] instead enchants the fountain where Scylla bathes. The transformed Scylla has twelve claws, six heads and mouths, and a pack of dogs emerging from her waist. She throws herself into the sea, where the dictionary places her at the Sicilian strait.
