# Charon

Charon is the underworld ferryman, a son of Erebus and Night. He carries the properly buried dead across the infernal rivers in return for payment.

The dictionary relates the golden bough required for a living visitor and Charon’s punishment for admitting Hercules without it. It also collects other identifications, which should not be treated as a single consistent biography.
