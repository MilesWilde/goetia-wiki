# Djilbeguenn

Djilbeguenn is a monstrous magician of Siberian heroic tradition. He sometimes has nine heads, rides an ox with thirty horns, and understands the speech of animals.

The dictionary recounts his killing of Comdai-Mirguenn and says that, after many violent deeds, he goes to hell and does not return.
