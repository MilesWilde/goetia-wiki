# Lepapa

Lepapa is the primordial rock which, with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Taroataihetomeo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Taroa-Taihetoomoo]], produces the subordinate Eatuas in the dictionary’s account of Tahiti.
