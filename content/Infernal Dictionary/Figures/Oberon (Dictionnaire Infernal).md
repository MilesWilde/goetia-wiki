# Oberon

Oberon is king of fairies and aerial phantoms and the husband of [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Titania (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Titania]]. The dictionary places their home in India and describes their nightly journeys across the seas to dance in moonlight. They avoid daylight, fleeing at sunrise or hiding in tree buds, and the entry names Wieland’s poem as a literary treatment.

The supplemental continuation of the Devil article names Oberon and [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Titania (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Titania]] as rulers over the sprites used by Shakespeare. The author then identifies fairy royalty with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]] in disguise; this is his polemical interpretation, not an instruction to merge the fairy figures into [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]]’s identity.
