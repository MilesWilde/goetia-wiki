# Rannou

Rannou receives extraordinary strength from part of a potion given to his mother by a rescued siren. His mother tests the drink on the family cat, which gains the intelligence intended for the child. Rannou grows strong enough to play with millstones but cannot control the consequences of his strength, causing destruction and eventually his own downfall. The dictionary retells the Breton story as a moral tale.
