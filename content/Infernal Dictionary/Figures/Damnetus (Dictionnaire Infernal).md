# Damnetus

Damnetus is an ancient werewolf said to have been transformed after eating human flesh at a sacrifice to [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]] Lycaeus. After ten years he regains human form.

The dictionary says that he later wins a wrestling contest at the Olympic Games.
