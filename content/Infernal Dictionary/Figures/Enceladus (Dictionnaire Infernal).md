# Enceladus

Enceladus is a hundred-armed giant who troubles [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]]. Minerva overcomes him and buries him beneath Sicily; the dictionary connects his unrest with Etna.
