# Urgande

Urgande is a benevolent fairy in chivalric romance. The dictionary names [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Melye (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Melye the Wicked]] as her enemy and associates Urgande with the adventures of Amadis and Esplandian.
