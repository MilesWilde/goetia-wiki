# Mimir

Under the name Mimer, the dictionary discusses the spring of wisdom for which [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]] sacrificed an eye. It reports a local tradition identifying a clear spring near a Baltic hill as that source, while questioning the claimed nearby burial place of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]].
