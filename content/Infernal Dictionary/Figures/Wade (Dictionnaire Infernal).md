# Wade

Wade is a giant born to King Wilkin and a sea-woman, or haffru. He is the father of the smith [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Wayland (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Wayland]] and arranges his training first with Mimer and then with two dwarfs who live inside Mount Kallova. Wade dies in a storm while his son is apprenticed to the dwarfs.
