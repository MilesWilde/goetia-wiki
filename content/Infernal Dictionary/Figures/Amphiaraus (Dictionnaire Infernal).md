# Amphiaraus

Amphiaraus is a prophetic hero who foresaw his death in the war against Thebes. The dictionary relates his return from the underworld and the establishment of a sanctuary associated with him.

At this sanctuary, dreams and a sacred spring were connected with healing and oracular responses.
