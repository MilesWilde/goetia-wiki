# Merlin

Merlin is a legendary enchanter with supernatural parentage in the dictionary's account. It places his birth on the Breton island of Sein and calls his mother a druidess, possibly a fairy, and his father either a demon or, in the cabalists' version, a sylph. He speaks with extraordinary wisdom while only sixteen days old and reveals two dragons beneath the foundations of King Wortigern's tower.

The narrative credits him with moving rocks, creating enchanted gifts, and composing prophecies. It preserves several endings: death in old age, removal by the devil, or continued invisible life in Brocéliande, where Gawain can hear but not see him. These are the dictionary's collected versions rather than a single reconciled biography.
