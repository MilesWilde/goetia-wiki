# Rugner

Rugner is a Scandinavian giant whose enormous spear is made of whetstone. [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Thor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Thor]] breaks it with his weapon in a duel, scattering fragments that the dictionary’s tale uses to explain the whetstones found throughout the world.
