# Titania

Titania is queen of the fairies and [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Oberon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Oberon]]’s wife. Her own entry refers to [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Oberon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Oberon]]’s account, where the pair travel at night, dance in moonlight, and avoid the first rays of the sun.

Titania is named with [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Oberon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Oberon]] in the supplemental continuation of the Devil article as a ruler of the sprites appearing in Shakespeare. The dictionary’s author places this reference within a hostile religious interpretation of fairies, which should remain attributed rather than replace Titania’s separate literary identity.
