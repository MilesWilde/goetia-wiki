# Surtur

In the account of Muspelheim, Surtur the Black rules a bright, burning realm inaccessible to outsiders and carries a flaming sword. The dictionary says that at the end of the world he will overcome the gods and commit the universe to fire.

His separate entry describes him leading spirits of fire through an opening in heaven, breaking Bifrost, and defeating Frey before burning the world. The dictionary attributes this account to the Celts, a classification retained here as the author’s wording rather than silently modernized.
