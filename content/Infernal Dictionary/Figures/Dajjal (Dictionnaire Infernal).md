# Dajjal

Dajjal is the name given to the [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Antichrist (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Antichrist]] in the dictionary’s account of Chaldean and Muslim traditions. It interprets the name as an exceptional liar and impostor.
