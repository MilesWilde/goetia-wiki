# Faithful Eckart

Faithful Eckart is the loyal guardian in a German legend about a cave of Freya. A mysterious musician draws travellers into the cave with irresistible music.

Eckart rescues young princes from the spirits there and devotes himself to guarding the entrance. The story says he remains at his post invisibly, turning away those who would be lured inside.
