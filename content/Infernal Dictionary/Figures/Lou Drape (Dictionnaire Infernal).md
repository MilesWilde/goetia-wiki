# Lou Drape

Lou Drape is the frightening horse of Aigues-Mortes. He gathers stray children onto his back, which lengthens to carry dozens or even a hundred of them, then takes them to an unknown destination.

The dictionary presents the story as a warning that keeps children near their families and encourages parents to watch them.
