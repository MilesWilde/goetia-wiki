# Acheron

The entry treats Acheron both as an underworld river and as a mythic figure punished by [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]] for giving drink to the Titans. It also points to medieval representations of Acheron as a terrifying monster.
