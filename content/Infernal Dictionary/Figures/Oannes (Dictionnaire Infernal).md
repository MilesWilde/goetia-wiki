# Oannes

Oannes or Oes is a being with both human and fish features who comes from the sea near Babylon in the account attributed to Berossus. He has a human head beneath a fish’s head, human feet attached near the tail, and a human voice. He teaches writing, sciences, arithmetic, agriculture, and other civilizing arts, stays among people without eating, and returns beneath the sea at sunset.
