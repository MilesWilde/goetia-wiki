# Polkan

Polkan is a Slavic centaur-like being credited with exceptional strength and speed. The Russian tales described here give him a human body above the waist and either a horse’s or a dog’s body below it. The dictionary’s suggested connection with the polka is an authorial etymological claim, not an established derivation.
