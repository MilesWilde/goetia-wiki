# Antichrist

The Antichrist is presented as an end-time deceiver who claims messianic authority, persecutes the faithful, and attracts followers through apparent miracles.

The dictionary also repeats a demonographic explanation of his wealth: hidden treasures guarded by demons would be made available to support his power.
