# Robert the Devil

Robert the Devil is presented as a legendary man said to be the son of a demon. After a violent life he repents and dies as a hermit; a Norman belief nevertheless makes his wandering ghost continue its penance until the Last Judgment. The dictionary notes competing relationships between Robert and Richard the Fearless.
