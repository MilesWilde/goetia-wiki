# Angrboda

Angrboda is a giantess associated with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Loki (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Loki]]. The dictionary makes her the mother of the wolf [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Fenris (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Fenris]], the serpent Jormungandr, and [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Hel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Hel]], ruler of the subterranean world.
