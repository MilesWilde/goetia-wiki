# Diti

The dictionary names Diti as the mother of malevolent spirits and of [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Erouniakcha (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Erouniakcha]], who throws the world into the sea before [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vishnu (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vishnu]] defeats him. Its [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Garuda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Garuda]] article also names Diti as the mother of that fabulous bird and says that she incubates his egg for five years. These parentage claims are the particular account collected by this edition.
