# Jormungandur

Jormungandur is a monstrous serpent of the Scandinavian underworld. The dictionary describes him as the offspring of the devil and the giantess [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Angrboda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Angerbode]].

The dictionary also describes Yormoungandour as a monstrous Scandinavian serpent large enough to encircle the earth (PDF 721), but it does not explicitly connect the two entries. Its [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Angrboda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Angerbode]] article names her husband as Lock (PDF 47), while this entry calls the serpent’s father “the devil” (PDF 390).
