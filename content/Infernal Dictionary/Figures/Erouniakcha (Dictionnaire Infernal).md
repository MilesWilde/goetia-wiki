# Erouniakcha

Erouniakcha is a son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Diti (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Diti]], whom the dictionary calls the mother of malevolent spirits. In its account of Hindu mythology, he throws the world into the sea. [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vishnu (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vishnu]] takes the form of a boar, kills him, and restores the world to its place.
