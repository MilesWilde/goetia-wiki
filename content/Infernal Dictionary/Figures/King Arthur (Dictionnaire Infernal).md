# King Arthur

The dictionary’s Arthur is a legendary king who sleeps at Avalon and may return. It also reports traditions connecting him with nocturnal hunting and a supernatural retinue in Brittany.

These stories are presented alongside the romances of the Round Table, rather than as an ordinary historical biography.
