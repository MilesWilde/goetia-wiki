# Samorindo

Samorindo, also called Samoundo, is named as the wife of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Erlik-Khan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Erlik-Khan]] in the dictionary's description of that underworld ruler. The passage does not give her a separate office or narrative.
