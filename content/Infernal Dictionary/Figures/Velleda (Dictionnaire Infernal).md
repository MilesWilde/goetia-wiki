# Velleda

Velleda is a Germanic druidess whom the dictionary describes as half fairy and half prophetess. From her tower she exerts authority that the author compares with that of kings, and warriors seek her approval and dedicate spoils to her. The account cites Tacitus while adding its own supernatural characterization.
