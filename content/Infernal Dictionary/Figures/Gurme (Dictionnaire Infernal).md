# Gurme

Gurme is the terrifying dog that the dictionary calls a Cerberus of the Celtic underworld. He remains chained at a cavern's entrance until the last day, when he will attack and kill the deity named Tyr or [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Thor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Thor]]. The author explicitly identifies him with the wolf [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Fenris (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Fenris]].
