# Garuda

Garuda is a fabulous bird and [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vishnu (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vishnu]]'s mount. The dictionary describes a handsome young man's head, a white collar, and an eagle's body. Its particular birth story names [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Diti (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Diti]] as his mother and says that she incubated his egg for five years.
