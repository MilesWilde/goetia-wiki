# Prometheus

The dictionary retells the punishment of Prometheus at the Caucasus, where a bird devours his entrails. In its version, Jupiter releases him while preserving the terms of an oath by requiring him to wear a ring containing a fragment of the rock. The passage presents this as an explanation of enchanted rings attributed to Pliny.
