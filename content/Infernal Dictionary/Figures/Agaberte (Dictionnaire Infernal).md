# Agaberte

In a story attributed to Torquemada, Agaberte is the daughter of the northern giant [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Vagnoste (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vagnoste]]. She is credited with changing her appearance and with astonishing powers over the sky and landscape.
