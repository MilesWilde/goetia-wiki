# Pra-Ariaseria

Pra-Ariaseria is a colossal figure placed in Siam during the time of Sommona-Codom. The dictionary reports enormous bodily measurements but immediately remarks that their relationship is difficult to understand and that the figure’s actual shape is unknown.
