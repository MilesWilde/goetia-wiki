# Fenris

Fenris is the wolf born of Loke and the giantess [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Angrboda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Angerbode]]. The dictionary describes him as powerful enough to shake the earth and bound until the end of the world, when he will break free and devour [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]].
