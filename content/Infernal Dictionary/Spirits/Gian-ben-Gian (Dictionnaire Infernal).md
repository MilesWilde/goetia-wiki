# Gian-ben-Gian

Gian-ben-Gian is the most famous king of the genies in the dictionary's account of beings who inhabited the earth before Adam. The author derives the name of the ginnes or genies from his name. The larger account places the genies in Ginnistan after their removal from the human world.
