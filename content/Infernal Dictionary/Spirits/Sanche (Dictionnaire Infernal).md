# Sanche

Sanche is a deceased servant who appears to his former master Pierre d’Engelbert. He says that he and other spirits must revisit Castile to expiate their wartime crimes, especially his theft of church ornaments, and asks for prayers and alms. His statement that the household still owes him eight sous is later confirmed by his master’s wife in the story.
