# Egeria

Egeria is the nymph who assists Numa Pompilius. The dictionary gathers later classifications of her as a succubus or an elemental spirit.

Some cabalists call her an undine, others a salamander and daughter of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vesta (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vesta]]. These alternatives remain distinct interpretations.

Egeria is the nymph from whom Numa claims to receive the laws he gives Rome. The dictionary also reports demonographers’ interpretation of her as a familiar demon whom Numa had summoned, preserving both presentations rather than resolving them.
