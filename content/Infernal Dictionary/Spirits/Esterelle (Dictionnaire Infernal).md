# Esterelle

Esterelle is a fairy said to have lived beside a spring in Provence. Citing a legend of Saint Armentaire written in 1300, the dictionary says that people brought offerings to her and that she gave enchanted drinks to women. It places the monastery of Notre-Dame de l'Esterel on her former dwelling place.
