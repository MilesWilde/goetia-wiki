# Sakhar

Sakhar is the infernal spirit who temporarily usurps Solomon’s throne in the story attributed here to the Talmud. Taking Solomon’s appearance, he obtains the king’s ring from Amina and rules while Solomon wanders unrecognized. After forty days he flees and throws the ring into the sea; a fish swallows it and returns it to Solomon’s possession through the king’s meal.
