# Guecuba

Guecuba is the evil spirit attributed to the Araucanians by the dictionary. The short entry directs readers to [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Toqui (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Toqui]] for the associated account.

Guecuba is the demonic enemy of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Toqui (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Toqui]] in the dictionary’s brief account of Araucanian belief. The notice supplies no appearance or separate actions.
