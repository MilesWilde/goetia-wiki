# Acatriel

Acatriel is named as one of three princes of benevolent spirits in the dictionary’s account of Jewish cabala. The entry says that this system distinguishes spirits of two natures.

Acatriel is one of three superior intelligences in the dictionary's account of cabala, with [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Metatron (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Metatron]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Sandalphon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Sandalphon]]. The [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ensoph (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ensoph]] article uses the variant Acatries and associates the three with the leadership of good spirits.
