# Hellequin

Hellequin is the leader of a wandering company of dead hunters in the dictionary's account. One tale makes him a former warrior in Charles Martel's wars who, with his sons, turns to banditry. His guardian angel obtains a limited punishment instead of eternal damnation: the family must wander and hunt until Judgment Day, sometimes pursued by a pack from hell.
