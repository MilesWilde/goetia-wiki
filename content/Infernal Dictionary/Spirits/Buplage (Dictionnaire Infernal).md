# Buplage

Buplage is a soldier whose corpse rises after battle to rebuke a Roman who is stripping it. He prophesies future disasters, climbs a tree, and predicts that a wolf will devour him.

After the body is eaten, the severed head continues to speak and forbids burial. The dictionary explicitly finds the story difficult to believe.
