# Georges Raulin

Georges Raulin is the name claimed by a malodorous, peasant-shaped apparition troubling the priest Bayer in 1726. It eats meat and bones, drinks heavily, and returns to disturb the household.

The dictionary explicitly says that the claimed name and origin were false. The profile therefore preserves an apparition’s assumed identity rather than identifying a historical person.
