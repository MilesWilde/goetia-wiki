# Jezer-Tob

Jezer-Tob is the chief of the spirits of light in the dictionary's account of cabala. He stands opposite [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Jezer-Hara (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jezer-Hara]], who leads the spirits of darkness.
