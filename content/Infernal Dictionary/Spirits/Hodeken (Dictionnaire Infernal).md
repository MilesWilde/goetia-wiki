# Hodeken

Hodeken, or Hecdekin, is the 'spirit with the cap' said to have stayed in the bishop's palace at Hildesheim in 1130. He advises on diplomatic affairs, carries water, and helps in the kitchen. After a kitchen boy insults him and the steward ignores his complaint, he takes violent revenge and leaves the household permanently.
