# Puck

The dictionary tells of Puck as a mischievous spirit serving the Dominicans at Schwerin. In monkey form he turns the spit, draws wine, and sweeps the kitchen, while playing tricks on visitors. His wages are two pewter pots and a colourful coat with bells.

The entry compares this servant spirit with [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Rusch (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Rusch]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Bronzet (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bronzet]], Robin Goodfellow, [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Red-cap (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Red-cap]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Hodeken (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Hodeken]], and Nissegodreng, and describes helpful but easily offended household spirits. These are the dictionary’s proposed connections; they do not make every regional story an interchangeable biography.

The supplemental Devil article refers readers to Puck after discussing popular sprites and the fairy royalty of Shakespeare. This is a cross-reference rather than a description of Puck’s appearance or individual powers.
