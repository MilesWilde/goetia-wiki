# Adamas

Adamas is the perfect man produced by [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Autogenes (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Autogenes]] in the genealogy reported under [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Barbelo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbelo]]. His consort is perfect knowledge.
