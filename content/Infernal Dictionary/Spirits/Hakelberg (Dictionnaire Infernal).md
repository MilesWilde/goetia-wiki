# Hakelberg

Hakelberg is a spectral hunter who surrendered his share of paradise in exchange for hunting until Judgment Day. The dictionary identifies him with a lord of Rodenstein whose grave is guarded by hellhounds; at midnight he emerges to hunt with a terrifying retinue. His appearance is taken as an omen of war. The entry also connects the hunter's name and tradition with Woden or [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]].
