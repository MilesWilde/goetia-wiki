# Asweith

Asweith is the dead companion in a Danish story about friends who swear never to abandon each other. Asmund shuts himself in Asweith’s tomb, but the corpse rises and attacks him.

The dictionary explains the revenant as either a body animated by a demon or a vampire. Asmund eventually decapitates it; the story ends with the bodies burned.
