# Rusch

Rusch, called Rush in his short alphabetical entry, is a Swedish household spirit compared with [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Puck (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Puck]]. The dictionary describes him as the monk Rusch of Swedish legend but gives no separate account of his actions in the brief cross-reference.
