# Samuel’s Apparition

The apparition summoned at Endor foretells Saul’s disasters. The dictionary lays out competing explanations: Samuel’s own soul, a demon appearing in the prophet’s form, or a demon borrowing his body. It cites arguments for each reading rather than treating the apparition’s identity as settled.
