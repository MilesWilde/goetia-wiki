# Terragon

Terragon is an alleged familiar spirit of Henry III in a pamphlet dated 1589. The king supposedly addressed the spirit as his brother, and the account describes a ring containing the king’s soul. The dictionary reports the pamphlet’s accusation rather than independently establishing the relationship.
