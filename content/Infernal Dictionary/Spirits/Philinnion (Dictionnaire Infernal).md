# Philinnion

In the story attributed to Phlegon, Philinnion returns after her death to visit Machates, a guest in her parents’ house. They exchange tokens and meet secretly until her parents discover them. She tells them that their intervention has ended the brief life granted her by the gods, then falls dead again. Her tomb is found empty except for the gifts Machates gave her.
