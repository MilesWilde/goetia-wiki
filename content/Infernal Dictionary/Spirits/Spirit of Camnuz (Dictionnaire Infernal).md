# Spirit of Camnuz

The spirit of Camnuz, near Bingen, is a named local haunting said to make noises and throw stones while remaining invisible. It steals objects, plants them to accuse others, and sets houses and crops on fire.

The story ends when exorcists sent by the bishop of Mainz expel it.
