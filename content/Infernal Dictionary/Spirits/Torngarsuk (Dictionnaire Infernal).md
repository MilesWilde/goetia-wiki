# Torngarsuk

Torngarsuk is a powerful spirit in the dictionary’s Greenland account. Fishers invoke him, and the anguekkok consults him about illness. He may appear as a bear, a one-armed man, or a human only a finger high, and is ordinarily invisible except to the ritual specialist. The entry describes him as benevolent.
