# Hermeline

Hermeline, also called Hermione or Hermelinde, is the invisible familiar said to have accompanied Benedetto Berna for forty years. In the account attributed to Pico della Mirandola, Berna eats, drinks, and talks with this companion. The dictionary also records an alternative explanation that Berna was mistaken about the experience, rather than treating the reported familiar as independently established.
