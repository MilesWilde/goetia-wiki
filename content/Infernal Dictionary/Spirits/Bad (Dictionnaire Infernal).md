# Bad

Bad is a Persian spirit governing winds and storms. The dictionary associates him with the twenty-second day of the lunar month.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=84|PDF 84]].
