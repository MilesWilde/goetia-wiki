# Polycrite

Polycrite returns as a black-clothed spectre after his death, attempting to prevent the execution of his wife and intersex child. When the crowd refuses to relent, he kills and consumes the child, leaving a head that foretells disaster. The dictionary calls the figure in this tale a vampire or ogre.
