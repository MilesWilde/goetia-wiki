# Melye

Melye is a wicked fairy and the adversary of the benevolent [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Urgande (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Urgande]] in the dictionary's account of chivalric romance. Her short headword appears in a discussion of fairies of unequal strength and directs readers to the longer [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Urgande (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Urgande]] episode.

In that episode, Melye supports the Saracens from a rocky height, evades Esplandian's enchanted sword, and retreats into a cave. She later welcomes [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Urgande (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Urgande]] into her grotto and attacks her. Enchantment overcomes the accompanying knights, while Esplandian's sword protects him and lets him rescue [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Urgande (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Urgande]].
