# Murzanti

An alleged possessing spirit in Poncini claimed to be the soul of Murzanti, a man murdered during gambling. It demanded prayers and masses for the dead man, promising to leave the young woman it occupied. The dictionary reports that she recovered after these rites; it presents the identity as the spirit’s claim.
