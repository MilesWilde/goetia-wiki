# Haridi

Haridi is the spirit said to inhabit a serpent honored at Akhmim in Egypt. The story begins with a dead dervish whose soul is claimed to have entered the animal. The serpent acquires a reputation for healing and immortality, including surviving dismemberment. The dictionary rejects the miracles as manipulation and substitution by its keepers, while recording the animal's title of 'angel.'
