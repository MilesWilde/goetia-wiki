# Harppe

Harppe is a revenant whom the dictionary explicitly calls a vampire. After requesting burial upright beside his kitchen, he returns as a murderous apparition. Olaus Pa wounds him with a spear, which is later found in the corpse; burning the body and scattering its ashes ends the appearances. The author regards the surviving story as poorly established and probably altered.
