# Kantius the Silesian

Kantius is a named vampire in a story relayed to Henry More by a Silesian physician. Rumors that the dead alderman walked abroad lead to the exhumation of an unusually heavy corpse. The horse whose kick killed him must pull it from the grave, and the body is finally burned in pieces. The dictionary distinguishes the alleged appearances from the widespread fear they caused.
