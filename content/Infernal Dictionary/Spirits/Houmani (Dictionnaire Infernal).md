# Houmani

Houmani is a female spirit who rules the region of the stars in the account the dictionary labels 'Oriental.' The entry points to Schadaschivaoun for the associated tradition.

Houmani is the principal wife associated with the Schada-Schivaoun, spirits said to govern the world in the dictionary’s account of Indian traditions. She governs heaven and the region of the stars. The author interprets these wives as personified attributes rather than giving them independent narratives.
