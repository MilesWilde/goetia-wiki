# Kleudde

Kleudde is a malicious Flemish sprite associated with fog, deserted fields, moss-covered ruins, and underground caverns. He shuns daylight and Christian holy places, frightens travelers with a harsh cry, and cannot be grasped: an attempted touch leaves a dark, painful mark like a burn. The dictionary describes him through comparisons with the basilisk, vampire, and griffin.
