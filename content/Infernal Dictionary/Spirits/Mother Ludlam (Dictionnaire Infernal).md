# Mother Ludlam

Mother Ludlam is a benevolent figure variously called a witch, fairy, or magician, associated with a cave near Farnham in Surrey. People who ask properly can borrow tools and cooking vessels from her. When one borrower keeps a great cauldron beyond the promised time, she refuses its return and stops granting such requests.
