# Eros

Eros appears in the dictionary's story of Iamblichus summoning two spirits from a spring at Gadara. Called Love in the narrative and Eros in its footnote, he has golden hair braided and falling over his shoulders, shining like sunlight. His companion is [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Anteros (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Anteros]], or Counter-Love.
