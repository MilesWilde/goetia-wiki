# Barbelo

Barbelo is a virginal spirit in the dictionary’s account of a Gnostic genealogy. She receives foreknowledge, incorruptibility, and eternal life, and brings forth light that is subsequently called Christ.

The entry continues through a chain of spiritual generations involving [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Autogenes (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Autogenes]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Adamas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Adamas]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Prunic (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Prunic]], and [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Protarchon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Protarchon]]. Its hostile commentary belongs to the nineteenth-century author and is not necessary to the genealogy.
