# Hera (Westphalian fairy)

Hera is a benevolent fairy of Westphalia in the dictionary. She travels through the air between Christmas and Epiphany, spreading abundance and happiness over the earth.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=344|PDF 344]].
