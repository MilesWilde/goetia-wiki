# Robin Goodfellow

The dictionary’s supplemental Devil article includes the Robin Goodfellows in Heywood’s catalogue of popular spirits, beside elves and other household or wandering sprites. The plural wording is retained as evidence of a traditional name or group, rather than used to invent a distinct biography.
