# Sougai-Toyon

Sougai-Toyon is the thunder spirit in the dictionary’s account of Yakut belief. He is described as harmful and as the minister of [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Oulon-Toyon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Oulon-Toyon]]’s vengeance.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=640|PDF 640]].
