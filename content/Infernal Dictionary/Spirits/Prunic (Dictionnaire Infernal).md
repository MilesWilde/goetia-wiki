# Prunic

Prunic is the wisdom or Holy Spirit in the genealogy reported under [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Barbelo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbelo]]. The dictionary says Prunic brings forth [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Protarchon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Protarchon]], the first prince.
