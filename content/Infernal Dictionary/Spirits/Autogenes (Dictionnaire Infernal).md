# Autogenes

Autogenes is a spiritual being in the Gnostic genealogy reported under [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Barbelo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbelo]]. He is generated by reason and intelligence, and in turn produces [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Adamas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Adamas]] and his consort, perfect knowledge.
