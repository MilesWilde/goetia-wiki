# Red-cap

Red-cap is a Scottish household or mischievous spirit. His short entry refers to [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Puck (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Puck]], whose longer discussion compares Red-cap with related northern serving spirits; the dictionary does not provide a separate violent or blood-dyeing story for him.
