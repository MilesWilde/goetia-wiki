# Desfontaines

Desfontaines is the dead friend in a story told by the priest Bezuél. After unusual faintness, Bezuél sees his friend, who reports drowning near Caen and describes the circumstances of his death.

The apparition cannot be embraced like a living body, although it seems to grip Bezuél’s arm. It asks for prayers and messages to the family. The dictionary preserves both the reported appearances and alternative explanations involving premonition or sympathy.
