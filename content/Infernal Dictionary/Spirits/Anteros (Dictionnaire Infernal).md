# Anteros

Anteros is the spirit called Counter-Love in the dictionary's account of Iamblichus summoning two beings from a spring at Gadara. The text distinguishes him from the more brilliant golden-haired [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Eros (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Eros]], without giving him a separate set of powers.
