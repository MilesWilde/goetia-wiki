# Cayol’s Ghost

Cayol is a deceased landowner whose ghost appears to his son after a tenant is ordered to pay the same rent twice. The apparition says the payment was already received and identifies the hidden receipt.

The son finds the document behind a mirror, acknowledges his error, and reimburses the tenant’s legal costs.
