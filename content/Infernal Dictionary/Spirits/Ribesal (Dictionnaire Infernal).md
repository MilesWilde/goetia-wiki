# Ribesal

Ribesal is a mountain spirit whose home the dictionary places on the summit of the Risemberg in Silesia. He suddenly covers the mountain in cloud and raises storms. The entry explicitly identifies him with Rubezal.

The explicit Rubezal entry calls him prince of the gnomes in the Sudeten Mountains and describes his pranks upon mountain-dwellers. It also notes the literary treatment of his exploits by Musaeus.
