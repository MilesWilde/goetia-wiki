# Bensozia

Bensozia leads nocturnal companies of women and fairy or demonic companions riding winged animals through the air. Participants owe her complete obedience.

The dictionary associates her with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Diana (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Diana]], Nocticula, Herodias, and the moon, citing medieval ecclesiastical accounts. These are reported identifications of the night-riding leader rather than proof that every tradition is identical.
