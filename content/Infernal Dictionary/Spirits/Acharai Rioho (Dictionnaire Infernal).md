# Acharai Rioho

Acharai Rioho, called the powerful, is the chief of eight tribes of malevolent spirits inhabiting the Yakut underworld Mang-Taar. The dictionary says that entirely white cattle are sacred to him and that deceased shamans are believed to join these spirits.
