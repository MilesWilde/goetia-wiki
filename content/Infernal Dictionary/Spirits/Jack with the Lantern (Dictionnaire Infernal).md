# Jack with the Lantern

Jack with the Lantern is the English will-o'-the-wisp, classed here among lower fire demons and also called the monk of the marshes. The dictionary tells of travelers led toward danger by his light and of his anger when mocked. It also places these stories beside physical explanations involving luminous marsh phenomena, rather than treating every reported light as an independently verified spirit.
