# Cienga

Cienga is a name given to an evil spirit in the dictionary’s broad account of Oceanian peoples. No individual appearance or story is supplied.

At the end of its account of the creator [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Motogon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Motogon]], the dictionary gives Cienga as the name of a harmful spirit or devil in the same Australian setting. It supplies no further individual description.
