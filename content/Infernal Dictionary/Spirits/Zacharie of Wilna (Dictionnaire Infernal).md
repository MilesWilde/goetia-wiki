# Zacharie of Wilna

Zacharie is the supposed dead bridegroom in the story of Bietka at Wilna. After his reported death, he returns, marries her, and displays knowledge of present and future events.

An Italian magician later claims that the supposed revenant is an escaped demon and confines it in a ring. The dictionary also reports a sceptical interpretation in which the death and subsequent marvels were a deception.
