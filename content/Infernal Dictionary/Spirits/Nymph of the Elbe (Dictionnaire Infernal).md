# Nymph of the Elbe

The Nymph of the Elbe appears on the riverbank combing her hair like a siren, in an account attributed to Praetorius. The dictionary connects her with German legends collected by the Grimm brothers and interprets such water spirits as beautiful but dangerous beings whose supernatural origin ultimately becomes apparent.
