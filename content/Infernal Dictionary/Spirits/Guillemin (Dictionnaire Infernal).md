# Guillemin

Guillemin is Michel Verdung's familiar spirit. With his help, Verdung is said to have been able to run as fast as he wished.

Guillemin is the name of a spirit claimed by Michel Verdung in the witch-trial account summarized under Verdung. The dictionary supplies no independent appearance or powers for this spirit.
