# Lanthila

Lanthila is the superior being who commands the Netos, the malevolent spirits of the Moluccas in the dictionary's account.

Lanthila is named as the chief of the Netos, harmful spirits attributed by the dictionary to the Moluccas. No individual appearance or additional role is supplied.
