# Tomtegobbe

Tomtegobbe, the Old Man of the Granary, is a Swedish household goblin. The dictionary’s discussion of [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Puck (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Puck]] places him among spirits who can help a household when treated gently and become troublesome when offended.
