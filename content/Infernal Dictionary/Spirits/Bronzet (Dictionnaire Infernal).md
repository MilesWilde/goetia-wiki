# Bronzet

Bronzet is a goblin associated with the abbey of Montmajour near Arles. The dictionary directs the reader to [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Puck (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Puck]] for comparison.

Bronzet is the spirit associated with the abbey of Montmajor near Arles in the [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Puck (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Puck]] entry. The dictionary treats him as another regional instance of the mischievous serving-spirit tradition but gives him no distinct appearance here.
