# Jezer-Hara

Jezer-Hara is the leader of the spirits of darkness or demons in the dictionary's account of cabala. The paired entry contrasts him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Jezer-Tob (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jezer-Tob]], chief of the spirits of light.
