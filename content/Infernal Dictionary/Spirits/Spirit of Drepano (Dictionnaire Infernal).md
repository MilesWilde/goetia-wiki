# Spirit of Drepano

The spirit of Drepano makes loud noises, throws harmless stones, and tosses household utensils without breaking them. It sings while remaining unseen.

It also announces the master’s return before anyone can see him and urges the family to light a fire when he comes home soaked by rain. The dictionary describes the household’s religious conduct as preventing the haunting from succeeding.
