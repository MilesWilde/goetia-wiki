# Peter Plogojowits

Plogojowits is a deceased villager described as a vampire in the dictionary’s account of Kisolova. Several people claimed that he attacked them at night, and his widow said he returned asking for his shoes. Villagers attributed a series of deaths to him, opened his grave, and interpreted the corpse’s condition as evidence of vampirism. The account ends with the body being staked and burned.
