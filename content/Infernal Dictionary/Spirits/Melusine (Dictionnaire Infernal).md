# Melusine

Melusine is the fairy who marries the head of the Lusignan family on condition that he never enter her room on Saturday. On that day she must take her natural form, half woman and half serpent. When he spies through the door and sees her, she departs. The dictionary also reports a Poitou belief that her cries around the castle announce the death of one of her descendants.
