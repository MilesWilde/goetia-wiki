# Monsieur de Laforet

Monsieur de Laforet is an earlier name for the Great Huntsman of Fontainebleau. The dictionary also connects him with a German child's werewolf testimony: the figure gives the child a wolf skin, offers wealth, and asks for allegiance, but can be driven away with signs of the cross.
