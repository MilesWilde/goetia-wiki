# Agrafena-Shiganskaia

Agrafena-Shiganskaia is a deceased magician whose spirit is blamed for episodes of involuntary imitation and distress in northeastern Siberia. The dictionary reports belief that she possesses those affected.

The author also considers bodily or dietary explanations for the condition, so the entry does not offer possession as its only explanation.
