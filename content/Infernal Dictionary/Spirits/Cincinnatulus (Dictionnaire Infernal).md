# Cincinnatulus

Cincinnatulus, the little curly-haired one, is the spirit said to speak through a woman named Jocaba in a report attributed to Rhodiginus. The dictionary identifies her as a ventriloquist, preserving a natural explanation alongside the claimed spirit.
