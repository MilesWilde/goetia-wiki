# Nickar

The dictionary identifies Nickar or Hnickar with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]] acting as a destructive spirit. In a water-horse form compared with the Scottish kelpie, he inhabits Scandinavian lakes and rivers, raises storms, and overturns fishermen’s boats. The entry traces related water-spirit beliefs and the English name Old Nick to this figure; those connections are the author’s interpretation.
