# Millo

Millo is the named revenant in a Hungarian vampire story about Stanoska. She reports that he has tried to strangle her, nine weeks after his burial or death. The villagers later exhume and destroy his remains.

The dictionary retells the episode with different intervals: the Millo entry says Stanoska dies nine days after the reported encounter (PDF 473), while the Stanoska account says three days (PDF 645). The continuation on PDF 646 offers a frightened imagination as an alternative explanation. These are variant reports within the book, not a single consistent chronology.
