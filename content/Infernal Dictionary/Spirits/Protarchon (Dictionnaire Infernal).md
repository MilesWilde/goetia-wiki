# Protarchon

Protarchon is the first prince brought forth by [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Prunic (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Prunic]] in the genealogy reported under [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Barbelo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbelo]]. With Arrogance, he produces the vices and their branches.
