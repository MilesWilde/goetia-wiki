# Morgan le Fay

Morgane is Arthur's sister and [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Merlin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Merlin]]'s pupil, famous in romances for enchantments and tricks played on Guinevere. The dictionary also describes her as a great Breton fairy, the most powerful of nine druidess sisters from the island of Sein, and the Singer of the Seas.

It notes an alternative explanation of Morgane as a mirage, and separately mentions [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Morgiane (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Morgiane]] or Mergiann as an 'Oriental' peri. These neighboring forms are reported without forcing them into a single biography.
