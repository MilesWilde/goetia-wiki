# Raum

Raum is a great infernal count who appears as a raven when conjured. He destroys cities and grants dignities. The entry places him in the former order of Thrones and gives him command of thirty legions.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=583|PDF 583]].
