# Beelzebub

Beelzebub is a leading infernal power whose name is connected with flies. The dictionary gathers accounts of his worship and a variety of appearances.

His rank changes between the authorities cited: some place him beneath [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]], while others make him sovereign of the infernal monarchy. The source preserves this disagreement.
