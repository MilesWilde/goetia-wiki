# Behemoth

Behemoth is associated with gluttony and serves as an infernal cupbearer or wine steward. The dictionary gives several animal forms rather than one fixed appearance.

It also gathers divergent readings of the creature in Job and a tradition of an immense ox feeding on a thousand mountains and reserved for a future banquet. These literary and theological interpretations should not be collapsed into a single zoological claim.
