# Furfur

Furfur is an infernal count who appears as a stag with a flaming tail. He lies unless confined within a triangle, and may assume an angel's form while speaking in a rough voice. He maintains harmony between husbands and wives, produces lightning and thunder where commanded, and answers questions about abstract matters. The dictionary gives him twenty-six legions.
