# Cat (Maillat demon)

Cat is one of five named demons said to have possessed Louise Maillat in 1598. The dictionary lists the five as Wolf, Cat, Dog, Joly, and Griffin, and describes their departure during the alleged deliverance. The names belong to this particular possession account, not to the ordinary animals or to every creature bearing the same name.
