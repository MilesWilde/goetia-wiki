# Satan

The dictionary reports several incompatible rankings for Satan: a demon of the first order, of the third according to Reginald Scot, the commonly recognized chief of hell, or a disruptive prince in [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]]’s empire. It explains his name as enemy or adversary and recounts his leadership of the angelic rebellion and fall.

Its literary and philosophical discussion also cites Milton’s gigantic Satan and presents other writers’ interpretations of his place in creation. These accounts are separate traditions assembled by the dictionary, rather than a single consistent infernal hierarchy.
