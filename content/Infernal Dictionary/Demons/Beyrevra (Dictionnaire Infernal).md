# Beyrevra

Beyrevra leads souls wandering through the air as aerial demons. He has large hooked nails.

When [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]] insults a superior divinity, Beyrevra is sent to punish him and removes one of his five heads with a nail. Eswara then consoles [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]] that he will remain honoured with four heads.
