# Focalor

Focalor is an infernal general who appears as a man with griffin wings. He controls the winds and sea, drowns people, and overturns warships, although he reluctantly obeys an exorcist. He commands thirty legions. The entry adds that he hopes to return to heaven after a thousand years, a hope the author rejects.
