# Diambiliche

Diambiliche is the name for the devil in Madagascar according to the dictionary. The brief entry says that he is revered more than the gods and receives the first fruits of all sacrifices from the priests. These claims are the historical author’s account, not a general description of present-day Malagasy belief.
