# Anarazel

Anarazel guards underground treasure. With [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Gaziel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Gaziel]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Fecor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Fecor]], he moves hidden riches beyond the reach of searchers.

The entry associates these spirits with shaking foundations, storms, bells ringing at midnight, apparitions, and nocturnal terror.
