# Bedargon

Bedargon is one of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Samael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Samael]]’s lieutenants in the dictionary’s account of Jewish cabala.

Bedargon is named beside [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Asmodeus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Asmodeus]] as a lieutenant of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Samael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Samael]] or [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]] in the dictionary's account of the evil spirits of cabala.
