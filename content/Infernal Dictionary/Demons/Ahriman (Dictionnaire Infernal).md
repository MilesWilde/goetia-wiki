# Ahriman

Ahriman is the source of evil and prince of the infernal realm in the dictionary’s account of ancient Persian religion. Born in darkness, he opposes Oromaze or [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ormuzd (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ormuzd]], the principle of good.

The account contrasts his created and ultimately limited existence with the enduring good principle.
