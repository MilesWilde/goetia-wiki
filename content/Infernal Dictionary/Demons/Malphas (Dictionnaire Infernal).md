# Malphas

Malphas is a great infernal president who appears as a raven or as a human with a hoarse voice. He builds impregnable towers and citadels, destroys enemy fortifications, finds skilled workers, and gives familiar spirits. He receives sacrifices but deceives those offering them, and commands forty legions.
