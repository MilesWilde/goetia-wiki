# Abrahel

Abrahel is a succubus in a story attributed to Nicolas Remy and dated 1581 at Dalhem. She approaches the shepherd Pierron in the appearance of a woman he desires and demands obedience.

After Pierron’s son dies through her intervention, Abrahel offers an apparent restoration in exchange for worship. A year later the animating demon leaves and the child’s body collapses. The dictionary itself calls aspects of the story incomplete or poorly connected.
