# Baltazo

Baltazo is a demon name used in the account of Nicole Aubry’s possession at Laon. The episode summarized here concerns an impostor claiming the demonic identity, appearing at supper, and failing to perform the promised deliverance.

The dictionary therefore supplies a claimed role in a possession story, not a reliable independent portrait of a spirit.
