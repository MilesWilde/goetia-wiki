# Baalpharas

Baalpharas is one of the Baal figures listed in the [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Baalberith (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Baalberith]] entry. The dictionary characterizes him as harmful, but gives no further appearance or narrative.
