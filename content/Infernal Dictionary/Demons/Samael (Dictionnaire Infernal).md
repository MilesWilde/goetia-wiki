# Samael

The dictionary attributes to rabbinic authorities several accounts of Samael: prince of demons, the rider of the serpent who tempted Eve, and the angel of death represented with a sword or with bow and arrows. It says that some identify him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Asmodeus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Asmodeus]]; this is an attributed comparison, not an unqualified identity.

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Samael as the angel presiding over hour 7. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.
