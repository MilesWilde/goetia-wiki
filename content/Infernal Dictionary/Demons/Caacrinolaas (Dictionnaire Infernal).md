# Caacrinolaas

Caacrinolaas is a grand president who appears as a dog with griffin wings. He teaches the liberal arts, foretells the future, inspires homicide, and grants invisibility. Thirty-six legions obey him.

The dictionary gives a different rank in the Grand Grimoire, where Classyalabolus is a subordinate who can serve as a mount for [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Cerberus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Nebiros]] or [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Cerberus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Naberus]].
