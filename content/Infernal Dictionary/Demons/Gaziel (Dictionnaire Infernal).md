# Gaziel

Gaziel is a companion of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Anarazel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Anarazel]] associated with underground treasure. He moves or conceals it, shakes buildings' foundations, produces flames and terrifying noises, and makes apparitions appear and vanish. The dictionary also assigns him the ability to animate a corpse briefly.
