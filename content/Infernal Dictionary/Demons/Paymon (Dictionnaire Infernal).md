# Paymon

Paymon is an infernal king appearing as a crowned rider on a dromedary, with a woman’s face and a diadem set with jewels. Two hundred legions obey him, drawn from the orders of Angels and Powers. When summoned with a sacrifice or libation, he appears with the princes [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Bebal (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bebal]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Abalam (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Abalam]].
