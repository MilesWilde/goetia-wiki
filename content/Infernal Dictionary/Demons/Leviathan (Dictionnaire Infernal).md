# Leviathan

Leviathan is the great admiral of hell in the dictionary's demonological account, and Wierus is cited for the title of great liar. He teaches deceit, possesses travelers, and is difficult to expel. The monthly table assigns February to him.

The same entry also records Leviathan as an enormous aquatic creature reserved for the Messiah's feast, and discusses proposed identifications with the whale and crocodile. These are different strands collected under one headword, rather than one consistent physical description.
