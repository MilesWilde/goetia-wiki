# Morail

Morail is a demon with the power to make people invisible. The dictionary attributes this ability to the Clavicles of Solomon.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=485|PDF 485]].
