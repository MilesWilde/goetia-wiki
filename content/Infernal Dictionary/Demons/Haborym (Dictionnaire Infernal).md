# Haborym

Haborym, also called Aym, is a duke and demon of fires. He rides a viper and carries a burning torch. His three heads are those of a serpent, a man, and a cat, and he commands twenty-six legions. The entry reports that some identify him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Raum (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Raum]], but explicitly calls that identification doubtful.

## Compare the other source

[[Spirits/Aim|Aim (Goetia)]]. **Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary explicitly calls Haborym Aym; both sources give a three-headed duke riding a viper, carrying fire, with twenty-six legions. The dictionary gives a cat as one head; the Goetia gives a calf. The dictionary also doubts the proposed identity with Raum.
