# Mullin

Mullin is an inferior demon serving as [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]]’s first valet. The dictionary also notes a Master Jean Mullin in witch-trial material, described as a deputy to the presiding master of the sabbath; it does not explain the relationship between the two notices.
