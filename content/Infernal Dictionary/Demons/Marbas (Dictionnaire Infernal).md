# Marbas

Marbas, also called Barbas, is a great president of hell. He appears as a furious lion, then takes human form before an exorcist to reveal hidden things. He sends diseases, teaches mechanical arts, and transforms people into different shapes. The dictionary gives him thirty-six legions.
