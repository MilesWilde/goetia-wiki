# Alastor

Alastor is the severe executioner who carries out the infernal monarch’s sentences. The dictionary compares his office with that of Nemesis.

It collects differing identifications with a destroying angel or [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Azazel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azazel]], and also notes the plural use of alastores for avenging spirits. These comparisons do not establish that all the names denote one being.
