# Mephistopheles

Mephistopheles is Faust's demon, characterized by cold malice, bitter laughter, and pleasure in suffering. The dictionary emphasizes his use of mockery and slander against virtue, talent, and reputation, and calls him the most formidable leader of hell after [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]].
