# Bael

Bael is the first king of the eastern infernal realm and commands sixty-six legions. He has the heads of a man, cat, and toad, speaks hoarsely, and gives cunning and invisibility.

The dictionary raises, but does not settle, whether he should be identified with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Baal (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Baal]].
