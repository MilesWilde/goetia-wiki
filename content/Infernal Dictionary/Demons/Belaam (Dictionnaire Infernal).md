# Belaam

Belaam is named among the demons said to possess a nun at Loudun in 1632. The entry associates him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Isaacarum (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Isaacarum]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Behemoth (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Behemoth]] in the account of her exorcism.
