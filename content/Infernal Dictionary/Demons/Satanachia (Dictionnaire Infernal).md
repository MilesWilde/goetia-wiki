# Satanachia

Satanachia is a great general in the grimoire hierarchy quoted by the dictionary. He commands [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Pruflas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pruflas]], Aamon, and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Barbatos (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbatos]], and the passage attributes to him power over women and a large body of spirits. These roles belong to this particular quoted hierarchy.
