# Brudemort

Brudemort is a black demon in the dictionary’s account of Norman folklore. He is accompanied by a vast company of terrifying nocturnal spirits and is opposed by [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Burgifer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Burgifer]].
