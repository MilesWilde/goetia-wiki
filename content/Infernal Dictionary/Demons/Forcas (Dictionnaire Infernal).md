# Forcas

The dictionary combines the names Forcas, Forras, and Furcas in one entry, describing a knight and great president of hell. He is a vigorous man with a long beard and white hair, riding a large horse and carrying a sharp spear. He knows the virtues of herbs and precious stones, and teaches logic, aesthetics, palmistry, pyromancy, and rhetoric.

He grants invisibility, ingenuity, and eloquence, recovers lost things, and discovers treasures. The entry gives him twenty-nine legions. These titles and powers are retained together as this edition presents them.

## Compare the other source

[[Spirits/Foras|Foras (Goetia)]]. **Qualified comparison.** The Dictionnaire groups Forças, Forras, and Furcas, combining Foras-like treasure, lost-object, and invisibility functions with Furcas-like appearance and divination. The Goetia separates Foras and Furcas. The dictionary combines characteristics of both, so neither comparison establishes a complete identity.

[[Spirits/Furcas|Furcas (Goetia)]]. **Qualified comparison.** The Dictionnaire groups Forças, Forras, and Furcas. Its mounted, white-haired knight and palm-reading and fire-divination functions correspond to Goetia Furcas. The dictionary also includes functions and a legion count associated with Goetia Foras; its combined account remains ambiguous.
