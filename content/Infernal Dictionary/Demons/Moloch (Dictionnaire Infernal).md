# Moloch

Moloch is a prince of the land of tears and a member of the infernal council. The dictionary describes a crowned, calf-headed bronze image on a metal throne and connects it with accounts of child sacrifice; it also cites Milton's dreadful representation of the figure.

A further account gives the statue seven internal compartments for different offerings and explains how this led to a confusion with Mithras. These descriptions and the proposed confusion are claims of the collected historical sources.
