# Rimmon

Rimmon is an inferior demon serving as the infernal emperor’s first physician, although he is not highly respected. The dictionary also describes a deity worshipped at Damascus under the names Remmon or Remnon, variously equated with Saturn or the sun and credited with healing leprosy. These identifications remain attributed and uncertain.
