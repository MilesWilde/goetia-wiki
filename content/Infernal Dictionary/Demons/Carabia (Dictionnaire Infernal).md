# Carabia

Carabia, also called Decarabia, is king of one part of hell and count of another. He appears as a five-rayed star, like [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Buer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Buer]].

He knows the properties of plants and precious stones and governs birds, making them familiar. Thirty legions serve him.
