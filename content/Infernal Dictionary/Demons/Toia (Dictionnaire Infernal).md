# Toia

Toia is the name the dictionary gives to a devil regarded as an author of evil in its account of the peoples of Florida. It supplies no further individual narrative.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=673|PDF 673]].
