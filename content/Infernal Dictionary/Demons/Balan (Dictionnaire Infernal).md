# Balan

Balan is a powerful infernal king with the heads of a bull, man, and ram, a serpent’s tail, and fiery eyes. He is usually shown riding a bear and carrying a hawk.

He knows past, present, and future, grants invisibility and cunning, and commands forty legions. The entry places his earlier rank among the Dominations.
