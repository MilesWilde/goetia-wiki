# Diave

Diave is the name for the devil in the Maldives in the dictionary’s account, citing Pyrard de Laval’s voyage printed in 1615. The world is imagined as a floating disk protected from surrounding waters by a copper wall. Diave tries to pierce that wall each night; success would bring the final flood and the end of the world. The account connects this danger with prayers before dawn asking God to prevent it.
