# Barrabas

Barrabas or Barrabam is given as a contemptuous name used for the devil in witch-trial accounts. The entry does not describe a separate infernal office.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=93|PDF 93]].
