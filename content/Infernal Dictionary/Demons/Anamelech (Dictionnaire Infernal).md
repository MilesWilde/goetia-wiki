# Anamelech

Anamelech is an obscure bearer of bad news, worshipped at Sepharvaim and represented as a quail. The dictionary reports a proposed interpretation of Anamelech as the moon and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Adramelech (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Adramelech]] as the sun, rather than treating that interpretation as certain.
