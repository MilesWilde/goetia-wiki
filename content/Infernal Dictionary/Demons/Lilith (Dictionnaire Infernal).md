# Lilith

Lilith is the prince or princess of the succubi in the account attributed to Wierus and other demonographers. The demons under this ruler share the name and threaten newborn children. The dictionary reports an inscription invoking Adam and Eve and excluding Lilith as a protection for a woman after childbirth.
