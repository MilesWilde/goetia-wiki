# Nysrock

Nysrock is paired with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Kelen (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Kelen]] among the demons that demonographers assign to debauchery, dances, and orgies. No separate appearance or narrative is supplied.

Nysrock is a second-order demon and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]]’s cook, associated with refined temptation and the pleasures of the table. The entry supplies no appearance or separate narrative.
