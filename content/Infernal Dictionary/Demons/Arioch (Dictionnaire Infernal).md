# Arioch

Arioch is a demon of vengeance acting on behalf of particular people who employ him. The dictionary expressly distinguishes this private vengeance from [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Alastor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Alastor]]’s office.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].
