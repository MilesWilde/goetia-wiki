# Antesser

Antesser is the demon who carries witches to Blokula in the dictionary’s account of the Swedish trials around 1670. He takes several forms, commonly a figure in a grey coat, red breeches, blue stockings, a pointed hat, and a red beard.

At the gathering he presides over feasting, games, violence, and music. The story belongs to allegations and confessions collected in a witch-trial narrative.
