# Gabino

Gabino is a demon of the same kind as [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Kleudde (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Kleudde]]. The dictionary says that he usually appears in the skin of a wild horse and is greatly feared around Vannes.

## Sources
