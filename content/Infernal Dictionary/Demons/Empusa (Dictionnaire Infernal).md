# Empusa

Empusa is a noonday demon and shapeshifting spectre. The dictionary describes forms including a dog, woman, ox, and viper, together with an ass’s foot, a bronze foot, and fire around the head.

It cites Aristophanes’ Frogs and reports later rural fears that the creature harms people working during haymaking and harvest.
