# Berith

Berith is a duke with twenty-six legions, appearing as a red-clad soldier on a red horse and wearing a crown. He is credited with knowledge of past, present, and future.

He changes metals into gold and grants dignities, but the dictionary calls him deceptive. It also gives Beal and Bolfri as names used for him.
