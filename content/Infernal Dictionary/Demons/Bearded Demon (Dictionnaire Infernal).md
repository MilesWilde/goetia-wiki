# Bearded Demon

The Bearded Demon is associated with the philosopher’s stone. The dictionary explicitly questions attempts to identify him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Barbatos (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbatos]] and distinguishes him from [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Marbas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Barbas]]. His beard is the only appearance detail supplied.
