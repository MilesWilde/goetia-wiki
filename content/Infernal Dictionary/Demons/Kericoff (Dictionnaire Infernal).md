# Kericoff

Kericoff is a feared Russian lake demon. He beats the stormy water with horses' feet, raises waterspouts, and sinks boats with large black hands. He then pursues a sailor trying to escape on a plank or barrel, who may turn to see the spirit's enormous human head.
