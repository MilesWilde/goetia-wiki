# Uphir

Uphir is a demon skilled in chemistry and medicinal plants. He is responsible for the health of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]] and the great figures of the infernal court.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=686|PDF 686]].
