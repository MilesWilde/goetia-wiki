# Verdelet

Verdelet is a demon of the second order and master of ceremonies at the infernal court. He transports witches to the sabbath. Citing Boguet, the dictionary gives Jolibois, Vert-Joli, Saute-Buisson, and Master Parsley as pleasant-sounding names used to entice women into his traps.
