# Caym

Caym is a grand president with thirty legions, formerly of the angelic order. He usually appears as a blackbird; in human form he bears a sword and answers from a fire.

He is an accomplished disputant, knows the future, and explains bird song, animal calls, and the sound of waves. The dictionary also gives a human appearance with a crest and peacock’s tail.
