# Leonard

Leonard is a high-ranking demon, master of Sabbaths and supervisor of sorcery and black magic. His chief form is a tall goat with three horns, fox ears, flaming eyes, a goat's beard, and a second face behind. The dictionary also gives him the forms of a greyhound, ox, large black bird, or tree trunk topped with a dark face. When he has feet, they are goose feet.

He is usually silent and melancholy, but presides over assemblies with imposing gravity. The entry's catalog of forms belongs to its depiction of the Sabbath master.
