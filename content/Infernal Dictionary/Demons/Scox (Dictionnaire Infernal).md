# Scox

Scox or Ghax is an infernal duke and great marquis appearing as a stork, with a hoarse voice and a deceitful disposition. He steals money and horses; the money is returned only after twelve hundred years if he is ordered to do so. The entry says he lies outside a triangle but speaks truth about supernatural matters when confined within one. He locates treasures unguarded by harmful spirits and commands thirty legions.
