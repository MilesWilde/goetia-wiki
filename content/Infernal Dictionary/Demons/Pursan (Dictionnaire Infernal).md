# Pursan

Pursan or Curson is a great infernal king who appears in human dress with a lion-like head and carries a snake. He sometimes rides a bear, continually preceded by trumpets. He knows past, present and future, reveals buried things and treasures, and is called the father of good familiar spirits. Twenty-two legions obey him.
