# Chamos

Chamos is the demon of flattery and a member of the infernal council. The dictionary connects him with Moabite and Ammonite worship.

It collects competing identifications with the sun, Jupiter-Ammon, Comus, or an underworld divinity. These alternatives are reported scholarly proposals rather than a settled identity.
