# Angat

Angat is a name for the devil in the dictionary’s account of Madagascar. He is represented as a serpent and described as cruel and bloodthirsty.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=47|PDF 47]].
