# Azazel

Azazel is a second-order spirit associated with goats and the scapegoat of the Day of Atonement. The dictionary also reports other literary and sectarian uses of the name, including Milton’s infernal standard-bearer.

These descriptions differ from the separate [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Azael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azael]] entry about a chained rebel angel.
