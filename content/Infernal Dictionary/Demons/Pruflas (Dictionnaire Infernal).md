# Pruflas

Pruflas or Busas is a great prince and duke of the infernal empire. The dictionary says he ruled in Babylon with an owl’s head. He provokes discord, wars, and quarrels, reduces people to begging, and answers questions freely. Twenty-six legions obey him.
