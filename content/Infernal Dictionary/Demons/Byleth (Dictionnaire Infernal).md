# Byleth

Byleth is a powerful infernal king who rides a white horse, preceded by cats sounding horns and trumpets. He initially responds with fury and is portrayed as difficult to command.

The dictionary assigns him eighty legions and reports his hope of returning to the seventh heavenly throne, while expressing doubt that this will happen.
