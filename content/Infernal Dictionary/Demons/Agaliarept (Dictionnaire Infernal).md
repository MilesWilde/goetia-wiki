# Agaliarept

Agaliarept is called both a grand seneschal and a general in the quoted pact text. He discovers the most closely guarded secrets of courts and governments and commands the second legion of spirits; [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Buer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Buer]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Gusoyn (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Gusoyn]], and Botis are assigned beneath him.
