# Adonis

Adonis appears here as a demon associated with burning and fires. The dictionary says that some identify him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Thamuz (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Thamuz]]; it does not present that identification as unanimous.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=21|PDF 21]].
