# Satiel

Satiel is one of the spirits whom [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Magoa (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Magoa]] is commanded to send in the grimoire invocation reproduced by the dictionary. The passage asks the attendant to answer the caller, but gives no separate appearance or rank.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=441|PDF 441]].
