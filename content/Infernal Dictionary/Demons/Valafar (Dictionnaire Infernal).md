# Valafar

Valafar or Malafar is a powerful infernal duke. In this edition he appears as an angel, or as a lion with a goose’s head and feet and a hare’s tail. He knows past and future, gives people intelligence and boldness, and commands thirty-six legions.

## Compare the other source

[[Spirits/Ipos|Ipos (Goetia)]]. **Qualified comparison.** The Dictionnaire Valafar entry describes an angel or lion with goose features, a hare’s tail, knowledge, boldness, and thirty-six legions: traits also associated with Ipos. The entry is headed Valafar. Its repeated Ipos-like description is a textual discrepancy rather than an established identity.

Goetia Valefor has a lion’s body, an ass’s head, a thieving familiar role, and ten legions, unlike the dictionary’s description.
