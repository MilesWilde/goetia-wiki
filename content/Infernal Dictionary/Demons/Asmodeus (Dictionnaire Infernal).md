# Asmodeus

Asmodeus is a destructive demon associated with gambling, dissipation, and error. The entry collects stories of his conflict with Solomon and his imprisonment by [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Raphael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Raphael]] after his defeat in the story of Tobias and Sarah.

In the infernal hierarchy he is a powerful king under [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Amoymon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Amoymon]], with seventy-two legions. He has the heads of a bull, man, and ram, a serpent’s tail, goose feet, and fiery breath, and rides a dragon carrying a lance and standard.

He teaches mathematics, astronomy, and mechanical arts, reveals treasure, and grants invisibility. Some of the source’s authorities identify him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Samael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Samael]], but that is one reported opinion.
