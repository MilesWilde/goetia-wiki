# Kiakiak

Kiakiak is the demon of Pegu in the dictionary's account. His temple stands on a mountain and only the religious attendants dare enter it. He will one day destroy the world, after which the supreme deity [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Dagoun (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Dagoun]] will make a better one.
