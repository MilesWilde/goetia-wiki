# Phoenix

The demonic Phoenix is a great marquis who appears as the bird of that name and speaks with a child’s voice. His arrival is preceded by melodious sounds, although the entry tells the exorcist to stop his ears when commanding a human appearance. He answers questions about the sciences and composes verse in reply to requests. He hopes to return to the seventh order of Thrones after a thousand years and commands twenty legions.
