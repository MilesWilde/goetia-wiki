# Astaroth

Astaroth is a powerful duke with forty legions. He appears as an ugly angel riding a dragon and carrying a viper in his left hand.

He knows about creation and the fall of the angels, teaches the arts, and claims that his punishment was unjust. The entry also mentions different appearances in Faust traditions, rather than giving him only one form.
