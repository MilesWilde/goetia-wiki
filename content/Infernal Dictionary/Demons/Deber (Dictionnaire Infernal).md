# Deber

Deber is the demon who harms at night. The dictionary contrasts him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Cheteb (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Cheteb]] or Chereb, whose harmful activity occurs at noon.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=213|PDF 213]].
