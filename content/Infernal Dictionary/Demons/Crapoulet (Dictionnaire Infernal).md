# Crapoulet

Crapoulet is named alongside [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Zozo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Zozo]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Mimi (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Mimi]] in a reported possession at Teilly in Picardy in 1816. The dictionary gives no separate appearance or powers for this demon.

The fuller discussion under Possessed People rejects this reported possession: it says the young woman wanted to pass as possessed, that the authorities judged her ill, and that she entered hospital, after which the possession report ceased.
