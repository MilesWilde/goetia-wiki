# Abalam

Abalam is a little-known prince of hell in [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Paymon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Paymon]]’s entourage. The short entry supplies no appearance or independent powers.

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Abalim (Goetia)|Abalim]]. **Corresponding accounts.** A named attendant of Paymon/Paimon; both sources pair that companion with another royal attendant. The dictionary calls Abalam a prince; the Goetia calls Abalim a king. The identification of their companions Bebal and Labal remains uncertain.
