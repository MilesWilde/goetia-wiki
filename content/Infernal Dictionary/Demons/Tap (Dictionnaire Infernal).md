# Tap

Tap or Gaap is a great president and prince who appears in human form at noon. The dictionary says he commands four principal infernal kings and compares his power with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Byleth (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Byleth]]’s. He arouses love and hatred, exercises authority over demons subject to Amaymon, transports people rapidly between countries, and commands sixty legions. The entry discusses books and rites attributed to Solomon, Ham, Elijah, and Elisha while disputing some of those attributions.
