# Abigor

Abigor is a grand duke commanding sixty legions. He appears as a handsome mounted warrior carrying a lance, standard, or sceptre.

He knows the future and the secrets of warfare, and teaches commanders how to win their soldiers’ affection. The later Éligor entry explicitly refers back to Abigor.
