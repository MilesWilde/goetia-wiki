# Zepar

Zepar is a great infernal duke appearing as a warrior. The dictionary associates him with passions it condemns and gives him twenty-eight legions. It suggests that he might be [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Vepar (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vepar]] or Separ, while still describing him under a separate name.
