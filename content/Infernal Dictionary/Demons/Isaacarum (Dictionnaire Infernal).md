# Isaacarum

Isaacarum is named as one of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Leviathan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Leviathan]]'s assistants in the alleged possession at Loudun. The dictionary gives no separate rank, appearance, or powers.
