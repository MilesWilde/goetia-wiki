# Rahuart

Rahuart accompanies [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]] in a morality play about the rich man and Lazarus, printed at Rouen and performed at the end of the fifteenth century. He carries away the dead rich man’s soul in the basket on his back. The dictionary says it knows little else about him.
