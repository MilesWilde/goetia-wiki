# Amy

Amy is a grand president who appears surrounded by flames in hell and assumes human features on earth. He teaches astrology and the liberal arts, provides servants, and reveals treasures.

Thirty-six legions obey him. The entry reports his hope of returning to a heavenly throne after two hundred thousand years, but says Weyer rejects that hope.
