# Barbatos

Barbatos is an infernal count and duke who appears as an archer or hunter in a forest, accompanied by kings and the sound of horns. Thirty legions serve him.

He understands animal voices, reveals hidden treasures and past and future events, and reconciles friends. The entry gives differing accounts of his former angelic order.
