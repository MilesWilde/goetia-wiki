# Goap

Goap is called the king of the demons of midday. The dictionary gives periods for his evocation from three in the morning to noon and from nine at night to midnight.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=317|PDF 317]].

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Göap (Goetia)|Göap]]. **Shared name.** The dictionary separately calls Goap king of the demons of the south/noon; Goetia separately names Göap among the four great kings associated with the quarters. The the dictionary entry is distinct from its Gaap-to-Tap redirect. Both sources distinguish this royal name from their numbered or separately described Gaap/Tap figure, without settling every relationship.
