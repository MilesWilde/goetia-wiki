# Agares

Agares is a grand duke of the eastern infernal realm, formerly of the order of Virtues, with thirty-one legions. He rides a crocodile and carries a hawk.

He rallies fugitives on the side he favours, routs opponents, grants dignities, teaches languages, and makes the spirits of the earth dance.
