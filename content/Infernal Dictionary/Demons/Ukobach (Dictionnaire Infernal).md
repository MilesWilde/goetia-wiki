# Ukobach

Ukobach is an inferior demon whose body is wholly aflame. The dictionary credits him with inventing frying and fireworks and says [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]] assigns him to maintain the oil in the infernal cauldrons.
