# Mimi

Mimi is one of the three demons named in the dictionary’s report of a young woman’s possession at Teilly in Picardy in 1816. The other names are [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Zozo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Zozo]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Crapoulet (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Crapoulet]]; no individual traits are assigned to Mimi.

The fuller discussion under Possessed People rejects this reported possession: it says the young woman wanted to pass as possessed, that the authorities judged her ill, and that she entered hospital, after which the possession report ceased.
