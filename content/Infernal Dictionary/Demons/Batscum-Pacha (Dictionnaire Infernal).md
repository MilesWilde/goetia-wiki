# Batscum-Pacha

Batscum-Pacha is a demon invoked for fair weather or rain. The dictionary calls him Turkish and says toasted bread is offered to win his favour.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=96|PDF 96]].
