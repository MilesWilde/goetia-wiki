# Caeculus

Caeculus is a small demon born from a spark that flies from [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vulcan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vulcan]]’s forge into Prenesta’s bosom. Raised among wild animals, he lives in fire as his natural element.

His very small eyes are affected by smoke. The dictionary says cabalists classify him as a salamander.
