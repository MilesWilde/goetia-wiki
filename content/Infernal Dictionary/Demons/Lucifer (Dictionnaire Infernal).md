# Lucifer

Lucifer is an eastern spirit in the dictionary's magical account, invoked on Monday and satisfied with a mouse as an offering. He is often treated as king of hell; some of the demonographers cited place him above [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]]. Other passages make him hell's chief judicial officer and the first name in the Sabbath litanies.

The entry combines several representations: a beautiful child, a fiery-faced figure when angry, and the gray-clad apparition described in Swedish witch testimony. It also reports mischievous behavior toward witches on their broomsticks. The monthly table assigns May to Lucifer.
