# Lucifuge Rofocale

In the grimoire passage quoted under Pacts, Lucifuge Rofocale is the infernal prime minister, subordinate to [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Lucifer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Lucifer]] and entrusted with the world’s riches and treasures. [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Bael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bael]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Agares (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Agares]], and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Marbas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Marbas]] are assigned to his command. The quoted invocation presents him as a negotiating spirit in a pact narrative.
