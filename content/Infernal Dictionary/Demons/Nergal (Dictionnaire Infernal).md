# Nergal

In the dictionary’s infernal bureaucracy, Nergal is a second-order demon, chief of police and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beelzebub (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beelzebub]]’s principal spy, supervised by [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Lucifer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Lucifer]]. The entry separately identifies Nergal or Nergel as an Assyrian idol and suggests an association with fire. Its demonological and historical interpretations should remain distinguished.
