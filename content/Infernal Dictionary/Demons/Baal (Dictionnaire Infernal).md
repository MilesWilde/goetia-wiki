# Baal

Baal is described as a grand duke with extensive infernal authority, sometimes made commander of hell’s armies. The entry gathers accounts of ancient worship under this name and mentions solar interpretations.

The separate [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Bael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bael]] entry questions whether its three-headed infernal king is the same being. The two therefore remain distinct here.
