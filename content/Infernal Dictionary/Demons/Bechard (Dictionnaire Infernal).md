# Bechard

Bechard is a spirit of winds and storms in the dictionary’s account of the Clavicles of Solomon. He is credited with producing hail, thunder, and rain.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=98|PDF 98]].
