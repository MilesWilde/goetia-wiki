# Amon

Amon is a marquis commanding forty legions. He appears as a wolf with a serpent’s tail and fiery breath, or in human form with an owl’s head and canine teeth.

He knows past and future events and reconciles friends. The entry also associates him with the Egyptian Amun; that is an identification made by this dictionary, rather than proof that the traditions are identical.
