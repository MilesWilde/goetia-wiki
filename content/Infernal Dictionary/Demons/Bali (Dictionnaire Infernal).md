# Bali

Bali is an infernal prince in the dictionary’s account of Indian mythology. [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vishnu (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vishnu]] casts him into the abyss, and his subsequent opportunity to emerge is strictly limited.

The entry also mentions household spirits called balis; those plural spirits should not automatically be identified with the named prince.
