# Zozo

Zozo is named with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Mimi (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Mimi]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Crapoulet (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Crapoulet]] in a reported possession of a young woman at Teilly in Picardy in 1816. The dictionary gives no distinct appearance, rank, or powers for Zozo.

The fuller discussion under Possessed People rejects this reported possession: it says the young woman wanted to pass as possessed, that the authorities judged her ill, and that she entered hospital, after which the possession report ceased.
