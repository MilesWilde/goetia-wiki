# Beherit

Beherit is a demon named in the Loudun possession accounts. In one episode he promises a sign involving a commissioner’s cap but fails to perform it.

The source raises a possible connection with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Berith (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Berith]] without establishing that they are identical.
