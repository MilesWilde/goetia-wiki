# Fleurety

Fleurety is a lieutenant general in the dictionary’s quoted grimoire passage. His powers include carrying out requested work during the night and causing hail. Bathim, [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Pursan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pursan]], and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Abigor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Abigor]] are assigned to his command.
