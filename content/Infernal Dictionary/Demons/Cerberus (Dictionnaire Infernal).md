# Cerberus

The dictionary’s demonographic Cerberus, also called Naberus, is a powerful marquis with nineteen legions. He appears as a raven or with three canine heads, speaks hoarsely, and teaches eloquence, agreeable conduct, and the fine arts.

The entry expressly contrasts this figure with the classical dog guarding the underworld, whose number of heads varies between authorities. Its use of a shared name should not erase that distinction.

The separate Naberus entry, also using the name Nebiros, describes an infernal marquis, field marshal, and inspector of armies. He appears as a raven and speaks hoarsely. The dictionary credits him with eloquence, social charm, instruction in the liberal arts, knowledge of metals, plants and animals, discovery of the hand of glory, and prediction of the future. He commands nineteen legions.

[[Spirits/Naberius|Naberius (Goetia)]]. **Corresponding accounts.** Nabérus/Cerberus description gives hoarse bird, rhetoric/liberal arts and nineteen legions, matching Naberius's offices/count. The dictionary equates Naberus with Cerberus and expands Nebiros’s offices. The Goetia describes a black crane rather than a raven or three-headed dog.
