# Orias

Orias is an infernal marquis associated with astrologers and diviners. He appears as a furious lion riding a horse with a serpent’s tail and holds a viper in each hand. He knows astronomy, teaches astrology, transforms people at their wish, obtains dignities and titles for them, and commands thirty legions.
