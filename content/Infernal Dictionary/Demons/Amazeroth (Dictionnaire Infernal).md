# Amazeroth

Amazeroth is an infernal duke commanding sixty legions. The dictionary attributes this brief information to Reginald Scot.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=37|PDF 37]].
