# Belial

Belial is an infernal king appearing as a beautiful angel in a fiery chariot. He grants dignities, favour, and servants, but the dictionary stresses the unreliability of his answers.

Eighty legions serve him. The entry relates his fall from heaven and includes the story of Solomon confining demons in a vessel that is later opened.
