# The Red Man

The Red Man is a dangerous weather spirit in a Breton story associated with Saint-Pol-de-Léon: he controls a storm and threatens a traveler with drowning. The same entry also records a little Red Man associated with Napoleon and ominous appearances. The dictionary places these accounts together without establishing that they describe one being.
