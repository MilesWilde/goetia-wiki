# Adramelech

Adramelech is hell’s grand chancellor, keeper of the sovereign’s wardrobe, and president of the infernal high council. The dictionary gives him the appearance of a mule or a peacock.

It also connects him with worship at Sepharvaim and reports child sacrifice there. The historical religious claim is part of the source’s account.
