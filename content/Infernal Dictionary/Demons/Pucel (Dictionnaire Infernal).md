# Pucel

Pucel is a powerful infernal duke appearing as a dark angel. He answers about occult sciences and teaches geometry and the liberal arts. He causes loud noises, including the roar of water where there is none, and commands forty-eight legions. The dictionary suggests that he might be [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pocel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pocel]], but does not settle that identification.
