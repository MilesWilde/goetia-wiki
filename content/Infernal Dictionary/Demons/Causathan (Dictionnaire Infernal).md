# Causathan

Causathan is an evil spirit whom Porphyry is said to have expelled from a public bath. The dictionary gives no further description.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=159|PDF 159]].
