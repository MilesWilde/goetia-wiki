# Chassi

Chassi is a tormenting demon in the dictionary’s account of the Mariana Islands. The infernal realm is described as his house.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=174|PDF 174]].
