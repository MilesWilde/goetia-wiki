# Eurynome

Eurynome is a higher demon and prince of death according to the demonologists cited by the dictionary. He has long teeth, a body covered in sores, and a fox skin for clothing. The entry also attributes to Pausanias a corpse-eating figure represented at Delphi with dark skin and large teeth, seated on a vulture skin.
