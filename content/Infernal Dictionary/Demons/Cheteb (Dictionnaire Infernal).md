# Cheteb

Cheteb, also called Chereb in the entry, is the demon who harms at midday. He is contrasted with [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Deber (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Deber]], the nocturnal demon.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=213|PDF 213]].
