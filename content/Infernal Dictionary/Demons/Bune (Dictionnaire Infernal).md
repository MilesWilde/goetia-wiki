# Bune

Bune is a grand duke with thirty legions, appearing as a dragon with three heads, only one of them human. This edition explicitly says that he speaks only through signs.

He moves corpses, frequents cemeteries, and gathers demons around graves. He also claims to enrich his followers and make them eloquent.
