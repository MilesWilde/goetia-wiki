# Clauneck

Clauneck governs possessions and wealth and finds treasure for a person he serves through a pact. The dictionary says [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Lucifer (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Lucifer]] favours him and allows him to distribute money.
