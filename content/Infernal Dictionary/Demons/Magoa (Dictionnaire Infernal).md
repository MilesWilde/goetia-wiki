# Magoa

Magoa is a powerful demon and king of the east. The dictionary reproduces a grimoire invocation commanding him either to appear or to send [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Massayel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Massayel]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Asiel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Asiel]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satiel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satiel]], [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Arduel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Arduel]], or [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Acorib (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Acorib]] to answer the caller. It gives no individual appearances for these attendants.
