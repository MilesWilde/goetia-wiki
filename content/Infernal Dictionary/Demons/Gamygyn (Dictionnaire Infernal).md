# Gamygyn

Gamygyn is an infernal marquis who appears as a small horse, or in human form with a hoarse voice. He teaches the liberal arts and brings before the exorcist the souls of drowned people and those suffering in the purgatory called Cartagra. He answers clearly and commands thirty legions.
