# Halphas

Halphas is a great infernal count who appears as a stork and has a noisy voice. He builds towns, orders wars, and commands twenty-six legions. The dictionary suggests that he may be the same as [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Malphas (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Malphas]], but leaves the identification uncertain.
