# Xaphan

Xaphan is a demon of the second order who joins [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]]’s rebellion and proposes setting heaven on fire. Cast into the abyss with the other rebels, he continually blows the coals of the furnaces with his mouth and hands. A bellows is his emblem.
