# Picollus

Picollus is described as a being honoured by the ancient Prussians with a dead person’s head and burning tallow. He appears near the final days of important people; repeated appearances require increasingly severe appeasement in the dictionary’s account. When satisfied, he can be heard laughing in his temple.
