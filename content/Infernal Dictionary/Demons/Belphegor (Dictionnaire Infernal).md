# Belphegor

Belphegor is associated with inventions, discoveries, and the acquisition of wealth. He may appear as a young woman.

The entry relates him to the Moabite Baal-Phegor and collects disputed comparisons with other ancient divinities. These scholarly comparisons are not uniform identifications.
