# Oze

Oze is a great infernal president who takes the shape of a leopard or a human. He teaches the liberal arts, answers about divine and abstract matters, transforms people, and can make a person believe he is a king or emperor. Although Oze wears a crown, the dictionary says his reign lasts only one hour each day.
