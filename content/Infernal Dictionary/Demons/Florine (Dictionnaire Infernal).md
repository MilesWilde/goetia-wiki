# Florine

Florine, also called Fiorina or Florinde, is the familiar demon associated with a sorcerer named Pinet. The dictionary attributes the report to Pico della Mirandola. Its separate Pinet entry says the association lasted thirty years; it gives no distinct appearance for the familiar.
