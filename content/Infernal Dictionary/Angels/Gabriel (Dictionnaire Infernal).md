# Gabriel

The dictionary's table of angels of the months assigns January to Gabriel. It attributes the calendar to cabalists and supplies no further description in this passage.

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Gabriel as the angel presiding over hour 4. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Gabriel (Goetia)|Gabriel]]. **Corresponding accounts.** Both sources explicitly identify Gabriel as an angel in ceremonial or cabalistic correspondence lists. the dictionary assigns January and the fourth hour; Goetia places Gabriel in the Iesod/Moon sequence and calls him a messenger. The dictionary assigns a month and an hour; the Goetia gives a different set of ceremonial correspondences.
