# Marot

Marot and [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Arot (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Arot]] are the angels in the dictionary's story explaining a prohibition of wine. Sent to earth, they become drunk and reveal the words by which they ascend to heaven. Their hostess uses those words and becomes the morning star, while the angels are condemned to hang by their feet in the well of Babel until Judgment Day.
