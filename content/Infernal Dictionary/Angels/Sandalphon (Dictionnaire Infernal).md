# Sandalphon

Sandalphon is one of the three superior intelligences of cabala in the dictionary, alongside [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Metatron (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Metatron]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Acatriel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Acatriel]]. The [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ensoph (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ensoph]] entry places them over the ranks of good spirits.

Sandalphon is identified as one of three higher intelligences of Jewish cabala. The dictionary’s short notice supplies no appearance, task, or names for the other two.
