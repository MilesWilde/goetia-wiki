# Michael

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Michael as the angel presiding over hour 1. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.

## Sources

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Michael (Goetia)|Michael]]. **Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary’s hourly angel list has Michaël at the first hour. Goetia explicitly attests Michael/Michaël as an angel and uses the name in its circle, triangle, and prayers. The dictionary assigns the first hour; the Goetia associates Michael with Hod, Mercury, and the heavenly host.
