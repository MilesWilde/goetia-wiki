# Mordad

Mordad is the angel of death among the Magi in the dictionary's brief entry. The separate [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Melek-al-Mout (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Melek-al-Mout]] article tentatively associates that angel with Mordad, [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Azrael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azrael]], and the name [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Asuman (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Asuman]].
