# Nakir

Nakir, called Nékir in the dictionary, accompanies [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Munkar (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Munkar]] in questioning the dead and punishing those judged condemned. Their shared entry describes frightening forms and voices like thunder, without distinguishing separate duties.
