# Sabaoth

Sabaoth is an angel recognized by the Archontics, a second-century religious sect in the dictionary’s account. The author calls the angel’s status doubtful and says only that he has some involvement in worldly affairs.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=598|PDF 598]].

## Compare the other source

[[Names/Zabaoth (Goetia)|Zabaoth]]. **Vocabulary comparison.** The dictionary calls Sabaoth an Archontic angel and elsewhere explains the divine title God of the heavenly hosts. The Goetia uses Sabaoth, Zabaoth, and Tzabaoth as forms of that title. The dictionary’s Archontic angel and the Goetia’s divine title use the same name in different religious contexts.
