# Arael

Arael is another spirit assigned the government of birds in the dictionary’s account of rabbinic tradition.

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Arael as the angel presiding over hour 8. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.
