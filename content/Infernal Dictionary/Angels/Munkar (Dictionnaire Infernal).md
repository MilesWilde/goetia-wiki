# Munkar

Munkar, called Monkir in the dictionary, and [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Nakir (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Nakir]] question a dead person in the tomb about their lord and prophet. The entry gives them terrifying appearances and thunderous voices, and assigns them the punishment of those condemned to hell with an implement of iron and fire.
