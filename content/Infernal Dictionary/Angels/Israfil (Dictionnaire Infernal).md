# Israfil

Israfil is the terrible angel whose trumpet announces resurrection and judgment. The dictionary warns that he is often confused with [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Azrael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azrael]], the angel of death, and treats them as distinct.
