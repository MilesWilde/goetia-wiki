# Arot

Arot accompanies [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Marot (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Marot]] in the dictionary's story about two angels sent to earth. After drinking wine, they reveal the words by which they ascend to heaven. Their hostess uses those words and is transformed into the morning star, while the two angels are condemned to hang by their feet in the well of Babel until Judgment Day. The dictionary presents the tale as an explanation of a prohibition of wine.
