# Azrael

Azrael is the angel of death. In the story reported here, a man asks Solomon to send him far away after encountering the angel, only to reach the place where his death was appointed.

The dictionary distinguishes Azrael from [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Israfil (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Israfil]], whose office is to sound the trumpet of resurrection.
