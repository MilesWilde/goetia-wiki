# Melek-al-Mout

Melek-al-Mout is the angel of death in the Persian account collected by the dictionary. The title 'angel with twenty hands' explains how he can dispatch so many souls. The author tentatively connects him with [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Azrael (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Azrael]] and with [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Mordad (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Mordad]], also called [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Asuman (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Asuman]].
