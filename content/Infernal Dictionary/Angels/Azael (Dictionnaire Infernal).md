# Azael

Azael is one of the angels who rebelled against God. According to the rabbinic account reported here, he is chained among sharp stones in a dark desert place until the last judgment.

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Azael as the angel presiding over hour 11. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.
