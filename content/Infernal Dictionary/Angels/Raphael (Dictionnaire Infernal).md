# Raphael

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Raphael as the angel presiding over hour 3. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=557|PDF 557]].

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Raphael (Goetia)|Raphael]]. **Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary explicitly identifies Raphael as the angel who imprisons Asmodeus in Egypt and lists him at the third hour. Goetia explicitly attests Raphael/Raphaë as an angel in its circle and explanatory prayer. The dictionary’s narrative and hourly assignment differ from the Goetia’s Tiphereth and Sun correspondence.
