# Uriel

The dictionary's table of angels of the months assigns September to Uriel. It attributes the calendar to cabalists and supplies no further description in this passage.

The dictionary’s discussion of the Heptameron lists Uriel as the angel presiding over hour 10. This is a correspondence from the magical work summarized under Pierre d’Apone, whose attribution the author disputes; it supplies no additional appearance or biography.
