# Metatron

Metatron is one of the three superior intelligences in the dictionary's account of cabala, together with [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Acatriel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Acatriel]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Angels/Sandalphon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Sandalphon]]. In the [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ensoph (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ensoph]] article, the three preside over the ranks of good spirits.

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Metatron (Goetia)|Metatron]]. **Corresponding accounts.** The dictionary names Metatron as one of three superior cabalistic intelligences; Goetia explicitly names Metatron in the Kether correspondence and Methrattton in its explanatory prayer. The dictionary groups Metatron with Acatriel and Sandalphon; the Goetia places him among distinct angelic and divine correspondences.
