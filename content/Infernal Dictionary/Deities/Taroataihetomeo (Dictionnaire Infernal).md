# Taroataihetomeo

Taroa-Taihetoomoo is the supreme divinity in the dictionary’s account of Tahiti. With the rock [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Lepapa (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Lepapa]], this being is parent of the subordinate Eatuas, who in turn produce the first human.

The account says even the great divinity can be devoured by lesser spirits but has the power to recreate itself.

Taroataihetomeo is named as the supreme deity of Tahiti. The dictionary suggests that this may be the same figure elsewhere called Taaroa or Eatua, but expresses the identification as probable rather than certain.

The book uses Taroa-taihetoomoo in the Eatuas article and Taroataihetomeo in the separate entry for Tahiti's supreme deity. Its further identification with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Taaora (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Taaora]] or Eatua is presented as probable, not certain.
