# Bogaha

Bogaha is a tree divinity in the dictionary’s account of Ceylon. Its legendary tree flies from afar to provide shade for [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Buddha (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Buddha]] and establishes its roots on the island.
