# Clotho

Clotho is the youngest of the three Fates and spins the thread of destiny, carrying an enormous distaff.

The dictionary records differing locations for her: near [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pluto (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pluto]]’s realm, in [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Charon (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Charon]]’s boat, or in the moon, whose movements she governs. These placements are attributed to different classical authors.
