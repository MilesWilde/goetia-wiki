# Teutates

The dictionary compares Teutates with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pluto (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pluto]] and places his sanctuaries in the forests of ancient Gaul. It describes an atmosphere of fear and worshippers approaching on their knees. The comparison with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pluto (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pluto]] belongs to the source’s interpretation.
