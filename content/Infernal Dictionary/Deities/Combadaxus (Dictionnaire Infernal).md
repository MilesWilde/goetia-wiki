# Combadaxus

Combadaxus is a sleeping divinity in the dictionary’s account of Japan. Formerly a monk, he is said to have built a magnificent temple when eight years old.

He announces that he will sleep for ten thousand years and enters a sealed cavern, where believers consider him still alive.
