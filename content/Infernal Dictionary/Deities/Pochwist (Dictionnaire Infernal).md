# Pochwist

Pochwist is identified as a pre-Christian Polish deity of winter and bad weather. The dictionary gives no individual appearance.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=561|PDF 561]].
