# Choun

Choun is a creator divinity in the dictionary’s account of Peru, with a body without bones or muscles. He lowers mountains, fills valleys, and makes the land passable.

He creates the first inhabitants and teaches them to live on plants and fruit. When offended he makes fertile land barren, but later restores springs and rivers out of compassion.
