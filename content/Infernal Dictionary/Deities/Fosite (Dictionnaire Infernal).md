# Fosite

Fosite is the divinity worshipped on Fositeland in the dictionary's account of the Frisians and Saint Willibrord. Animals and a spring on the island are protected by religious prohibitions, which the saint deliberately breaks. The author classifies the worship as demonic; that classification belongs to his religious interpretation.
