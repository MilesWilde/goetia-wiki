# Varonnin

Varonnin is described as an Indian god of light and identified with the sun. He rides a crocodile and carries a silver whip.

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=695|PDF 695]].
