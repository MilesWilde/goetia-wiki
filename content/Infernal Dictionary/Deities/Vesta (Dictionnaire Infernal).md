# Vesta

The dictionary's calendar of ancient divinities assigns December to Vesta. The list identifies a monthly association rather than providing a full account of the deity.

Vesta is identified as the goddess of fire. The dictionary adds that cabalistic writers make her Noah’s wife; this is an attributed interpretation rather than an unqualified identification.
