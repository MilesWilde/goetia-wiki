# Menra

Menra, or the Word, is the creator in the dictionary's account of cabala. The [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ensoph (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ensoph]] entry explains that [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Ensoph (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ensoph]] creates through Menra, who in turn produces the three great sephiroth.
