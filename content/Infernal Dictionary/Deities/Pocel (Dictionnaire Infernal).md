# Pocel

Pocel is called king of the underworld among the Prussians; Pocol is also given as the name of a chief of aerial spirits. The entry cross-references [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Picollus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Picollus]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Pucel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pucel]], but those references alone do not establish that all the named figures are identical.
