# Poulaguin

Poulaguin is one of the nine lesser Brahmas listed as sons of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]]. This edition says [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]] produces him from his navel.
