# Pollux

Pollux and [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Castor (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Castor]] are divine sons of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]] and Leda and protectors associated with the sea. Their names were given to the luminous phenomenon later called Saint Elmo’s fire.

The dictionary recounts their appearance as horsemen bringing news of a Roman victory, while also preserving an explanation that the supposed apparition was staged.
