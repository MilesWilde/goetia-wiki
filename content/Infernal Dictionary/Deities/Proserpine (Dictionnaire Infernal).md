# Proserpine

Proserpine is [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pluto (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pluto]]’s wife and queen of the underworld in the dictionary’s mythological description. It separately gives her the demonographers’ titles of archduchess and sovereign princess of evil spirits. Its suggested serpent-related etymology is an interpretation belonging to the source.
