# Crom Cruach

Crom Cruach is the chief Irish idol in the dictionary’s account of the period before Saint Patrick. At the saint’s approach, the image falls while lesser divinities sink into the earth.

The dictionary treats the supposed surviving traces of this event as part of the legend.
