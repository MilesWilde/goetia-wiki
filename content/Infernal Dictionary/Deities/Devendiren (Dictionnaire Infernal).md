# Devendiren

Devendiren tests a courtesan’s fidelity by appearing as a man and then pretending to die. When she prepares to follow him in death, he stops the test, reveals himself, marries her, and takes her to his paradise.

The story appears within the dictionary’s discussion of courtesans serving in temples.
