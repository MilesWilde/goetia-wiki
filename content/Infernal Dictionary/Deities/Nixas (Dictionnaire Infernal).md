# Nixas

Nixas or Nicksa is a river or ocean deity associated here with the Baltic shores. The dictionary compares this figure with Neptune and discusses the feared sea-spirit Old Nick alongside the gentler Germanic water-nymph tradition. These proposed relationships belong to the dictionary’s comparative account.
