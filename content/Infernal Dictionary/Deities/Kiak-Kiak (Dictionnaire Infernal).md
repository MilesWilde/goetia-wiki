# Kiak-Kiak

Kiak-Kiak is the chief deity of Pegu in the dictionary’s account. A large reclining human-shaped image, presented as sleeping, stands in a richly adorned temple open to everyone. The author recasts this deity in demonological terms, an interpretation that belongs to his religious perspective.
