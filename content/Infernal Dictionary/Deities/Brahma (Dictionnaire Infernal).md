# Brahma

Brahma is a creator divinity in the dictionary’s account of India. The entry describes nine sons, generated from different parts of his body or from his mind, as lesser Brahmas.

The names and bodily correspondences belong to this edition’s particular retelling; they should not be silently replaced by a genealogy from another source.

A further account has him reflect within a golden egg before making heaven and earth from its shell. The entry describes fourteen worlds produced from his body and says he loses one of five heads in battle. This differs from the punishment story told under [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Beyrevra (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Beyrevra]].
