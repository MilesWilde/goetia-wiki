# Altangatufun

Altangatufun is a Kalmyk idol with a serpent’s head and body and four lizard-like feet. Carrying its image reverently is said to make a warrior invulnerable.

In the legend reported here, archers cannot strike a book while the image hangs from it, but pierce the book once the image is removed. The dictionary labels this a Cossack legend.
