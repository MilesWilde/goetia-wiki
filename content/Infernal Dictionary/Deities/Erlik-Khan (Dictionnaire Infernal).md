# Erlik-Khan

Erlik-Khan is a ruler of hell in the dictionary's account. One representation gives him a buffalo's head and horns and a necklace of skulls; another gives him a human head, four hands, and a sceptre associated with death's heads. The entry names his wife [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Samorindo (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Samorindo]] or Samoundo.
