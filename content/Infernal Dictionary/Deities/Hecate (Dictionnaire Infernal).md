# Hecate

The dictionary casts Hecate as an infernal guardian of roads and crossroads, with three faces: a horse's, a dog's, and a woman's. It attributes to Delrio a description of her arrival accompanied by trembling earth, fire, and barking dogs. A second explanation presents the triple Hecate as [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Diana (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Diana]] on earth, Proserpina below, and the moon in heaven.
