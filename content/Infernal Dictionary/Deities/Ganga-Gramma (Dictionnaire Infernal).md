# Ganga-Gramma

Ganga-Gramma is an Indian divinity whom the author labels a female demon. The dictionary describes one head and four arms, including a bowl and a three-pronged fork, and associates her with a procession in a cart, offerings, and pilgrims' vows. These are the particular descriptions collected by this edition, rather than a general account of Hindu worship.
