# Anninga

Anninga is the moon in the dictionary’s account of Greenland. Originally a boy pursuing his sister [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Malina (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Malina]], he continues the chase into the sky after she marks his face with black and becomes the sun.
