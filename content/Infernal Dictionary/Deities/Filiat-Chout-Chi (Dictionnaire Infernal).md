# Filiat-Chout-Chi

The dictionary names Filiat-Chout-Chi as the father of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Touita (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Touita]] among the deities of the Kamchadals. Under Alkalalai, the broom festival, it gives the spelling Filiat-Choul-Chi and describes a sequence of three deities: this father, [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Touita (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Touita]] his son, and [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Gaetch (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Gaetch]] his grandson. The two source spellings are retained as aliases.
