# Castor

Castor and [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pollux (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pollux]] are sons of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]] and Leda who become maritime divinities. The dictionary connects their names with the lights later called Saint Elmo’s fire.

In a Roman story, the pair appears as young men on white horses to announce victory in Macedonia. The entry also cites a sceptical explanation involving men dressed as the divine twins.
