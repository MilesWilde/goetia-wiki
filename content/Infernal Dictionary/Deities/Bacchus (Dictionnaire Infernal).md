# Bacchus

The dictionary focuses on demonographers’ treatment of Bacchus as an ancient leader of the witches’ Sabbath under the name Sabasius. It describes horns, a weapon, and associations with satyrs and dancing.

It compares this figure with later Sabbath names such as [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Leonard (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Leonard]] and [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Satan (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Satan]]. These are the author’s demonographic associations, rather than a neutral account of all ancient worship of Bacchus.

Sabasius is an epithet of Bacchus which some demonographers reinterpret as the chief of the sabbath. The dictionary adds that cabalists class him as a gnome. It thus records several different interpretive settings for the name.
