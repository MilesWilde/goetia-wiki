# Touita

Touita is the son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Filiat-Chout-Chi (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Filiat-Choul-Chi]] and father of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Gaetch (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Gaetch]] in the three-generation divine group honoured at the broom festival described under Alkalalai.
