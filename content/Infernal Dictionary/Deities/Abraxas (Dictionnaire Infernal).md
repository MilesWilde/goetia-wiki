# Abraxas

The dictionary presents Abraxas through several traditions. On amulets he has a rooster’s head, serpentine legs, and a whip; demonographers give him a king’s head and serpent feet.

In its account of Basilidian teaching, he is the supreme being associated with 365 heavens and their subordinate powers. The numerical value assigned to his Greek name explains the number 365. These descriptions belong to different systems collected in one entry.
