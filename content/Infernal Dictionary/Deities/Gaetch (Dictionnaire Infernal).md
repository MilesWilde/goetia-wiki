# Gaetch

Gaetch is the grandson of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Filiat-Chout-Chi (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Filiat-Choul-Chi]] and son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Touita (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Touita]] in the divine group associated with the festival described under Alkalalai.

Gaetch is named as the Kamchadal god of the dead and a son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Touita (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Touita]]. The article on lizards calls them his spies: encountering one is interpreted as a warning of death, and killing it is supposed to prevent it from carrying news back to the god.
