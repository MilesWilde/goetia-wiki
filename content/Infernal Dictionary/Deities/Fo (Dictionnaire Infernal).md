# Fo

Fo, also called Foë, is presented as one of the principal Chinese divinities, born in India about a thousand years before the Christian era. The dictionary recounts his mother’s dream of swallowing a white elephant and gives his age at death as seventy-nine.

The entry attributes to monks a belief that he was born eight thousand times, passing through many animal bodies before attaining divinity. It connects this with his representations as a dragon, elephant, monkey, and other animals in temples, and describes him as a lawgiver of humanity.
