# Kurma

Kurma is the tortoise form described as [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Vishnu (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Vishnu]]’s second incarnation. The dictionary’s heading uses the historical transcription Courma-Vataram.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=200|PDF 200]].
