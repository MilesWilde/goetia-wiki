# Kali

Kali is presented by this dictionary as queen of demons in an Indian infernal realm. It describes her as black and wearing a necklace of golden skulls.

The entry also reports former human sacrifices in her honour. These are the historical author’s claims and categories, rather than a comprehensive description of Hindu worship.
