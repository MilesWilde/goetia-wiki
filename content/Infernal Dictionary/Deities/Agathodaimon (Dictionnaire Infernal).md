# Agathodaimon

The dictionary describes Agathodaimon, the good spirit, as an Egyptian object of worship represented by a serpent with a human head. It says Greeks in Arcadia also applied this name to [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jupiter (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jupiter]].

It distinguishes this named figure from the plural good spirits represented as winged serpents.
