# Demogorgon

Demogorgon begins inside the inert earth with Chaos as his only companion. Bored with isolation, he makes a spherical vehicle and rides it in a circle.

In this creation story, the journey forms the heavens; he encounters fire, makes it the sun, and constructs the world piece by piece. The dictionary associates his worship with Arcadia.
