# Odin

The dictionary describes Odin with two ravens that gather news throughout the world and return to tell him what they have learned. It associates the birds with thought and memory and the wolves at his feet with power. It says that [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Fenris (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Fenris]] will devour him at the end of time.

A second account treats Odin as an ancient priest, ruler, warrior, and poet who could foretell events and revive the dead, and who promised a place after death to brave warriors. The later Woden entry explicitly identifies that Germanic name with Odin; these historical and mythic presentations remain the dictionary’s own interpretations.
