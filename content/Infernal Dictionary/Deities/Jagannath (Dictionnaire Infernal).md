# Jagannath

Jagannath appears under the source spelling Jagghernat, with Jogonnata as a cross-reference. The dictionary's hostile account centers on a temple-car procession and an 1847 newspaper report alleging deaths beneath its wheels. The author calls the divinity a demon; the report and that religious judgment are his, not a neutral description of Hindu worship.
