# Youman-Ava

Youman-Ava is [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Youma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Youma]]’s wife and shares divine power with him and their children in the dictionary’s account of the Cheremis. The passage supplies no separate appearance or story.
