# Astarte

The dictionary describes Astarte as the female counterpart of [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Astaroth (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Astaroth]] and gives her the head of a heifer. This is a brief demonographic identification rather than a full account of the ancient goddess.
