# Ormuzd

Oromaze creates twenty-four deities and places them inside an egg in the Persian myth retold here. [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Ahriman (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Ahriman]] creates an equal number of opposing beings, which pierce the egg and cause evil to mingle with good.

Yesdhoon is the sole deity of the fire-worshippers in the dictionary’s account, also called Yesdoon-Urmuzd or Ormuzd and said to have a thousand and one names. The entry describes three attendant angels assigned to fire, water, and trees.

The dictionary explicitly uses Ormuzd in both the Oromaze and Yesdhoon accounts. They are gathered under that shared name here, with their different source descriptions retained.
