# Hel

Under the name Héla, the dictionary describes the daughter of [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Angrboda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Angerbode]] and queen of the dead, governing the nine worlds of Niflheim. A popular tradition makes her travel at night on the three-legged horse [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Helhest (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Helhest]], spreading plague and other disasters. The entry also uses her name to explain the later name [[Infernal Dictionary/Spirits/Hellequin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Hellequin]].
