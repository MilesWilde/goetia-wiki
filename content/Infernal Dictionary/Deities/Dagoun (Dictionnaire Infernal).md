# Dagoun

Dagoun is the supreme deity in the dictionary's account of Pegu. The [[Infernal Dictionary/Demons/Kiakiak (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Kiakiak]] article says that the demon will eventually destroy the world, but that Dagoun anticipates this and prepares to create a much better one.
