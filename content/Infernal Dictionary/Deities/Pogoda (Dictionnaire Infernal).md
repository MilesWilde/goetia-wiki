# Pogoda

Pogoda is the fair-weather deity in the dictionary’s account of pre-Christian Polish belief. The short notice contrasts this role with [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Pochwist (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Pochwist]]’s winter and bad-weather sphere.
