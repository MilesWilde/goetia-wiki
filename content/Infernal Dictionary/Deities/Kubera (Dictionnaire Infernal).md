# Kubera

Kubera is the god of riches and a great-grandson of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]] in this account. The dictionary describes three legs, eight teeth, and a gold piece covering one eye.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=201|PDF 201]].
