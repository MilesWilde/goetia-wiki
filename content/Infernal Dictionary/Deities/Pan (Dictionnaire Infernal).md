# Pan

The dictionary describes Pan with a human upper body and a goat’s lower body and places him among eight principal Egyptian deities. It separately reports demonographers’ treatment of Pan as a prince of incubi. These different classifications reflect the source’s comparative interpretation, not a single consistent ancient theology.
