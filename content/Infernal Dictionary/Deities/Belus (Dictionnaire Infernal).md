# Belus

Belus is an early Assyrian king said to have received worship during his life and to have delivered oracles after death. The dictionary associates him with astrology.

It suggests that he may be the same as [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Bel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bel]], but presents the identification as uncertain.
