# Bel

Bel is a creator figure in the dictionary’s account of Chaldean mythology. He defeats monstrous beings of chaos, and life is formed through a mixture of blood and earth.

The entry also records a demonographic Bel with an aged appearance and hollow voice. These are different descriptions assembled under the same name.

## Compare the other source

[[Entities/Bel (Goetia)|Bel]]. **Shared name.** The dictionary’s Bel entry collects Chaldean creator and demonographic accounts. The existing Goetia Bel note records the worshipped figure destroyed by Daniel. Both sources attest Bel in ancient religious contexts. The name occurs in both sources, but the dictionary’s creator and demonographic accounts differ from the Goetia’s biblical reference.
