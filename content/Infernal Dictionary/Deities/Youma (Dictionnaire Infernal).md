# Youma

Youma is the principal deity in the dictionary’s account of the Cheremis near Kazan. He is omnipresent and shares his power with his wife [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Youman-Ava (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Youman-Ava]] and their children. The entry says that these deities are not represented by images.
