# Ensoph

In the dictionary's account of Jewish cabala, Ensoph is the hidden supreme deity, the source of creation through [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Menra (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Menra]], the Word. [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Menra (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Menra]] produces the three great sephiroth, from which the lesser sephiroth proceed. Ensoph's emanations extend through ten spheres and four worlds, from the highest spirits to matter.
