# Thor

Thor is the Germanic god of thunder in the dictionary’s short entry, identified by his hammer. The separate story of the giant [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Rugner (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Rugner]] recounts a combat between the giant and Thor.
