# Takin

Takin is one of the nine lesser Brahmas listed as sons of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Brahma (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Brahma]]. This edition says he originates from a toe of the creator.

## Sources

Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire infernal*, sixth edition (1863): [[sources/dictionnaireinfernal.pdf#page=130|PDF 130]].
