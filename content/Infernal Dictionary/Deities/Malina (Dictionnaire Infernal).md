# Malina

Malina becomes the sun in the dictionary’s Greenlandic narrative. While her brother [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Anninga (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Anninga]] pursues her, she blackens his face and rises into the sky; he follows as the moon.
