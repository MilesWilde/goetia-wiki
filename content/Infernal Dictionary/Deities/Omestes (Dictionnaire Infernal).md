# Omestes

Omestes is an epithet of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Bacchus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bacchus]] in an account that casts him as leader of ogres or werewolves that eat raw flesh. The entry is about this particular interpretation of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Bacchus (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Bacchus]], not a separate biography.
