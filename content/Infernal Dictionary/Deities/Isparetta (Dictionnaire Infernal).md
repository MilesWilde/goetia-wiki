# Isparetta

Isparetta is the principal divinity of the Malabar coast in the dictionary's account. Before creation, Isparetta becomes an egg from which heaven, earth, and everything in them emerge. The representation described has three eyes, eight hands, a bell at the neck, and a crescent moon and serpents on the forehead.
