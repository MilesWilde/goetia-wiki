# Motogon

The dictionary describes Motogon as a creator in an Australian tradition. He is imagined as a powerful and wise man who brings the earth, water, sun, trees, and animals into existence by speaking and breathing. The author compares this narrative with Genesis; that comparison is his interpretation.
