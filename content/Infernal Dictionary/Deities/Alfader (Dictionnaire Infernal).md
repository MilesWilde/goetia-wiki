# Alfader

Alfader is described as a major Scandinavian creator, formerly a prince of the giants. The good are to live with him after death, while the wicked descend to [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Hel (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Hel]]’s realm.

The dictionary lists several other names and says Alfader can also be applied to [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]]; it does not consistently distinguish a separate creator from that title.
