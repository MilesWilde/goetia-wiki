# Vishnu

In the dictionary's account, Vishnu takes the form of a boar to fight [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Erouniakcha (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Erouniakcha]] after that son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Diti (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Diti]] throws the world into the sea. He kills the attacker and restores the world to its place; the author calls this Vishnu's third incarnation. The [[Infernal Dictionary/Figures/Garuda (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Garuda]] entry identifies the fabulous bird as Vishnu's mount.
