# Sophia

Sophia, called Sophie or Wisdom, is the mother of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Jaldabaoth (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Jaldabaoth]] in the dictionary's account of Ophite deities. The same notice names [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Chaos (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Chaos]] as his father.
