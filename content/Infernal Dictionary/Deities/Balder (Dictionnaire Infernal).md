# Balder

Balder is the son of [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Odin (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Odin]] and Frigga. The dictionary recounts how [[Infernal Dictionary/Deities/Loki (Dictionnaire Infernal)|Loki]] brings about his death through Hoder and says that he remains in the underworld.
