# Isis

The dictionary says that Isis had a temple at Isemberg, the 'mountain of Isis,' in the canton of Zurich, and reports a belief that she also received worship in Paris. In its article on the moon, the author identifies Isis with the Egyptian lunar divinity; these historical identifications are presented as the dictionary's claims.
