# Ritual names: what the words are doing

The conjurations contain **names of God, titles, abbreviated prayers, Greek expressions, and words whose transmission is uncertain**. They are not all names of individual characters. Within the ritual, the operator uses them to claim authority, request protection, and require a spirit to appear and answer. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]].

The original wiki indexed many of these strings in [[Goetia Divine Names]] without giving them contextual notes. That was a limit of the extraction, not evidence that nothing is known about them. **[[Emmanuel (Goetia)|Emmanuel]]**, for example, has a recognizable biblical meaning even though this edition provides only a brief ritual use. The notes below distinguish information actually supplied by this PDF from external linguistic, biblical, and historical context.

The notes cite the passages and contextual sources supporting each description. Their `ritual-name` classification is an indexing category, not a claim that each expression is a separate being.

## Why the book's “explanations” need context

The section entitled *Explanation of Certain Names* is largely a sequence of prayers arranged beside circle and triangle names. The translator flags misspellings; the editor expressly disputes treating the accompanying petitions as word meanings. Later notes suggest a prayer used while preparing or consecrating the figures. It can tell us **what the practitioner asks for**, without necessarily telling us **what a Hebrew or Greek word means**. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|PDF 69]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=70|PDF 70]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=71|PDF 71]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|PDF 96]].

Likewise, a conjuration's claim that [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]], [[Daniel (Goetia)|Daniel]], or [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] used a particular name supplies a precedent within its religious narrative. It does not date the composition of the surviving text or prove that those figures performed its ceremonies. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]].
