# Angels and celestial orders

Angels appear as authorities, guardians, messengers, and former orders of the numbered spirits. The book contains several systems; these tables keep each in its own context.

## The magical circle

The translator's note gives nine sequences around the circle: a divine name, a Sephirah, an archangel, an angelic choir, and a celestial sphere. The names below preserve that note's spellings. **The Sephiroth and spheres are not additional individual angels.** [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52, printed 38]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=53|diagram, PDF 53]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=95|editorial explanation, PDF 95]].

The [[Goetia Explanation of Names|explanatory prayer]] also uses **Haioth**, **Ophanim**, and **Hasmalim** (with **Chashmalim** proposed as a correction). Its adjacent statements are prayers, not reliable literal translations of the names. Both the translator and editor warn about it. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|PDF 69]]; [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|editor's assessment, PDF 96]].

## Former angelic orders of the spirits

Several profiles describe a pre-fall order: for example [[Agares]] and [[Barbatos]] are associated with **Virtues**, [[Beleth]] with **Powers**, [[Marchosias]] with **Dominations**, and [[Murmur]] with **Thrones** and **Angels**. Follow each profile for its precise wording and uncertainty.

The electronic editor explains a nine-order Christian hierarchy: **Seraphim, Kerubim, Thrones; Dominations or Dominions, Powers, Virtues; Principalities, Archangels, Angels**. The note also mentions **Blessed Souls** when that hierarchy is fitted to ten Sephiroth. This explanatory scheme is distinct from the circle's Hebrew choir table. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=93|PDF 93, printed 77]].

## Collective and unnamed celestial beings

- **Four living beasts before the throne**, described with eyes before and behind; invoked collectively without individual names. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].
- **The angel who wrestled [[Jacob (Goetia)|Jacob]]**, referred to by the [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] without a name. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]].
- **Holy angels, heavenly hosts, and the company of heaven**, invoked as groups in the prayers and curses. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=66|66]].
- **The 72 angels of the Shem ha-Mephorash**, discussed by the electronic editor as a formation of names from Exodus. Their complete names are not listed here, so no external angel-to-demon pairings are supplied. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=92|PDF 92]].
- **Angels of the hours and Zodiac**, described as the subject of *Ars Paulina*, another *Lemegeton* book. This PDF does not contain that catalogue. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=22|PDF 22]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=91|91]].
- **Spirits of the Four Altitudes**, described as the good spirits of *Ars Almadel*. One introductory version specifies twenty chief spirits; individual names are not supplied here. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=22|PDF 22]].
