# Abrasax

**Abrasax appears as a spoken name within the [[Goetia Preliminary Invocation|Preliminary Invocation]].** The passage does not give it a Goetic rank, seal, office, or separate biography.

## In this edition

The name occurs in sequences that address the power invoked in the [[Bornless One (Goetia)|Bornless One]] ritual. Its textual role is to contribute to the invocation's accumulated divine or magical names. This is different from the numbered entries describing individual spirits and their offices. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=11|PDF 11]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=12|PDF 12]].

The electronic editor discusses corrupt transmission of these word sequences and specifically distinguishes **Abrasax** from the erroneous **Abrasar** in the de Lawrence edition. The misspelling is not evidence for another being. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=90|PDF 90]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=91|PDF 91]].

## Historical context and associated imagery

A bloodstone gem catalogued by the Fitzwilliam Museum, dated approximately **200–400 CE**, has an anguipede figure—one with serpent-like legs—and inscriptions identified as Abrasax and Iao. It provides a concrete example of the name's ancient amuletic setting. [Fitzwilliam Museum, gem CG 510](https://data.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/id/object/124588).

That object is a historical comparison, not an illustration supplied by this Goetia edition or proof that the invocation describes the pictured figure. The wiki keeps those kinds of evidence separate.
