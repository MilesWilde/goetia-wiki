# Anaphaxeton

**Anaphaxeton is a name of ritual authority associated with binding and the triangle.** Its numerous spellings are part of this edition's evidence.

## In this edition

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] claims that [[Aaron (Goetia)|Aaron]] heard and spoke the name and became wise; its footnote gives **Anapezeton**. The [[Goetia Constraint|Constraint]] includes **Anaphaxeton / Anapheneton** among the seven names with which [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]] supposedly bound the spirits. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]].

The triangle instructions explicitly mention **Anepheneton** as a spelling of Anaphaxeton. The later triangle prayer writes **Anephezeton** and places it beside [[Primeumaton (Goetia)|Primeumaton]] and [[Michael (Goetia)|Michael]]. Under that heading the prayer addresses [[God (Goetia)|God]] as ruler of the heavenly host. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=71|PDF 71]].

## What remains uncertain

The book does not establish a secure etymology or word-for-word translation. The triangle's prayer explains how the name is being used; it is not a linguistic dictionary. The warning attached to the prayer section explicitly disputes treating its glosses as literal meanings. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|PDF 69]].
