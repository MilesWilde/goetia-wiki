# Shaddai

**Shaddai is a divine name used to authorize the conjuration and to mark a place in the protective circle.**

## In this edition

The [[Goetia First Conjuration|first conjuration]] prints **Shaddäi** among the names by which the operator commands the spirit. The circle uses **Shaddaï el Chai** at the beginning of the Yesod sequence, followed by [[Gabriel (Goetia)|Gabriel]], [[Goetia Angels and Orders#The magical circle|Cherubim]], and the Moon. The compound belongs to the circle's ordered correspondences. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52]].

The later prayer writes **Sadai**, addressing [[God (Goetia)|God]] in connection with wisdom, knowledge, and the petitions accompanying Yesod. The different spelling is recorded here so that a reader encountering it can find the relevant divine-name context. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=70|PDF 70]].

## Historical and biblical context

Shaddai is conventionally rendered **Almighty**, but its etymology is uncertain. It occurs both alone and with [[El (Goetia)|El]]. [Jewish Encyclopedia, “God, Names of,” Shaddai](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/6727-god-names-of).

The prayer's wording about wisdom is therefore a petition associated with the name, not a reliable word-for-word translation.
