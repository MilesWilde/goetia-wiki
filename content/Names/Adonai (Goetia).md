# Adonai

**Adonai is a divine title invoked as authority over spirits.** It is not another member of the 72-spirit catalogue.

## In this edition

The [[Goetia First Conjuration|first conjuration]] includes Adonai among the names of [[God (Goetia)|God]] by which the practitioner commands a spirit to appear. The second connects it with the plague of locusts and invokes the seal of Adonai; a footnote records *seat* and *throne* as alternative readings. These are claims made by the ritual text about [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]], rather than quotations establishing that he performed this ceremony. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].

The [[Goetia Constraint|Constraint]] includes Adonai among the seven names associated with [[Solomon (Goetia)|Solomon]]'s binding of spirits. Its conclusion says that Adonai commands the spirit: the operator presents the demand as backed by divine authority. The letters also occupy the circle's hexagrams. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52]].

## Historical and biblical context

Adonai is a Hebrew address conventionally rendered **“my Lord”**, also used when reading the divine name YHWH aloud. [USCCB, Exodus 3, note on 3:14](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/exodus/3).
