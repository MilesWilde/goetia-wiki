# Primeumaton

**Primeumaton is a ritual name whose use is clearer than its derivation.** This edition supplies a role and a prayer, but no secure linguistic explanation.

## In this edition

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] attributes the name to [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]] in the destruction of [[Korah (Goetia)|Kora]], [[Dathan (Goetia)|Dathan]], and [[Abiram (Goetia)|Abiram]]. The [[Goetia Constraint|Constraint]] says it commands the heavenly host and invokes it as authority for punishing a disobedient spirit. These associations make it a particularly forceful name within the ritual sequence. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]].

The triangle prayer places Primeumaton between [[Anaphaxeton (Goetia)|Anephezeton]] and [[Michael (Goetia)|Michael]]. Its petition addresses the first and last and asks that spirits submit and remain bound in the triangle until released. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=71|PDF 71]].

## What remains uncertain

The phrase about first and last is the prayer attached to the name, **not a demonstrated translation of Primeumaton**. The translator calls this section a paraphrased prayer; the editor objects to taking its sentences as the meanings of the words. Later editorial notes connect it with consecrating the ritual space. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|PDF 69]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|PDF 96]].
