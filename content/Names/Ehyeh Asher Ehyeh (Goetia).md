# Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh

**Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh is a biblical divine self-designation used here as a name of command.**

## In this edition

The complete phrase appears among the names of [[God (Goetia)|God]] in the [[Goetia First Conjuration|first conjuration]]. The circle begins with the shorter **Ehyeh**, paired with Kether, [[Metatron (Goetia)|Metatron]], and [[Goetia Angels and Orders#The magical circle|Chaioth ha-Qadesh]]. The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] instead has the compound **Asher Ehyeh Oriston**. These different written sequences should remain visible rather than being silently standardized. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].

The later prayer begins **Eheie**; the editor's vessel transcription gives **Asher Eheyeh**. An editorial note proposes that **Aye, Sarayé** is a corruption of the full phrase. That last connection is an editorial hypothesis, not a separate translated name established by the main text. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=69|PDF 69]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=95|PDF 95]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=96|PDF 96]].

## Historical and biblical context

Exodus 3:14 presents this expression as [[God (Goetia)|God]]'s answer to [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]]. It is often translated **“I am who I am”**; the Hebrew also allows a future sense, “I will be.” No single English rendering resolves every interpretive question. [USCCB, Exodus 3:14 and note](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/exodus/3).
