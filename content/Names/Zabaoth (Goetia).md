# Zabaoth

**Zabaoth, Tzabaoth, and Sabaoth are spellings associated with the divine title “Lord of Hosts.”** The edition itself glosses Tzabaoth as hosts or armies. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].

## In this edition

The [[Goetia First Conjuration|first conjuration]] includes Zabaoth in its divine-name sequence. The second associates it with [[Moses (Goetia)|Moses]] turning rivers to blood. The [[Goetia Constraint|Constraint]] invokes the Lord God of Hosts and later repeats Zabaoth beside [[Adonai (Goetia)|Adonai]]. These passages use the name to reinforce command over the summoned spirit. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=61|PDF 61]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=63|PDF 63]].

The circle has **[[Iehovah (Goetia)|Iehovah]] Tzabaoth** and **[[Elohim (Goetia)|Elohim]] Tzabaoth** in different sequences. The later devotional prayer writes **Sabaoth**. Preserve the compounds when interpreting the circle: a repeated component does not make the surrounding sequences identical. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=52|PDF 52]], [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=70|PDF 70]].

## Historical and biblical context

Hebrew *tseva'ot* means hosts or armies; its divine-title usage is documented beyond this grimoire. [Jewish Encyclopedia, “God, Names of,” Ẓeba'ot](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/6727-god-names-of).
