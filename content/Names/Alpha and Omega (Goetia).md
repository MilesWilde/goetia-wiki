# Alpha and Omega

**Alpha and Omega expresses divine completeness: the beginning and the end.**

## In this edition

The [[Goetia Second Conjuration|second conjuration]] claims that [[Daniel (Goetia)|Daniel]] used this name when destroying [[Bel (Goetia)|Bel]] and the [[Dragon of Daniel (Goetia)|Dragon]]. The claim makes the formula part of a sequence of biblical precedents intended to compel the spirit. It should be attributed to the grimoire, rather than treated as a quotation from Daniel. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=62|PDF 62]].

The later explanation of the parchment figures directly addresses [[God (Goetia)|God]] as beginning and end under this heading. Its position beside petitions for protection and obedience shows why the title belongs to the ritual's language of encompassing divine authority. [[sources/ac_goetia.pdf#page=71|PDF 71]].

## Historical and biblical context

Alpha and omega are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet. Revelation uses them as a divine title, explicitly pairing them with beginning and end. [Vatican, Revelation 1, note on 1:8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0839/_P12K.HTM); [USCCB, Revelation 22:13](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/revelation/22).

They form one expression in this context; the wiki does not turn the two letters into separate beings.
